A person fleeing persecution should have a fair hearing and protection when it is needed. I regard that obligation as independent of whether the person will be economically profitable. Work visas and asylum should be debated against their different purposes, rather than making protection conditional on a favourable fiscal forecast.

The scale should be stated honestly. [Around 108,000 people claimed asylum in the UK in 2024, above the previous modern-record peak of about 103,000 in 2002](#fact-asylum-claims-in-historical-and-european-context/claims-are-at-their-highest-in-the-modern-record-but-only-just). In the same year, [the UK ranked fifth by volume and seventeenth per person against the EU+, at 16 applicants per 10,000 residents](#fact-asylum-claims-in-historical-and-european-context/per-head-of-population-the-uk-is-mid-table-not-top). [Its rank for grants of protection was fourteenth per person](#fact-asylum-claims-in-historical-and-european-context/per-head-of-population-the-uk-is-mid-table-not-top). The UK historical comparison and the European comparison answer different questions; neither should be hidden.

Nor are asylum and small boats interchangeable. [Some asylum claimants previously arrived on visas or other permission](#fact-how-people-actually-arrive/asylum-and-small-boats-overlap-but-are-not-nested). [Detected illegal arrivals numbered 46,497 in 2025, alongside 813,000 long-term arrivals](#fact-how-people-actually-arrive/detected-illegal-arrivals-were-about-one-in-eighteen-of-everyone-who-moved-here). [Study was the largest reason for non-EU+ immigration](#fact-how-people-actually-arrive/study-is-the-largest-single-reason-anyone-moves-to-britain). These distinctions are a reason to specify what a proposed restriction is supposed to achieve.

The administration can improve. [The number awaiting an initial asylum decision fell from 175,000 at the June 2023 peak to 49,000 in March 2026](#fact-asylum-backlog-and-hotel-use-have-fallen/the-initial-decision-queue-is-72-below-its-peak). [Hotel use fell 35% in a year to 21,000 people](#fact-asylum-backlog-and-hotel-use-have-fallen/hotel-use-fell-by-about-a-third-in-a-year). Those findings support a case for investing in decisions and accommodation capacity. They do not prove that every problem has a single administrative cause or that the decisions themselves were correct.

My programme would be to decide claims promptly, provide safe routes, allow people to contribute while waiting, and carry out lawful removals with due process when protection is refused. These are policy preferences, not claims that any one measure is costless or guaranteed to end dangerous journeys.

This view must accept the costs. [The accommodation contracts’ estimate rose from £4.5 billion to £15.3 billion over ten years](#fact-asylum-accommodation-cost-overrun/the-contracts-are-running-at-more-than-three-times-their-original-price). [Hotels accounted for a disproportionate share of that spending](#fact-asylum-accommodation-cost-overrun/hotels-are-the-reason). I would argue for meeting a real obligation efficiently, not pretend that the obligation has no price.

It must also accept unwelcome outcomes. [The initial asylum grant rate fell to 39% in the year to March 2026](#fact-asylum-grant-rate-fell/it-fell-from-77-to-39-in-three-and-a-half-years). [That measure excludes appeals and reflects the caseload and decision-making together](#fact-asylum-grant-rate-fell/a-grant-rate-is-a-fact-about-the-caseload-and-the-decision-maker-together); it does not entitle us to presume every refusal wrong. [Public opposition is stronger for asylum than for work or study](#fact-immigration-is-salient-and-objection-varies-by-route/the-public-does-not-hold-one-view-of-immigration-it-sorts-by-route), and [the number of deportable offenders living in the community has grown](#fact-deportable-offenders-living-in-the-community/that-group-grew-36-between-the-end-of-2022-and-march-2026). A protection system needs public explanation and credible rules as well as compassion.

Finally, [the evidence does not establish how much proposed deterrence policies would reduce Channel crossings](#fact-deterrence-would-reduce-channel-crossings/finding). [Australia’s experience involved physical interception, whose role differs from offshore processing](#fact-deterrence-would-reduce-channel-crossings/australian-arrivals-did-fall-but-the-mechanism-is-disputed). I oppose making protection depend on a claimed deterrent effect that has not been established. The moral case should be stated openly, with the evidence for its practical consequences available to anyone who disagrees.
