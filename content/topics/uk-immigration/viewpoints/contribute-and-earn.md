---
name: Choose migrants for what they contribute, and make settlement something they earn
summary: "The gain from immigration [comes from well-paid workers who stay; the low-paid care route and its dependants cost the public finances](#fact-immigration-economy-and-public-finances/skilled-workers-gain-care-workers-cost). Select for contribution, police each route for its purpose, and make permanent settlement something people earn by working and paying in."
order: 7
---
Immigration is not good or bad in bulk; it depends on who comes and what they do. The government's advisers model [Skilled Worker main applicants outside health and care at a lifetime gain of £689,000 each, against a cost of £36,000 for each care worker and £67,000 for each Health and Care dependant](#fact-immigration-economy-and-public-finances/skilled-workers-gain-care-workers-cost). The gain is concentrated: [the top tenth of those earners provide 39% of their contribution, and for lower earners temporary stays are better for the public finances than permanent ones](#fact-immigration-economy-and-public-finances/gain-from-top-earners-and-stayers). That points to a system that selects hard and makes permanence depend on contribution.

The care route shows what happens otherwise. [The skill threshold was cut to A-level in 2020 and care workers were added in 2022](#fact-work-and-study-routes/skill-threshold-lowered-then-raised); [Health and Care visa holders then brought 1.21 dependants each](#fact-work-and-study-routes/dependants-near-half-at-peak). [Sponsor licence revocations reached a record number, with adult social care among the worst sectors](#fact-work-and-study-routes/revocations-at-a-record), and [about half the students who switched straight into work became care workers](#fact-work-and-study-routes/graduate-switchers-jobs). Meanwhile [British-held care posts fell by about 130,000](#fact-work-and-study-routes/british-held-posts-fell). Since overseas recruitment ran down, [care vacancies have not risen](#fact-work-and-study-routes/vacancies-held-as-recruitment-fell), and [the vacancy rate is the lowest in the series](#fact-work-and-study-routes/care-vacancies-fell).

Most of the fall came before the formal changes: [care visas collapsed through scrutiny of sponsors well before the route closed](#fact-work-and-study-routes/care-visas-collapsed-before-the-closure), which shows that enforcement works. As a share of the much larger number of sponsors, [revocations are back to their mid-2010s level](#fact-work-and-study-routes/revocation-rate-not-a-record). And [British-held posts are still falling](#fact-work-and-study-routes/british-held-posts-fell), while [care pay sits near the legal minimum](#fact-work-and-study-routes/care-pay-near-the-floor): ending overseas recruitment only works if employers and government raise pay to recruit at home.

Every route must be used for its purpose. [Two in five people claiming asylum had held a visa](#fact-work-and-study-routes/visa-holders-two-in-five-claims), and [claims from Graduate visa holders more than doubled](#fact-work-and-study-routes/graduate-claims-doubled), though they remain a small fraction of the visas each route issues.

Settlement is the point at which someone gains [most of the benefits that temporary visa holders are barred from](#fact-settlement-and-migrant-status/nrpf-costs), so it should follow contribution. On current circumstances [hundreds of thousands would not yet meet the proposed employment requirement](#fact-settlement-and-migrant-status/non-working-groups); a longer route gives them time to. [Those who contribute earn a share](#principle-contribution-earns-a-share), and a government owes its [first duty to its own people](#principle-first-duty-to-its-own-people), who fund what settlement unlocks. A ten-year default would [make the UK an outlier among high-income countries](#fact-settlement-and-migrant-status/ten-year-baseline-outlier). That is a fair price on the lower-paid routes, and high earners would keep a short path.

The routes for the most talented [are a small slice of work visas](#fact-work-and-study-routes/talent-routes-are-small) and should grow. Keep the higher skill and salary thresholds, police sponsors and routes for their purpose, and make settlement earned: ten years as the default, shorter for those who earn and pay in most.
