---
name: Abolish permanent settlement, even for people who already have it, and aim for more people leaving than arriving
summary: "Britain is about to grant permanence to [about 1.6 million people in five years](#fact-settlement-and-migrant-status/forecast-1-6-million), many of them on routes that cost the public money over a lifetime. Replace settlement with renewable visas, keep benefits for citizens, and for a time let more people leave than arrive."
order: 3
---
Indefinite leave to remain is, as the law stands, a decision a country cannot easily take back. Once granted, a person can stay for life and [draw on most benefits, which temporary visa holders cannot](#fact-settlement-and-migrant-status/nrpf-costs). The Home Office expects [between 1.3 and 2.2 million people to qualify in 2026–30, about 1.6 million on its central estimate](#fact-settlement-and-migrant-status/forecast-1-6-million), driven by the care, skilled-worker and Hong Kong visas issued since 2021. That is a choice voters should get to make before it is made for them.

Many of them came on routes that do not pay their way. The government's own advisers put [each care worker at a lifetime cost of £36,000 to the public finances, and each Health and Care dependant at £67,000](#fact-immigration-economy-and-public-finances/skilled-workers-gain-care-workers-cost); [partners arriving on the family route come out at £109,000 each](#fact-immigration-economy-and-public-finances/average-resident-is-a-lifetime-net-cost). [Around half of adult Skilled Worker dependants were not working, and a third of adults on family visas were not working by their fifth year](#fact-settlement-and-migrant-status/non-working-groups). The same advisers conclude that [for lower earners, temporary migration is better for the public finances than permanent](#fact-immigration-economy-and-public-finances/gain-from-top-earners-and-stayers), because [old age reverses most of the gains](#fact-immigration-economy-and-public-finances/old-age-reverses-gains). Settlement turns a working stay into a claim on pensions, health and care for decades.

A renewable visa keeps everyone worth keeping. The well-paid engineer who [contributes hundreds of thousands of pounds over a lifetime](#fact-immigration-economy-and-public-finances/skilled-workers-gain-care-workers-cost) is renewed; the person who does not work is not. The terms of settlement have changed before: [the family route went from two years to five in 2012](#fact-settlement-and-migrant-status/2012-family-change), and the guidance tells workers only that they [“may be able to apply”](#fact-settlement-and-migrant-status/guidance-said-may-be-able-to-apply).

Removing settlement from people who already hold it is harder, and the case for it rests on principle more than data. [No official figures show what indefinite-leave holders earn or claim](#fact-settlement-and-migrant-status/no-official-data-on-ilr-holders-work-or-benefits), so the argument about them leans on what visa holders do. It would [need a new Act of Parliament](#fact-settlement-and-migrant-status/revocation-needs-new-law), it would leave out [the far larger number with EU settled status](#fact-settlement-and-migrant-status/euss-outnumber-non-eu-ilr), and [the public opposes it 58% to 29%](#fact-settlement-and-migrant-status/revoking-opposed-58-29). But Parliament makes the law, and a country has the right to decide [who becomes a permanent member of it](#principle-democratic-consent-over-membership). [Ending settlement for new arrivals already divides the public evenly](#fact-settlement-and-migrant-status/ending-for-new-arrivals-splits); the argument for going further has to be won, and can be.

Lower arrivals alone would leave the [2.5 million added by net migration in 2021–24](#fact-immigration-against-the-long-run/four-years-gross-and-net) in place. Britain's past years of net emigration [came when arrivals were a fraction of today's](#fact-immigration-against-the-long-run/negative-years-came-from-emigration), and [with no net migration the population ages fastest](#fact-immigration-against-the-long-run/migration-slows-ageing-but-does-not-stop-it). That is a real cost, and one worth paying: [British nationals already leave on net](#fact-immigration-against-the-long-run/british-and-eu-net-emigration), so arrivals need only fall below departures.

A government's [first duty is to its own citizens](#principle-first-duty-to-its-own-people). Replace indefinite leave with renewable five-year visas, reserve benefits for citizens, and keep arrivals below departures until the country has absorbed the change it never chose.
