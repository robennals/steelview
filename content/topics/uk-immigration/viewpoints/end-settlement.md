Britain should stop giving migrants the right to stay for good. People who already have it should move onto visas that must be renewed. And for a time, more people should leave Britain than arrive.

### A wave of permanent settlement is about to arrive

Indefinite leave to remain, or settlement, is the right to stay in Britain for life. [The Home Office expects about 1.6 million people to qualify for it by 2030](#fact-settlement-and-migrant-status/forecast-1-6-million). [The rise comes from care, skilled-worker and Hong Kong visas issued since 2021](#fact-settlement-and-migrant-status/forecast-1-6-million). Once settled, [a person can claim most of the benefits that people on temporary visas cannot](#fact-settlement-and-migrant-status/nrpf-costs). Voters should get to decide that before it happens.

### Many of those settling will cost the public money for life

Government advisers find that [care workers, and the adult family members of health and care workers, cost more than they pay over a lifetime](#fact-immigration-economy-and-public-finances/skilled-workers-gain-care-workers-cost). [Partners arriving on the family route come out at a cost of £109,000 each](#fact-immigration-economy-and-public-finances/average-resident-is-a-lifetime-net-cost). [About half of adult skilled-worker dependants were not working, and a third of adults on family visas were not working by their fifth year](#fact-settlement-and-migrant-status/non-working-groups).

The same advisers conclude that [for lower earners, a temporary stay is better for the public purse than a permanent one](#fact-immigration-economy-and-public-finances/gain-from-top-earners-and-stayers). That is because [old age reverses most of the gains](#fact-immigration-economy-and-public-finances/old-age-reverses-gains). Settlement turns a working stay into decades of claims on pensions, health and care.

### A visa that must be renewed keeps the people worth keeping

Under a five-year renewable visa, the well-paid engineer stays. [A skilled worker outside health and care adds hundreds of thousands of pounds over a lifetime](#fact-immigration-economy-and-public-finances/skilled-workers-gain-care-workers-cost). The person who does not work is not renewed.

Nobody was promised permanence. [The official guidance said only that workers "may be able to apply"](#fact-settlement-and-migrant-status/guidance-said-may-be-able-to-apply). [Rule changes have been applied before to people already in Britain](#fact-settlement-and-migrant-status/transitional-protection-precedent), and [the family route went from two years to five in 2012](#fact-settlement-and-migrant-status/2012-family-change).

### Parliament has the right to change settlement for people who already hold it

While [no official figures show what people with settlement earn or claim](#fact-settlement-and-migrant-status/no-official-data-on-ilr-holders-work-or-benefits), this step rests on principle, not numbers. Settlement was granted by law, and Parliament can change the law. [It would need a new Act of Parliament](#fact-settlement-and-migrant-status/revocation-needs-new-law), passed openly by the people's representatives. A country has the right to decide [who becomes a permanent member of it](#principle-democratic-consent-over-membership).

While [EU citizens with settled status far outnumber them and are protected under the withdrawal agreement](#fact-settlement-and-migrant-status/euss-outnumber-non-eu-ilr), that is a reason to act where Parliament can. And while [most people oppose taking settlement from those who hold it](#fact-settlement-and-migrant-status/revoking-opposed-58-29), [ending it for new arrivals already splits the country evenly](#fact-settlement-and-migrant-status/ending-for-new-arrivals-splits). [Reform voters already back removal](#fact-settlement-and-migrant-status/revoking-opposed-58-29). The argument has to be won, and it can be.

### Fewer arrivals alone will not undo the change

Lower arrivals would leave [the 2.5 million people added by net migration in 2021–24](#fact-immigration-against-the-long-run/four-years-gross-and-net) in place. Only a time when more people leave than arrive would reverse it. While [Britain's past years of net emigration came when arrivals were a fraction of today's](#fact-immigration-against-the-long-run/negative-years-came-from-emigration), [British citizens already leave on net](#fact-immigration-against-the-long-run/british-and-eu-net-emigration). So arrivals need only fall below departures.

While [with no net migration the population ages fastest](#fact-immigration-against-the-long-run/migration-slows-ageing-but-does-not-stop-it), that is a price worth paying. A country should not have to accept for ever a change it never chose.

### Replace settlement with renewable visas and keep benefits for citizens

[A government's first duty is to its own citizens](#principle-first-duty-to-its-own-people). The welfare state they pay for should be theirs. Britain should replace indefinite leave with renewable five-year visas and reserve benefits for citizens. It should keep arrivals below departures until the country has absorbed the change.
