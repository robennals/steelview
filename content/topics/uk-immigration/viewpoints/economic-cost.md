Britain has taken in more people than it can house, and its people are no richer for it. A government's first duty is to its own people. Parliament should cut immigration hard and make sure it stays down.

### Britain has just had the biggest wave of immigration on record

[More people moved to Britain in each year from 2021 to 2024 than in any year since the record began in 1964](#fact-immigration-against-the-long-run/record-immigration-2021-to-2024), and in those four years net migration added about 2.5 million people. [Migration now makes up almost all of the country's population growth](#fact-immigration-against-the-long-run/migration-is-almost-all-population-growth).

### More people make the economy bigger, but barely make anyone richer

[Since 2019, output per person has barely moved](#fact-immigration-economy-and-public-finances/gdp-per-head-barely-grew), even as the economy as a whole grew. The budget watchdog expects no more: [in its scenarios, migration moves output per person by under 1%, up or down](#fact-immigration-economy-and-public-finances/obr-per-head-effect-small), and [extra migrants leave output per person lower if buildings and equipment do not keep up](#fact-immigration-economy-and-public-finances/capital-does-not-adjust), which [public investment does not do straight away](#fact-immigration-housing-and-public-services/investment-does-not-adjust). While [Canada and Germany stalled too](#fact-immigration-economy-and-public-finances/g7-stalled-alike), so migration is not the only cause, a bigger economy is not a richer people.

### Migration is expected to add half of England's new households, and building falls short

[Migration accounts for just over half of the extra households expected in England](#fact-immigration-housing-and-public-services/migration-share-of-household-growth), yet [England is adding fewer homes each year than the households projected](#fact-immigration-housing-and-public-services/building-below-household-growth). [About a third of private renters were born abroad](#fact-immigration-housing-and-public-services/migrants-concentrated-in-private-renting), and [rent growth dropped to less than half its peak as net migration fell](#fact-immigration-housing-and-public-services/rent-growth-fell-as-migration-fell). While [immigration explains only a small part of the long rise in house prices](#fact-immigration-housing-and-public-services/immigration-explains-4-6-percent-of-house-price-rise), [in countries like Britain it raises rents more than prices](#fact-immigration-housing-and-public-services/rent-effects), and rent is what young people without a home of their own pay.

### Low-paid routes bring people who cost more than they pay in

[Skilled workers outside health and care are a large gain to the public purse](#fact-immigration-economy-and-public-finances/skilled-workers-large-net-gain). But care workers, and the adult family members of health and care workers, cost more than they pay over their lives. The average resident is a lifetime cost too, but that is no reason to add more people at that cost. While [lower migration raises borrowing within five years](#fact-immigration-economy-and-public-finances/lower-migration-raises-borrowing), that window ends before [the costs that come as migrants grow old](#fact-immigration-economy-and-public-finances/old-age-reverses-gains), and [lower migration raises public spending per person](#fact-immigration-housing-and-public-services/lower-migration-raises-spending-per-person), which is what residents receive.

### The fall in numbers rests on rules a future government can undo

[Net migration has fallen by about four-fifths from its peak](#fact-immigration-against-the-long-run/net-migration-is-not-unusual-at-all), and the net flow for work and study fell as visa rules tightened. A future minister can loosen those rules again, and promises have failed before: [since the 2010 pledge, net migration has reached the promised "tens of thousands" only in 2020, during Covid](#fact-immigration-against-the-long-run/tens-of-thousands-met-only-in-covid), and [later Conservative and Labour pledges named no number](#fact-immigration-against-the-long-run/later-pledges-unquantified).

### So Parliament should set a binding cap and lengthen the wait to settle

[Most people want immigration reduced](#fact-immigration-against-the-long-run/public-preferences), and a country should have a say over who joins it. So Parliament should set a binding yearly cap on net migration, and close the low-paid routes that cost more than they bring in. It should also lengthen the wait for settlement, the right to stay for good: [about 1.6 million people are expected to settle by 2030](#fact-settlement-and-migrant-status/forecast-1-6-million), and [once settled, they can claim most of the benefits that people on temporary visas cannot](#fact-settlement-and-migrant-status/settlement-unlocks-benefits).
