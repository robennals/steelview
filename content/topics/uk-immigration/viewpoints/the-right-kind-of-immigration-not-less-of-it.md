A single total is a poor starting point for deciding whom Britain should welcome. Start with what the number contains: [47% of non-EU+ arrivals in 2025 came for study, 23% for work, and 14% for asylum](#fact-how-people-actually-arrive/study-is-the-largest-single-reason-anyone-moves-to-britain). Those categories should be judged against different purposes. A university place, a job and a request for protection are not interchangeable policy decisions.

Nor does “student” mean permanent settler. [Students count as long-term immigrants when they move for at least twelve months](#fact-what-happens-to-students-after-they-graduate/a-student-counts-as-a-long-term-immigrant-on-arrival-because-the-count-uses-a-twelve). But the comfortable claim that they all leave is wrong too: [41% of the 2020 study cohort still held leave five years later, compared with 17–18% of the 2012–2016 cohorts](#fact-what-happens-to-students-after-they-graduate/chart-series). [Permission to remain is not a direct measurement of physical presence](#fact-what-happens-to-students-after-they-graduate/still-holds-leave-is-not-is-still-in-the-country). We should evaluate the route using the outcome we actually care about.

The strongest reason to distinguish routes is the public-finance evidence. [The Skilled Worker route is modelled as fiscally positive over migrants’ lifetimes](#fact-skilled-worker-fiscal-gain-concentrated/the-route-as-a-whole-is-clearly-positive), yet [72% of the gain among main applicants outside health and care comes from the top 30% of earners](#fact-skilled-worker-fiscal-gain-concentrated/chart-earnings-concentration). Meanwhile [care workers have an estimated lifetime contribution of −£36,000, against +£166,000 for other health and care occupations](#fact-care-worker-route-fiscally-negative/finding). These are substantial differences that a headcount alone conceals.

That does not make the highest possible salary threshold the right policy. [The MAC estimates a fiscal break-even salary of roughly £29,000 for a main applicant on a route allowing dependants](#fact-fiscal-break-even-salary/the-break-even-is-roughly-29-000-a-year), with [different break-even estimates by age](#fact-fiscal-break-even-salary/it-rises-with-age-at-entry). I would use earnings and likely contribution to select applicants, while allowing for early-career progression and services whose public value is not fully captured by tax receipts.

The rules have already changed the mix. [The work-visa skill threshold was lowered in the 2020 reforms and raised again in July 2025](#fact-work-visas-swung-to-low-skill-and-back/the-bar-was-lowered-then-raised-again); [the below-degree share rose from under 10% in early 2021 to nearly 60% in early 2024](#fact-work-visas-swung-to-low-skill-and-back/in-between-the-mix-swung-hard). [Care-worker visa grants then fell from about 108,000 to 1,400 a year](#fact-care-worker-route-opened-then-closed/one-rule-change-accounts-for-much-of-both-the-rise-and-the-fall-in-the-headline-numbe). My objection is to treating these very different outcomes as one undifferentiated success or failure.

There are concessions this argument owes. [Health and care employs a quarter of the jobs held by employees registered with non-EU nationality](#fact-health-and-care-relies-on-migrant-workers/a-quarter-of-the-jobs-held-by-recent-non-eu-migrants-are-in-health-and-care), and [fiscal modelling excludes potentially important benefits of care to the wider population](#fact-care-worker-route-fiscally-negative/it-counts-fiscal-flows-only). [Public preferences favour making care-worker migration easier more than financial-sector migration](#fact-immigration-is-salient-and-objection-varies-by-route/the-sorting-does-not-follow-skill). A selection policy should explain how it weighs those priorities rather than pretend the spreadsheet decides them.

Nor should it claim that overseas recruitment has been proved to undermine domestic training. [The directly measured effects on training were not negative](#fact-overseas-recruitment-and-domestic-training/no-negative-effect-on-training-was-found-and-one-study-found-a-positive-one), although [that evidence comes from the earlier free-movement system](#fact-overseas-recruitment-and-domestic-training/those-studies-were-estimated-on-eea-free-movement). [Measured wage effects are generally small](#fact-wage-effects-small-and-uneven/what-is-established-is-a-small-unevenly-distributed-effect), and [migration drives population growth in the official projections](#fact-migration-is-the-only-source-of-population-growth/finding). My conclusion is to choose and explain the composition, not to assume that reducing every route equally is an adequate policy.
