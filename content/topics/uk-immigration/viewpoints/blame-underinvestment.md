More people need more homes, hospitals and schools. Britain's shortages come from failing to build them, for decades and for every newcomer, not from the newcomers themselves. Britain should invest for the population it has, and stop making migrants' lives deliberately hard.

### Britain has long invested less than other rich countries, and investment does not follow population

[The UK government invested a smaller share of GDP than the typical rich country in every year for three decades](#fact-immigration-housing-and-public-services/public-investment-low). And [much public investment is fixed years ahead, so it does not grow when the population does](#fact-immigration-housing-and-public-services/investment-does-not-adjust). That matters, because [migration is just over half of the extra households England expects](#fact-immigration-housing-and-public-services/migration-share-of-household-growth). While [health spending per person has almost doubled since 2000](#fact-immigration-housing-and-public-services/health-spending-per-person-doubled), [Britain spends less than the average rich country on health buildings and equipment](#fact-immigration-housing-and-public-services/health-capital-below-average). The newcomers are real; the failure is not building for them.

### Cheap borrowing, not immigration, drove most of the rise in house prices

[In countries like Britain, raising the immigrant share by one point raises house prices by under 1%, and rents by a little more](#fact-immigration-housing-and-public-services/rent-effects). So more people do push housing costs up, a little. But [Bank of England economists find that falling interest rates more than account for the rise in house prices against incomes since 1985](#fact-immigration-housing-and-public-services/falling-interest-rates-explain-price-rise), and [the government's advisers put immigration's share of the long rise at about 4–6%](#fact-immigration-housing-and-public-services/immigration-explains-4-6-percent-of-house-price-rise). Building fell short too: [for over a decade, England has built fewer homes for social rent each year than in any year of the two decades before](#fact-immigration-housing-and-public-services/social-rent-building-fell-after-2010).

### Migrants staff the NHS and care homes, and care is short because it is underfunded

[About a fifth of NHS staff in England are not British, and over a third of doctors](#fact-work-and-study-routes/fifth-of-nhs-staff). [About 150,000 care jobs are done by people on health and care visas](#fact-work-and-study-routes/care-posts-on-visas). The government's migration advisers found that [the root cause of care's staff shortages is underfunding, which immigration could not solve alone](#fact-work-and-study-routes/mac-names-underfunding), and [care pay sits only about 5% above the legal minimum](#fact-work-and-study-routes/care-pay-near-the-floor). Cutting the migrants who do the work would leave the shortage and lose the staff.

### Checks meant for illegal migrants harmed people with every right to be here

The hostile environment is the set of checks on work, homes and services meant to make life hard for people here illegally. In the Windrush scandal those checks fell on people here lawfully, and [the state has since paid out well over a hundred million pounds in compensation](#fact-settlement-and-migrant-status/windrush-compensation-paid). The official review found that [the state denied people with every right to be here work, homes and services, and detained or removed others](#fact-settlement-and-migrant-status/windrush-record). Suspicion meant for people here illegally did not stay with them.

### Visas tied to one employer leave lawful workers open to exploitation

[Visas tied to one employer left care workers open to exploitation](#fact-work-and-study-routes/visas-tied-to-one-employer). [The Home Office cancelled a record number of employers' licences to hire migrants](#fact-work-and-study-routes/revocations-at-a-record), for underpaying workers and failing to give them promised work. Those figures count employers, not how many workers were exploited. Lawful migrants also pay heavily: [the fee for settlement is ten times what it costs the Home Office to process](#fact-settlement-and-migrant-status/ilr-fee-ten-times-cost), and [people on temporary visas are usually barred from most benefits](#fact-settlement-and-migrant-status/nrpf-costs).

### So invest in homes and services, and end the hostile environment

Britain should plan investment for the population it actually has, and build social housing, hospitals and care. [Councils are already building more affordable homes than at any time since records began in 1991-92, though only about one in six of the total](#fact-immigration-housing-and-public-services/council-building-record). It should fund care so it can pay a living wage, untie visas from employers, cut fees to cost, and stop barring families with children from public support.

A decent society gives priority to the worst off, and rules must mean what they say for people who came lawfully.
