The shortages people blame on immigration were caused by decades of low investment. Britain should invest in homes and services, and stop making migrants' lives deliberately hard.

### Britain has invested less than most rich countries for decades

[The UK government invested a smaller share of GDP than the typical rich country in every year for three decades](#fact-immigration-housing-and-public-services/public-investment-low). [For over a decade, England has built fewer homes for social rent each year than in any year of the two decades before](#fact-immigration-housing-and-public-services/social-rent-building-fell-after-2010). [Britain also spent a smaller share of GDP than the average rich country on health buildings and equipment](#fact-immigration-housing-and-public-services/health-capital-below-average). While [health spending per person has almost doubled since 2000](#fact-immigration-housing-and-public-services/health-spending-per-person-doubled), the gap is in investment: homes, buildings and equipment.

### Migrants staff the NHS and care homes, and care is short because it is underfunded

[About a fifth of NHS staff in England are not British, and over a third of doctors](#fact-work-and-study-routes/fifth-of-nhs-staff). [About 150,000 care jobs are done by people on health and care visas](#fact-work-and-study-routes/care-posts-on-visas). The government's migration advisers found that [the root cause of care's staff shortages is underfunding](#fact-work-and-study-routes/mac-names-underfunding). [Care pay sits only about 5% above the legal minimum](#fact-work-and-study-routes/care-pay-near-the-floor).

### Britain's visa rules leave migrants open to exploitation

The hostile environment is the set of rules that make migrants' lives hard on purpose. [Visas tied to one employer left care workers open to exploitation](#fact-work-and-study-routes/visas-tied-to-one-employer). [The Home Office cancelled a record number of employers' licences to hire migrants](#fact-work-and-study-routes/revocations-at-a-record), [for underpaying workers and failing to give them promised work](#fact-work-and-study-routes/licence-revocation-reasons). Those figures count employers, not how many workers were exploited. [People on temporary visas are usually barred from most benefits](#fact-settlement-and-migrant-status/nrpf-costs), and [the councils that report spent £94 million supporting them in one year](#fact-settlement-and-migrant-status/nrpf-council-costs). [The fee for settlement is ten times what it costs the Home Office to process](#fact-settlement-and-migrant-status/ilr-fee-ten-times-cost).

### Hate crime spikes after major events, so politicians should take care whom they blame

[Racially and religiously motivated crimes spike after major events](#fact-crime-terrorism-and-group-exploitation/hate-crime-spikes-follow-events). [After the Southport murders, August 2024 was the worst month on record](#fact-crime-terrorism-and-group-exploitation/august-2024-worst-month). The figures cannot show how much the politics around these events added. Still, the risk is reason enough for care.

### So invest in homes and services, and end the hostile environment

Britain should invest in social housing and the NHS, and fund care so it can pay a living wage. It should untie visas from employers and cut fees to cost. And it should stop barring families with children from public support.

While [immigration does add to housing costs](#fact-immigration-housing-and-public-services/rent-effects), [it explains only a small part of the rise in house prices](#fact-immigration-housing-and-public-services/immigration-explains-4-6-percent-of-house-price-rise). The answer is to build. [Councils are already building more affordable homes than at any time since records began in 1991-92, though only about one in six of the total](#fact-immigration-housing-and-public-services/council-building-record). A decent society gives priority to the worst off, and rules must mean what they say for people who came lawfully.
