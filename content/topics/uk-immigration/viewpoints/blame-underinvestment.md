The shortages people blame on immigration were caused by decades of low investment. Britain should invest in homes and services, and stop making migrants' lives deliberately hard.

### Britain has invested less than most rich countries for decades

[The UK government invested a smaller share of GDP than the typical rich country in every year for three decades](#fact-immigration-housing-and-public-services/public-investment-low). [It spent a smaller share of GDP than the average rich country on health buildings and equipment](#fact-immigration-housing-and-public-services/health-capital-below-average). [In England, building of homes for social rent fell by more than four-fifths after 2010, and is still under a third of its old level](#fact-immigration-housing-and-public-services/social-rent-building-fell-after-2010).

[Housebuilding peaked in 2019-20](#fact-immigration-housing-and-public-services/building-peaked-before-migration), before [net migration peaked in 2023](#fact-immigration-against-the-long-run/net-migration-peaked), and [much public investment is fixed up to the end of a spending period, so it does not respond straight away to the population](#fact-immigration-housing-and-public-services/investment-does-not-adjust). While [school funding per pupil is higher than in 2010-11](#fact-immigration-housing-and-public-services/day-to-day-spending-per-head-above-2010) and [health spending per person has almost doubled since 2000](#fact-immigration-housing-and-public-services/health-spending-per-person-doubled), that shows where the gap is: in homes, buildings and equipment.

### Migrants make up a growing share of NHS staff

[About a fifth of NHS staff in England are not British, and over a third of doctors](#fact-work-and-study-routes/fifth-of-nhs-staff). [About 150,000 care jobs are done by people on health and care visas](#fact-work-and-study-routes/care-posts-on-visas).

Care is short of staff because it is underfunded. The government's migration advisers said so: [the root cause of care's staffing problems is underfunding, and immigration could not solve that alone](#fact-work-and-study-routes/mac-names-underfunding). [Care pay sits about 5% above the legal minimum](#fact-work-and-study-routes/care-pay-near-the-floor).

### Britain's visa rules leave migrants open to exploitation

The hostile environment is the set of rules that make migrants' lives hard on purpose. [Visas tied to one employer left care workers open to exploitation](#fact-work-and-study-routes/visas-tied-to-one-employer). [The Home Office cancelled a record number of employers' licences to hire migrants](#fact-work-and-study-routes/revocations-at-a-record), [for underpaying workers and failing to give them promised work](#fact-work-and-study-routes/licence-revocation-reasons). Yet those figures count employers, not how many workers were exploited.

[People on temporary visas are usually barred from most benefits](#fact-settlement-and-migrant-status/nrpf-costs). [The councils that report spent £94 million supporting them in one year](#fact-settlement-and-migrant-status/nrpf-council-costs). [Settlement, the right to stay for good, costs ten times what it costs to process](#fact-settlement-and-migrant-status/ilr-fee-ten-times-cost). While [the yearly health charge is set at the average person's expected use](#fact-settlement-and-migrant-status/surcharge-matches-average-use), migrants pay it on top of the taxes that fund the NHS. And the Windrush scandal showed where suspicion leads: [people with every right to be here were detained, removed and denied work and homes](#fact-settlement-and-migrant-status/windrush-record).

### Hate crime has spiked after major events

[Racially and religiously motivated crimes spike after major events](#fact-crime-terrorism-and-group-exploitation/hate-crime-spikes-follow-events), and [after the Southport murders, August 2024 was the worst month on record](#fact-crime-terrorism-and-group-exploitation/august-2024-worst-month). While the figures cannot separate the events from the politics around them, that is reason enough for politicians to take care whom they blame.

### Invest in homes and services, and end the hostile environment

While [immigration does add to housing costs](#fact-immigration-housing-and-public-services/rent-effects), [immigration explains only a small part of the rise in house prices](#fact-immigration-housing-and-public-services/immigration-explains-4-6-percent-of-house-price-rise). The answer is to build. [Councils are already building more affordable homes than at any time since records began in 1991-92, though only about one in six of the total](#fact-immigration-housing-and-public-services/council-building-record).

A decent society gives [priority to the worst off](#principle-priority-to-the-worst-off), and [rules must mean what they say](#principle-rules-must-mean-what-they-say) for people who came lawfully. Britain should invest in social housing and the NHS, and fund care so it can pay a living wage. It should untie visas from employers and cut fees to cost. And it should stop barring families with children from public support.
