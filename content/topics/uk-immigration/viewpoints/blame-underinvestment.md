Invest in homes and services, and stop making migrants' lives deliberately hard."

The shortages people blame on immigration were built by decisions about investment. [UK government investment was below the OECD median in every year from 1995 to 2024](#fact-immigration-housing-and-public-services/public-investment-low), and [health capital spending is below the OECD average](#fact-immigration-housing-and-public-services/health-capital-below-average). [Social rent building fell by more than four-fifths after 2010-11 and is still less than a third of its earlier level](#fact-immigration-housing-and-public-services/social-rent-building-fell-after-2010). [Housebuilding started falling before migration peaked](#fact-immigration-housing-and-public-services/building-peaked-before-migration), and [public investment is fixed in cash for years at a time, so it does not grow with the population](#fact-immigration-housing-and-public-services/investment-does-not-adjust). Day-to-day spending tells a different story — [health spending per person has nearly doubled since 2000, and school funding per pupil is above its 2010 level](#fact-immigration-housing-and-public-services/day-to-day-spending-per-head-above-2010) — which is why the gap shows up in homes, buildings and equipment.

Migrants hold up what is left. [About a fifth of NHS staff in England, and over a third of doctors, are not British](#fact-work-and-study-routes/fifth-of-nhs-staff), and [about 150,000 care posts were held by people on Health and Care visas](#fact-work-and-study-routes/british-held-posts-fell). The committee that advised adding care workers to the visa route said [the root cause of care's staffing problems is underfunding](#fact-work-and-study-routes/mac-names-underfunding), and [care pay sits pennies above the legal minimum](#fact-work-and-study-routes/care-pay-near-the-floor). It also said [immigration could not solve that alone](#fact-work-and-study-routes/mac-names-underfunding).

The hostile environment adds harm of its own. [Visas tied to one employer left care workers open to exploitation](#fact-work-and-study-routes/mac-names-underfunding), and [sponsor licences were revoked in record numbers for underpaying workers and failing to provide promised work](#fact-work-and-study-routes/revocations-at-a-record). Nobody [counts how many people are exploited](#fact-work-and-study-routes/exploitation-not-measured), or [how many people have no recourse to public funds, though the councils that report spent £94 million supporting them in one year](#fact-settlement-and-migrant-status/nrpf-costs). [Indefinite leave costs £3,226 against a processing cost of £310](#fact-settlement-and-migrant-status/ilr-fee-ten-times-cost), on top of [a health surcharge paid in addition to tax](#fact-settlement-and-migrant-status/surcharge-matches-average-use), even if the surcharge itself is set at average use. And the Windrush scandal showed where suspicion leads: [people with every right to be here were detained, removed and denied work and homes](#fact-settlement-and-migrant-status/windrush-record).

Blame has consequences. [Racially and religiously aggravated offences peak after events, and August 2024, after the Southport murders, was the worst month on record](#fact-crime-terrorism-and-group-exploitation/hate-crime-spikes-follow-events). The statistics cannot separate the events from the politics around them; that is reason enough for politicians to take care what they blame.

Population growth does add to housing demand, [if modestly](#fact-immigration-housing-and-public-services/immigration-explains-4-6-percent-of-house-price-rise). The answer is to build, and [councils are already building more homes than at any time since records began, from a low base](#fact-immigration-housing-and-public-services/council-building-record). A decent society gives [priority to the worst off](#principle-priority-to-the-worst-off), and [rules must mean what they say](#principle-rules-must-mean-what-they-say) for people who came lawfully. Invest in social housing and the NHS, fund care so it can pay a living wage, untie visas from employers, cut fees to cost, and end no recourse to public funds for families with children.
