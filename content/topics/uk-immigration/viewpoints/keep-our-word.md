People who came to Britain lawfully, on a path the state set out, should be allowed to finish it. Parliament can change the rules for people who have not yet come.

### Millions of people built their lives on the terms Britain offered

Settlement, or indefinite leave to remain, is the right to stay in Britain for good. [About 2.2 million people were on a five-year path to it at the end of 2024](#fact-settlement-and-migrant-status/path-2-2-million). They took jobs, moved their families and put their children in school.

They have also paid. [Over five years, a skilled worker pays thousands of pounds in fees and a yearly health charge, on top of tax](#fact-settlement-and-migrant-status/surcharge-matches-average-use). [The settlement fee alone is ten times what it costs the Home Office to process](#fact-settlement-and-migrant-status/ilr-fee-ten-times-cost). Changing the terms halfway tells them, and everyone thinking of coming, that Britain's word is worth nothing.

### Britain usually protects people already on a route, and courts have enforced it

The government once toughened the rules for skilled migrants already here. [In 2008 the High Court ruled that the scheme had made "a clear representation", and that changing it midway was unfair](#fact-settlement-and-migrant-status/high-court-struck-down-mid-route-change-2008). [MPs found it "common" for the Home Office to protect people already on a route](#fact-settlement-and-migrant-status/transitional-protection-precedent). [For Hong Kong's British Nationals (Overseas), the government called the route "a historic and moral commitment"](#fact-settlement-and-migrant-status/guidance-said-may-be-able-to-apply).

While [the skilled-worker guidance says only that people "may be able to apply"](#fact-settlement-and-migrant-status/guidance-said-may-be-able-to-apply), and [rules have sometimes been changed for people already here](#fact-settlement-and-migrant-status/transitional-protection-precedent), nobody uproots a family for "may" unless they trust the state to mean it. [Rules must mean what they say](#principle-rules-must-mean-what-they-say), in the sense people reasonably took them.

### Keeping the promise keeps people Britain needs

[Skilled workers on the main visa earn a median of £56,600 a year](#fact-immigration-economy-and-public-finances/earnings-by-route). The government's advisers find that [skilled workers who stay are worth far more to the public purse than those who leave](#fact-immigration-economy-and-public-finances/gain-from-top-earners-and-stayers).

While [around half of adult skilled-worker dependants were not working](#fact-settlement-and-migrant-status/non-working-groups), and [care workers cost more than they pay over a lifetime](#fact-immigration-economy-and-public-finances/skilled-workers-gain-care-workers-cost), that is a case for different terms for future arrivals. It is not a case for breaking the terms given to people already here.

### Insecure status does real harm

[A ten-year wait would make Britain an outlier among rich countries, where five years is usual](#fact-settlement-and-migrant-status/ten-year-baseline-outlier). While [no one has shown whether longer waits drive skilled people away](#fact-settlement-and-migrant-status/behaviour-evidence-limited), Britain has seen what happens when lawful status is treated as doubtful. In the Windrush scandal, [people with every right to be here were detained, removed and denied work and homes](#fact-settlement-and-migrant-status/windrush-record). Reopening the status of lawful residents risks the same mistakes.

### Change the terms only for people who have not yet come

The public draws the line in the same place. [Most people oppose taking settlement away from those who hold it](#fact-settlement-and-migrant-status/revoking-opposed-58-29). [Ending it for new arrivals splits the country evenly, and majorities want to keep it for full-time workers and NHS staff](#fact-settlement-and-migrant-status/ending-for-new-arrivals-splits). Parliament may set any terms it likes for people who have not yet come. Everyone who arrived on a five-year path should be able to finish it on the terms they were given.
