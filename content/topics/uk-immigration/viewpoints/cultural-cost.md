A country is more than an economy. It is a shared way of life: a language, laws applied equally to everyone, and values that people expect to hand on. Immigration on today's scale, from very different cultures, is wearing that away.

### Newcomers take years to join in, and Britain has far more of them

[The share of residents of England and Wales born abroad has roughly quadrupled since 1951, to one in six](#fact-integration-outcomes/foreign-born-share-quadrupled). Newcomers do join in, but slowly. [Recent arrivals are the most likely to struggle with English](#fact-integration-outcomes/recent-arrivals-struggle-with-english).

[Residents from outside the EU who came decades ago are far more likely to give a UK-only national identity](#fact-integration-outcomes/identity-rises-with-time). The faster people arrive, the more newcomers are still at the start.

### Some communities hold values far from the majority's

[British Muslims are markedly more conservative than the public on homosexuality](#fact-integration-outcomes/muslim-attitudes-homosexuality). A shared way of life rests on shared values, and on this one the gap is wide.

While [the same polls find their sense of belonging to Britain as high as the public's](#fact-integration-outcomes/muslim-belonging), belonging is not agreement. And while [the polls are few and each has a flaw](#fact-integration-outcomes/attitude-survey-methods), and [the evidence on whether younger generations differ is thin and mixed](#fact-integration-outcomes/attitudes-generations), no survey shows the gap closing.

### Fear of seeming racist let gangs abuse children, and the state still does not count offenders

In four towns, [inquiries described the gangs who abused children as Asian men or men of Pakistani or southern Asian heritage](#fact-crime-terrorism-and-group-exploitation/offenders-mostly-pakistani-heritage-in-places-investigated). [In Greater Manchester, about half of suspects in exploitation cases were Asian, against a fifth of residents](#fact-crime-terrorism-and-group-exploitation/asian-suspects-over-represented-in-areas-examined). [Official reports found that fear of seeming racist held officials back](#fact-crime-terrorism-and-group-exploitation/officials-feared-being-called-racist).

In Rotherham alone, [about 1,400 children were abused](#fact-crime-terrorism-and-group-exploitation/rotherham-1400-children). [Nearly two-thirds of the suspects in the National Crime Agency's investigation there were Pakistani, against 4% of the town](#fact-crime-terrorism-and-group-exploitation/stovewood-pakistani-share). [Some staff there were told not to mention the offenders' ethnicity](#fact-crime-terrorism-and-group-exploitation/rotherham-staff-told-not-to-identify-ethnicity). And [ethnicity is still not recorded for about two-thirds of abuse suspects in England and Wales](#fact-crime-terrorism-and-group-exploitation/ethnicity-unrecorded-nationally), so no one can say how big the problem is. [Rules must mean what they say](#principle-rules-must-mean-what-they-say), for every community alike.

While [most recorded child sexual abuse happens outside gangs](#fact-crime-terrorism-and-group-exploitation/most-child-sexual-abuse-is-outside-groups), that is no comfort to the children these gangs abused.

### Failure to integrate can grow, not fade, in the second generation

In Denmark, [men born to non-Western immigrants are convicted more often than the immigrants themselves](#fact-crime-terrorism-and-group-exploitation/danish-descendants-offend-more-than-parents). Problems can grow among people born in the country.

While [their conviction rate has fallen over time, and no comparable British figures exist](#fact-crime-terrorism-and-group-exploitation/danish-rate-fallen-and-no-british-figures), that gap is exactly what Britain does not measure.

In Britain, [Islamist extremists are the largest group of terrorist prisoners](#fact-crime-terrorism-and-group-exploitation/islamists-largest-group-of-terrorist-prisoners-most-british), and [most terrorist prisoners give their nationality as British](#fact-crime-terrorism-and-group-exploitation/most-terrorist-prisoners-british). While [the town inquiries describe the offenders' heritage, not their nationality or immigration status](#fact-crime-terrorism-and-group-exploitation/asian-pakistani-and-nationality-differ), a problem among people born here is still a failure of integration.

### Outcomes differ widely by origin, so who is chosen matters

In Denmark, [immigrants from the Middle East, North Africa, Pakistan, Afghanistan and Turkey cost the public purse on average, while Western immigrants pay in](#fact-integration-outcomes/denmark-menapt-vs-western). In the Netherlands, [region of origin produces gaps as large as the reason for coming](#fact-integration-outcomes/netherlands-by-region). So the mix Britain admits shapes how its newcomers fare on average.

While [those are one year's accounts](#fact-integration-outcomes/denmark-annual-not-lifetime), [for an intake that came especially for asylum or family reunion](#fact-integration-outcomes/denmark-intake-asylum-and-family), and [the average cost fell by about a third in five years](#fact-integration-outcomes/denmark-cost-fell-with-employment), the gap between the groups is still wide.

### So admit far fewer, choose for integration, and measure the results

A people is entitled to [continuity in its way of life](#principle-continuity-of-a-shared-way-of-life) and to [a say over who joins it](#principle-democratic-consent-over-membership). So Britain should admit far fewer people, and favour those likely to share its language and laws. It should record the ethnicity of every suspect, and publish how each group fares by origin and generation.

While [non-citizens are cautioned or convicted at about their share of adults](#fact-crime-terrorism-and-group-exploitation/non-citizens-share-matches-population), [most people say their area is one where people from different backgrounds get on](#fact-integration-outcomes/cohesion-stable), [segregation has fallen](#fact-integration-outcomes/segregation-fell) and [most minority groups outscore White British pupils](#fact-integration-outcomes/minority-pupils-outperform), national averages do not answer for Rotherham.
