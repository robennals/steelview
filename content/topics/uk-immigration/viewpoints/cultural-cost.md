---
name: Large-scale immigration from very different cultures is eroding Britain's shared way of life
summary: "A country can absorb newcomers who come to share its way of life, but where that has failed the cost has been grave, as [in towns where gangs abused children while officials feared being called racist](#fact-crime-terrorism-and-group-exploitation/officials-feared-being-called-racist). Admit fewer people, choose them with integration in mind, and measure how each group fares."
order: 5
---
A country is more than an economy. It is a shared way of life: a language, laws applied equally, and habits of trust people expect to pass on. [The share of residents born abroad has roughly quadrupled since 1951, to one in six](#fact-integration-outcomes/foreign-born-share-quadrupled). Newcomers do join that life, but slowly: [recent arrivals are the most likely to struggle with English](#fact-integration-outcomes/recent-arrivals-struggle-with-english), and [British identity grows over decades of residence](#fact-integration-outcomes/identity-rises-with-time). The faster people arrive, the more are at the start of that process at once.

Where integration fails, the cost can be terrible. In Rotherham, Telford, Rochdale and Oldham, [inquiries found most known offenders were men of Pakistani or southern Asian heritage, in towns where those groups are a minority](#fact-crime-terrorism-and-group-exploitation/offenders-mostly-pakistani-heritage-in-places-investigated). In Rotherham [about 1,400 children were abused](#fact-crime-terrorism-and-group-exploitation/what-each-inquiry-found), and [nearly two-thirds of the suspects in the national investigation were Pakistani, against 4% of the town](#fact-crime-terrorism-and-group-exploitation/asian-suspects-over-represented-in-areas-examined). [Officials held back for fear of being thought racist](#fact-crime-terrorism-and-group-exploitation/officials-feared-being-called-racist). The state still [does not record ethnicity for about two-thirds of suspects nationally](#fact-crime-terrorism-and-group-exploitation/ethnicity-unrecorded-nationally), and in 2020 the Home Office [concluded the offenders were most likely white on data that could not show it](#fact-crime-terrorism-and-group-exploitation/home-office-2020-conclusion). [Rules must mean what they say](#principle-rules-must-mean-what-they-say), for every community alike.

The inquiries describe [heritage, not nationality or immigration status](#fact-crime-terrorism-and-group-exploitation/asian-pakistani-and-nationality-differ), and [most child sexual abuse happens outside groups](#fact-crime-terrorism-and-group-exploitation/most-child-sexual-abuse-is-outside-groups). That makes it worse, not better: the failure ran across generations, in communities that migration built and the state was afraid to hold to the same standard.

Countries that measure outcomes by origin find large differences. In Denmark, [immigrants from the Middle East, North Africa, Pakistan, Afghanistan and Turkey are a net cost to the public finances on average, while Western immigrants contribute](#fact-integration-outcomes/denmark-menapt-vs-western), and [men born to non-Western immigrants are convicted more often than the immigrants themselves](#fact-crime-terrorism-and-group-exploitation/danish-descendants-offend-more-than-parents). Those figures are [one year's accounts, for an intake mostly of refugees](#fact-integration-outcomes/denmark-annual-not-lifetime), and the cost [fell as employment rose](#fact-integration-outcomes/denmark-cost-fell-with-employment). But [Britain does not follow immigrants and their children by origin at all](#fact-integration-outcomes/what-is-not-measured), so it cannot know whether its own picture is better.

Values differ too: [British Muslims are markedly more conservative than the public on homosexuality](#fact-integration-outcomes/muslim-attitudes-homosexuality), though [the polls are few and each has a flaw](#fact-integration-outcomes/attitude-survey-methods). [Islamists are the largest group of terrorist prisoners, and most are British](#fact-crime-terrorism-and-group-exploitation/islamists-largest-group-of-terrorist-prisoners-most-british), a failure of integration rather than of the border. Much is going well. [Non-citizens are convicted at about their share of the population](#fact-crime-terrorism-and-group-exploitation/non-citizens-share-matches-population), [segregation has fallen](#fact-integration-outcomes/segregation-fell) and [most minority pupils outscore White British pupils](#fact-integration-outcomes/minority-pupils-outperform). But national averages do not answer for Rotherham.

A people is entitled to [continuity in its way of life](#principle-continuity-of-a-shared-way-of-life) and to [a say over who joins it](#principle-democratic-consent-over-membership). Admit far fewer people, favour those likely to share the language and the law, record the ethnicity of every suspect, and publish outcomes by origin and generation.
