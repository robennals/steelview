---
name: Large-scale immigration from very different cultures is eroding Britain's shared way of life
summary: "A country can absorb newcomers who come to share its way of life, but where that has failed the cost has been grave, as [in towns where gangs abused children while officials feared being called racist](#fact-crime-terrorism-and-group-exploitation/officials-feared-being-called-racist). Admit fewer people, choose them with integration in mind, and measure how each group fares."
order: 5
---
A country is more than an economy. It is a shared way of life: a language, laws applied equally to everyone, and habits of trust that people expect to hand on. Immigration on today's scale, from very different cultures, is wearing that away.

### Britain has changed faster than newcomers can join its way of life

[The share of residents born abroad has roughly quadrupled since 1951, to one in six](#fact-integration-outcomes/foreign-born-share-quadrupled). Newcomers do join in, but slowly. [Recent arrivals are the most likely to struggle with English](#fact-integration-outcomes/recent-arrivals-struggle-with-english), and [feeling British grows over decades of living here](#fact-integration-outcomes/identity-rises-with-time). The faster people arrive, the more of them are at the start of that process at once.

### In several towns, gangs of mostly Pakistani-heritage men abused children for years

In Rotherham, Telford, Rochdale and Oldham, [inquiries found most known offenders were men of Pakistani or southern Asian heritage, in towns where those groups are a minority](#fact-crime-terrorism-and-group-exploitation/offenders-mostly-pakistani-heritage-in-places-investigated). In Rotherham alone, [about 1,400 children were abused](#fact-crime-terrorism-and-group-exploitation/what-each-inquiry-found). [Nearly two-thirds of the suspects in the National Crime Agency's investigation there were Pakistani, against 4% of the town](#fact-crime-terrorism-and-group-exploitation/asian-suspects-over-represented-in-areas-examined). [In Greater Manchester, about half of suspects in exploitation cases were Asian, against a fifth of residents](#fact-crime-terrorism-and-group-exploitation/asian-suspects-over-represented-in-areas-examined).

### The state looked away for fear of being called racist

[Official reports found that fear of being thought racist held officials back](#fact-crime-terrorism-and-group-exploitation/officials-feared-being-called-racist). [In Rotherham, some staff were told not to mention the offenders' ethnicity](#fact-crime-terrorism-and-group-exploitation/what-each-inquiry-found). The state still [does not record ethnicity for about two-thirds of suspects nationally](#fact-crime-terrorism-and-group-exploitation/ethnicity-unrecorded-nationally). Yet in 2020 [the Home Office said the offenders were most likely white, on data that could not show it](#fact-crime-terrorism-and-group-exploitation/home-office-2020-conclusion). [Rules must mean what they say](#principle-rules-must-mean-what-they-say), for every community alike.

### Integration can fail in the second generation as well as the first

While [the inquiries describe the offenders' heritage, not their nationality or immigration status](#fact-crime-terrorism-and-group-exploitation/asian-pakistani-and-nationality-differ), that makes it worse. The failure ran across generations, in communities that migration built. Other evidence points the same way. [Islamist extremists are the largest group of terrorist prisoners, and most terrorist prisoners give their nationality as British](#fact-crime-terrorism-and-group-exploitation/islamists-largest-group-of-terrorist-prisoners-most-british). In Denmark, [men born to non-Western immigrants are convicted more often than the immigrants themselves](#fact-crime-terrorism-and-group-exploitation/danish-descendants-offend-more-than-parents).

### Some groups fare much worse than others, and Britain does not measure it

In Denmark, [immigrants from the Middle East, North Africa, Pakistan, Afghanistan and Turkey cost the public purse on average, while Western immigrants pay in](#fact-integration-outcomes/denmark-menapt-vs-western). While [those are one year's accounts, for an intake mostly of refugees](#fact-integration-outcomes/denmark-annual-not-lifetime), and [the cost fell as more of them found work](#fact-integration-outcomes/denmark-cost-fell-with-employment), [Britain does not track immigrants and their children by origin at all](#fact-integration-outcomes/what-is-not-measured). So it cannot know whether its own picture is better.

Values differ too. [British Muslims are markedly more conservative than the public on homosexuality](#fact-integration-outcomes/muslim-attitudes-homosexuality). While [the polls are few and each has a flaw](#fact-integration-outcomes/attitude-survey-methods), they point the same way.

### Admit far fewer, choose for integration, and measure the results

While [non-citizens are convicted at about their share of the population](#fact-crime-terrorism-and-group-exploitation/non-citizens-share-matches-population), [segregation has fallen](#fact-integration-outcomes/segregation-fell) and [most minority pupils outscore White British pupils](#fact-integration-outcomes/minority-pupils-outperform), national averages do not answer for Rotherham. And while [most child sexual abuse happens outside gangs](#fact-crime-terrorism-and-group-exploitation/most-child-sexual-abuse-is-outside-groups), that is no comfort to the children these gangs abused.

A people is entitled to [continuity in its way of life](#principle-continuity-of-a-shared-way-of-life) and to [a say over who joins it](#principle-democratic-consent-over-membership). Britain should admit far fewer people and favour those likely to share its language and laws. It should record the ethnicity of every suspect, and publish how each group fares by origin and generation.
