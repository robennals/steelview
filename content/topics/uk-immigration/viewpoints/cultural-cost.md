A country is more than an economy. It is a shared way of life: a language, laws applied equally to everyone, and habits of trust that people expect to hand on. Immigration on today's scale, from very different cultures, is wearing that away.

### The foreign-born share has roughly quadrupled, and joining in takes time

[The share of residents of England and Wales born abroad has roughly quadrupled since 1951, to one in six](#fact-integration-outcomes/foreign-born-share-quadrupled). Newcomers do join in, but slowly. [Recent arrivals are the most likely to struggle with English](#fact-integration-outcomes/recent-arrivals-struggle-with-english), and [those from outside the EU who came decades ago are far more likely to give a UK-only national identity](#fact-integration-outcomes/identity-rises-with-time). The faster people arrive, the more of them are at the start of that process at once.

### In several towns, inquiries described gangs of Asian or Pakistani-heritage men abusing children for years

In Rotherham, Telford, Rochdale and Oldham, [inquiries described the offenders as Asian men or men of Pakistani or southern Asian heritage, in towns where those groups are a minority](#fact-crime-terrorism-and-group-exploitation/offenders-mostly-pakistani-heritage-in-places-investigated). In Rotherham alone, [about 1,400 children were abused](#fact-crime-terrorism-and-group-exploitation/rotherham-1400-children). [Nearly two-thirds of the suspects in the National Crime Agency's investigation there were Pakistani, against 4% of the town](#fact-crime-terrorism-and-group-exploitation/stovewood-pakistani-share). [In Greater Manchester, about half of suspects in exploitation cases were Asian, against a fifth of residents](#fact-crime-terrorism-and-group-exploitation/asian-suspects-over-represented-in-areas-examined).

### Fear of being called racist held officials back

[Official reports found that fear of being thought racist held officials back](#fact-crime-terrorism-and-group-exploitation/officials-feared-being-called-racist). [In Rotherham, some staff were told not to mention the offenders' ethnicity](#fact-crime-terrorism-and-group-exploitation/rotherham-staff-told-not-to-identify-ethnicity). The state still [does not record ethnicity for about two-thirds of suspects nationally](#fact-crime-terrorism-and-group-exploitation/ethnicity-unrecorded-nationally). Yet in 2020 [the Home Office said the offenders were most likely white, on data it called poor-quality](#fact-crime-terrorism-and-group-exploitation/home-office-2020-conclusion). [Rules must mean what they say](#principle-rules-must-mean-what-they-say), for every community alike.

### In Denmark, the second generation is convicted more often than the first

In Denmark, [men born to non-Western immigrants are convicted more often than the immigrants themselves](#fact-crime-terrorism-and-group-exploitation/danish-descendants-offend-more-than-parents). In Britain, [Islamist extremists are the largest group of terrorist prisoners](#fact-crime-terrorism-and-group-exploitation/islamists-largest-group-of-terrorist-prisoners-most-british), and [most terrorist prisoners give their nationality as British](#fact-crime-terrorism-and-group-exploitation/most-terrorist-prisoners-british). While [the inquiries describe the offenders' heritage, not their nationality or immigration status](#fact-crime-terrorism-and-group-exploitation/asian-pakistani-and-nationality-differ), a problem among people born here is still a failure of integration.

### In Denmark some groups cost far more than others, and Britain publishes no such accounts

In Denmark, [immigrants from the Middle East, North Africa, Pakistan, Afghanistan and Turkey cost the public purse on average, while Western immigrants pay in](#fact-integration-outcomes/denmark-menapt-vs-western). While [those are one year's accounts](#fact-integration-outcomes/denmark-annual-not-lifetime), [for an intake that came especially for asylum or family reunion](#fact-integration-outcomes/denmark-intake-asylum-and-family), and [the average cost fell by about a third in five years](#fact-integration-outcomes/denmark-cost-fell-with-employment), Britain publishes no such accounts for immigrants and their children by origin. So it cannot know whether its own picture is better.

Values differ too. [British Muslims are markedly more conservative than the public on homosexuality](#fact-integration-outcomes/muslim-attitudes-homosexuality). While [the polls are few and each has a flaw](#fact-integration-outcomes/attitude-survey-methods), the gap in this one is wide.

### Admit far fewer, choose for integration, and measure the results

While [non-citizens are cautioned or convicted at about their share of adults](#fact-crime-terrorism-and-group-exploitation/non-citizens-share-matches-population), [segregation has fallen](#fact-integration-outcomes/segregation-fell) and [most minority groups outscore White British pupils](#fact-integration-outcomes/minority-pupils-outperform), national averages do not answer for Rotherham. And while [most recorded child sexual abuse happens outside gangs](#fact-crime-terrorism-and-group-exploitation/most-child-sexual-abuse-is-outside-groups), that is no comfort to the children these gangs abused.

A people is entitled to [continuity in its way of life](#principle-continuity-of-a-shared-way-of-life) and to [a say over who joins it](#principle-democratic-consent-over-membership). Britain should admit far fewer people and favour those likely to share its language and laws. It should record the ethnicity of every suspect, and publish how each group fares by origin and generation.
