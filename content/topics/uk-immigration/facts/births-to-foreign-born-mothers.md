## Observations {#births-to-foreign-born-mothers--context}

### More than one in three babies born in England and Wales in 2024 had a foreign-born mother {#births-to-foreign-born-mothers--more-than-one-in-three-babies-born-in-england-and-wales-in-2024-had-a-foreign-born-mo}

, up from about one in four in 2008. These are births recorded here rather than moves across a border, so they appear in no migration statistic. [1](#source-births-to-foreign-born-mothers-1) [2](#source-births-to-foreign-born-mothers-2)

### The ratio is rising because its denominator is shrinking {#births-to-foreign-born-mothers--the-ratio-is-rising-because-its-denominator-is-shrinking}

To 2021 — the last year with official fertility estimates — migrant fertility was flat while UK-born fertility fell. [3](#source-births-to-foreign-born-mothers-3)

## Subtleties {#births-to-foreign-born-mothers--subtleties}

### What happened after 2021 is not decomposed, and that is where the steepest rise is {#births-to-foreign-born-mothers--what-happened-after-2021-is-not-decomposed-and-that-is-where-the-steepest-rise-is}

The share rose fastest between 2021 and 2024, and the fertility data stop in 2021. [4](#source-births-to-foreign-born-mothers-4)

### The growth is in children with two foreign-born parents {#births-to-foreign-born-mothers--the-growth-is-in-children-with-two-foreign-born-parents}

The share with one UK-born and one foreign-born parent has stayed roughly flat at about 13%. A reader who takes this as a restatement of "more migrants arrived" is missing that: the mixed-parent share did not move. [5](#source-births-to-foreign-born-mothers-5)
