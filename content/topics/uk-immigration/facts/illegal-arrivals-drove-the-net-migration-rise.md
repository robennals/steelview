The arithmetic settles this. Long-term immigration peaked at about 1.47 million
in the year to March 2023. Detected illegal arrivals have never exceeded about
46,000 a year — roughly three per cent of that, and they did not rise during the
surge in the way visa grants did. The post-2021 increase was made of student,
work and family visas issued by the Home Office under rules Parliament set.

This is worth stating plainly precisely because small boats dominate coverage and
polling. Someone who wants a much smaller headline number is talking about the
visa system, whether or not that is the argument they think they are having.
