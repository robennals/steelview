This is the single most under-discussed fact about the period. Ending free
movement did not reduce migration; it changed who came. EU migration reversed and
non-EU migration, which the UK has always controlled through its own visa rules,
rose to record levels. Whatever drove the 2021–23 surge, it was not the absence
of legal control — it was decisions taken with that control in hand.
