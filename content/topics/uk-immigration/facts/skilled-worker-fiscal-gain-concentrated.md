## Observations {#skilled-worker-fiscal-gain-concentrated--context}

### The route as a whole is clearly positive {#skilled-worker-fiscal-gain-concentrated--the-route-as-a-whole-is-clearly-positive}

The Migration Advisory Committee: "Overall, the SW visa route is clearly fiscally positive for the UK." That is close to guaranteed by the design, since main applicants need a job offer above a salary floor. [1](#source-skilled-worker-fiscal-gain-concentrated-1)

### The whole of the gain comes from main applicants {#skilled-worker-fiscal-gain-concentrated--the-whole-of-the-gain-comes-from-main-applicants}

Dependants have small lifetime contributions that are negative in aggregate, and within main applicants outside health and care, 72% of the gain comes from the top 30% of earners. [2](#source-skilled-worker-fiscal-gain-concentrated-2)

### On dependants the comparator changes the answer, so it has to be stated {#skilled-worker-fiscal-gain-concentrated--on-dependants-the-comparator-changes-the-answer-so-it-has-to-be-stated}

Against the resident population as a whole — mean -£39,000 — dependants look roughly level. Against residents matched on age, which is the comparison the MAC itself builds, they fall well short: skilled-worker dependants outside health and care contribute +£3,000 against a +£107,000 comparator, and health and care dependants -£67,000 against +£84,000. [3](#source-skilled-worker-fiscal-gain-concentrated-3) [5](#source-skilled-worker-fiscal-gain-concentrated-5)

## Subtleties {#skilled-worker-fiscal-gain-concentrated--subtleties}


### Main applicants beat their own age-matched comparator by a wide margin {#skilled-worker-fiscal-gain-concentrated--main-applicants-beat-their-own-age-matched-comparator-by-a-wide-margin}

Outside health and care they are far ahead of the +£117,000 age-matched comparator the MAC constructs for them. [4](#source-skilled-worker-fiscal-gain-concentrated-4)

### This is one route, not immigration {#skilled-worker-fiscal-gain-concentrated--this-is-one-route-not-immigration}

One cohort, one year, projected over a lifetime, on a route that requires a job offer above a salary floor. The MAC has not modelled the asylum, refugee, student or resettlement routes at all. [1](#source-skilled-worker-fiscal-gain-concentrated-1)
