The complicating source matters more than it looks. In a country running a
structural deficit, the median resident is also a lifetime net fiscal cost, so
"does this group pay for itself?" is the wrong question unless you ask it of
everybody. The right comparison is against an equivalently aged resident, and on
that comparison skilled main applicants outside health and care are far ahead
while their dependants are roughly level.
