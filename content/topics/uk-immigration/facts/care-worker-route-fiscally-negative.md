## Observations {#care-worker-route-fiscally-negative--context}

### The route mixes occupations with very different pay {#care-worker-route-fiscally-negative--the-route-mixes-occupations-with-very-different-pay}

The MAC's split is care workers on one side and "the other occupations" — nurses and doctors — on the other. [1](#source-care-worker-route-fiscally-negative-1)

### -£36,000 is close to the domestic benchmark, not far below it {#care-worker-route-fiscally-negative--36-000-is-close-to-the-domestic-benchmark-not-far-below-it}

The MAC's own comparison puts care workers "broadly similar to the UK median for the age group". [1](#source-care-worker-route-fiscally-negative-1)

## Subtleties {#care-worker-route-fiscally-negative--subtleties}

### It is a projection, not an observation {#care-worker-route-fiscally-negative--it-is-a-projection-not-an-observation}

A lifetime figure modelled from one cohort's early earnings. [1](#source-care-worker-route-fiscally-negative-1)

### It counts fiscal flows only {#care-worker-route-fiscally-negative--it-counts-fiscal-flows-only}

The MAC says explicitly that it excludes any effect these workers have on the cost of providing health and care to everyone else. [2](#source-care-worker-route-fiscally-negative-2)
