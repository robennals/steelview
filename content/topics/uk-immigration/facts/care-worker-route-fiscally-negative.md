The mechanism is wages, not nationality: care work is paid too little for
anyone doing it to be a net contributor under a progressive tax system. The same
arithmetic applies to a British care worker. What the visa route did was import
the wage level along with the worker, and then treat the resulting fiscal loss as
a fact about immigration rather than a fact about how the UK funds social care.
