## Observations {#asylum-grant-rate-fell--context}

### It fell from 77% to 39% in three and a half years {#asylum-grant-rate-fell--it-fell-from-77-to-39-in-three-and-a-half-years}

— 77% at the peak in the year to September 2022, 49% the year before last, 39% in the year to March 2026, back to roughly its pre-2020 level. [1](#source-asylum-grant-rate-fell-1)

### A grant rate is a fact about the caseload and the decision-maker together {#asylum-grant-rate-fell--a-grant-rate-is-a-fact-about-the-caseload-and-the-decision-maker-together}

Neither number tells you what proportion of arrivals "really are" refugees. [1](#source-asylum-grant-rate-fell-1)

## Subtleties {#asylum-grant-rate-fell--subtleties}

### Appeals are not in it {#asylum-grant-rate-fell--appeals-are-not-in-it}

These are initial decisions only. [1](#source-asylum-grant-rate-fell-1)
