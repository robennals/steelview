## Observations {#asylum-backlog-and-hotel-use-have-fallen--context}

### The initial-decision queue is 72% below its peak {#asylum-backlog-and-hotel-use-have-fallen--the-initial-decision-queue-is-72-below-its-peak}

49,000 people awaiting an initial decision at the end of March 2026, against 175,000 at the peak in June 2023, as the Home Office raised its rate of decisions. [1](#source-asylum-backlog-and-hotel-use-have-fallen-1)

### Hotel use fell by about a third in a year {#asylum-backlog-and-hotel-use-have-fallen--hotel-use-fell-by-about-a-third-in-a-year}

, to 21,000 supported asylum seekers, 21% of the total. [2](#source-asylum-backlog-and-hotel-use-have-fallen-2)

## Subtleties {#asylum-backlog-and-hotel-use-have-fallen--subtleties}

### Arrivals and backlogs cover different periods {#asylum-backlog-and-hotel-use-have-fallen--neither-fell-because-fewer-people-came}

Small-boat arrivals rose 13% in calendar 2025. The backlog and hotel-use statistics refer to March 2026; the series do not by themselves isolate why hotel use changed. [3](#source-asylum-backlog-and-hotel-use-have-fallen-3)

### Both track the Home Office's decision rate {#asylum-backlog-and-hotel-use-have-fallen--both-track-the-home-office-s-decision-rate}

The fall in the queue followed an increase in the number of initial decisions. [1](#source-asylum-backlog-and-hotel-use-have-fallen-1)

### What they do not show {#asylum-backlog-and-hotel-use-have-fallen--what-they-do-not-show}

is who should be admitted, whether the decisions were right, or what happens to those refused. A queue can be cleared by deciding faster in either direction. [1](#source-asylum-backlog-and-hotel-use-have-fallen-1)
