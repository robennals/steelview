The hotel split is the important line. A minority of people in the system were
absorbing most of the money, because hotels are the expensive fallback the Home
Office reaches for when claims are not decided fast enough to move people into
ordinary dispersed accommodation. Most of this cost is a processing failure
expressed in pounds, which is why it can fall sharply without a single fewer
person arriving.
