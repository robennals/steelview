---
question: Should benefits and social housing follow citizenship, or lawful residence and contribution?
kind: priority
divides: [end-settlement, contribute-and-earn, blame-underinvestment]
positions:
  - viewpoint: end-settlement
    holds: Citizenship. The welfare state belongs to its members, and non-citizens should support themselves.
  - viewpoint: contribute-and-earn
    holds: Contribution. Settlement, and the benefits it brings, should be earned by working and paying in, and should take longer for those who pay in less.
  - viewpoint: blame-underinvestment
    holds: Lawful residence and need. People who live and work here, and their children, should not be left destitute because of their visa.
---
Today the line is settlement: [people on temporary visas are usually barred from most benefits until they settle](#fact-settlement-and-migrant-status/nrpf-costs). What happens after that is barely measured. [No official data show what indefinite-leave holders earn or claim](#fact-settlement-and-migrant-status/no-official-data-on-ilr-holders-work-or-benefits), and [the Universal Credit count overstates them](#fact-settlement-and-migrant-status/uc-overstates-ilr).

The fiscal evidence cuts by group. [Around half of adult Skilled Worker dependants and a third of adults on family visas were not working](#fact-settlement-and-migrant-status/non-working-groups), and the government's advisers find [temporary migration better for the public finances among lower earners](#fact-immigration-economy-and-public-finances/gain-from-top-earners-and-stayers). On the other side, [councils spend tens of millions supporting families with no recourse to public funds, and nobody counts how many there are](#fact-settlement-and-migrant-status/nrpf-costs).

This is a choice between [a first duty to a country's own people](#principle-first-duty-to-its-own-people), [a share earned by contribution](#principle-contribution-earns-a-share) and [priority to the worst off](#principle-priority-to-the-worst-off). Official data on what settled migrants earn and claim would show how much each line costs; they would not say whose claim comes first.
