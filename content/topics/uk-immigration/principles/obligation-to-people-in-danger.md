---
name: Obligation to people in danger
heldBy: [honour-the-refugee-obligation, open-and-welcoming, restore-control]
---
Duties to people in serious danger are not extinguished by the fact that they are
strangers, and they grow stronger as the cost of helping falls and the ability to
help rises. Almost nobody rejects this outright. The disagreements are about how
far it extends beyond the people immediately in front of you, whether it can be
discharged by helping elsewhere rather than here, and what it takes to be
outweighed.

Even the most restrictive viewpoint on this page holds it in some form — its
complaint is that a system which cannot distinguish the people it is owed to from
the people it is not ends up honouring the obligation to nobody. The most
expansive holds that the obligation is prior to preference and that cost is a
reason to organise better, not a reason to refuse.
