People should have the power to choose their own leaders and shape the decisions
that govern their lives. Political authority rests on the consent of the governed,
and leaders must answer to the people who can vote them out.

The struggle against apartheid in South Africa makes the value of this principle
vivid: people excluded from political power claimed an equal voice in choosing
their government. The campaign for women's suffrage expresses the same ideal.
Each person's voice counts, and governing a people requires their consent.
