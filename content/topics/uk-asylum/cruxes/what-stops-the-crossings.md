---
question: What stops Channel crossings, the certainty of being sent back or a safe way to come?
kind: prediction
divides: [bar-and-remove, refuge, tighten-within-the-echr]
positions:
  - viewpoint: bar-and-remove
    holds: Certainty. People pay smugglers because a crossing usually ends in a life here. Once no illegal entrant can claim asylum or stay, the trade collapses, as it did in Australia.
  - viewpoint: refuge
    holds: Safe routes. People with a visa route do not get in boats. The nationalities crossing now are those whose main route was closed, and deterrence schemes have cost a fortune without sending anyone.
  - viewpoint: tighten-within-the-echr
    holds: Neither on its own. Fast decisions, returns deals and enforcement keep a route shut, as with Albania, while small, controlled routes take some pressure off. No single measure has been shown to work alone.
---
Each side has a best case. [Australia's boat arrivals fell to almost none after boats were turned back](#fact-deterrence-and-protection-policies/australia-arrivals-fell-after-turnbacks), though [which of its measures caused the fall is disputed](#fact-deterrence-and-protection-policies/australia-cause) and [turnbacks depend on the country of departure](#fact-deterrence-and-protection-policies/turnbacks-need-departure-country). [Ukrainians and Hong Kongers came in hundreds of thousands by visa and almost none by boat](#fact-safe-and-legal-routes/nationalities-with-safe-routes-rarely-came-by-boat), but [Afghans had a large route and crossed anyway](#fact-safe-and-legal-routes/afghans-and-syrians-had-both), and [a side-by-side count is not a test of cause](#fact-safe-and-legal-routes/not-a-test-of-cause).

The 2026 fall does not settle it. [Its cause is not established](#fact-channel-crossings-and-irregular-arrivals/what-caused-the-2026-fall-is-not-settled): [weather explains little](#fact-channel-crossings-and-irregular-arrivals/weather-explains-little-of-the-2026-fall), and [the fall came during a wider European one](#fact-channel-crossings-and-irregular-arrivals/the-uk-fall-is-within-the-european-range). [Albanian arrivals collapsed before the returns deal](#fact-channel-crossings-and-irregular-arrivals/albanian-arrivals-collapsed-before-the-deal) and [have stayed low as refusals and returns rose together](#fact-channel-crossings-and-irregular-arrivals/albanian-arrivals-have-stayed-low).

If removal without exception would stop crossings, the cost and legal risk of barring claims buys something large, and the case for routes rests on generosity alone. If routes open to the nationalities now crossing would empty the boats, deterrence is an expensive way to do worse. What would move people is a test neither side has: what happens to crossings by a nationality when a route for it opens or closes, as with [the pause in family reunion](#fact-safe-and-legal-routes/after-the-pause), or when its claims are reliably refused and returned.
