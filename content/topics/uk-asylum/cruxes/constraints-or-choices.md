question: Are Britain's refugee and human-rights treaties limits it must work within, or choices Parliament can revisit?
kind: priority
divides: [bar-and-remove, tighten-within-the-echr, refuge]
positions:
  - viewpoint: bar-and-remove
    holds: Choices. A treaty a government signed can be left lawfully, and the rules on who may stay belong to a Parliament voters can dismiss, not to a court they cannot.
  - viewpoint: tighten-within-the-echr
    holds: Limits, for now. Leaving is lawful but costly, and most of what goes wrong can be fixed in British law while staying in and pressing for a narrower reading.
  - viewpoint: refuge
    holds: Limits. The promise not to return people to danger is the point of the treaties, and it is worth most precisely when a government wants to break it.

[Democratic consent](#principle-democratic-consent-over-membership) and [rules that mean what they say](#principle-rules-must-mean-what-they-say) pull apart here. Parliament has already [tied the Refugee Convention's protection against penalties to the first safe country](#fact-asylum-law-and-the-echr/uk-law-ties-article-31-to-the-first-safe-country), a reading [UNHCR calls a misapplication of the treaty](#fact-asylum-law-and-the-echr/the-international-reading-is-still-adimis). [Leaving the ECHR is lawful in Lord Wolfson's advice](#fact-asylum-law-and-the-echr/wolfson-withdrawal-possible-refugee-convention-unnecessary), while [whether it breaches the Good Friday Agreement is disputed](#fact-asylum-law-and-the-echr/good-friday-agreement-is-disputed).

The public has not settled it either. [Remain has led withdraw since 2016](#fact-public-opinion-on-asylum-and-the-echr/remain-has-led-since-2016), but [the answer depends on whether the question offers a deportation power](#fact-public-opinion-on-asylum-and-the-echr/wording-moves-the-answer).

Evidence can show what a treaty costs, and what leaving it would cost, but not whether a promise to other states should bind a future Parliament. Someone who thinks the treaties block few removals can still want to leave on grounds of self-government; someone who thinks they block many can still want to stay because the promise matters more.
