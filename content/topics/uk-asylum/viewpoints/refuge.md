Britain has an [obligation to people in danger](#principle-obligation-to-people-in-danger). Britain should give refugees safe ways to come, fair hearings, and the right to work while they wait.

### Most people who cross the Channel are refugees

[Most small-boat arrivals who got a decision were granted protection](#fact-asylum-claims-and-decisions/small-boat-claims-mostly-granted). [The largest groups now crossing, Eritreans and Sudanese, are granted in the great majority of cases](#fact-asylum-claims-and-decisions/grant-rates-by-nationality).

### Many people refused at first win on appeal, so hearings must stay fair

[Two in five asylum appeals succeed](#fact-asylum-claims-and-decisions/two-in-five-appeals-allowed). [For claims made from 2007 to 2022, appeals raised the final grant rate by about 20 points on average](#fact-asylum-claims-and-decisions/final-outcomes-after-appeal).

While [grant rates have fallen since 2022, mostly within the same nationalities](#fact-asylum-claims-and-decisions/why-the-grant-rate-fell), some reflects [real change, such as Syria's government falling](#fact-asylum-claims-and-decisions/within-nationality-falls), and some may reflect [a higher bar of proof brought in that year](#fact-asylum-claims-and-decisions/standard-of-proof). That makes fair appeals matter more. Yet the government plans [a new appeals body whose judges need not be lawyers, with a chair the Home Secretary appoints](#fact-asylum-law-and-the-echr/immigration-and-asylum-bill). If [rules must mean what they say](#principle-rules-must-mean-what-they-say), the Refugee Convention's promise needs a fair hearing.

### Safe routes take people off the boats

[Hundreds of thousands of Ukrainians and Hong Kongers came on visas, and almost none by boat](#fact-safe-and-legal-routes/nationalities-with-safe-routes-rarely-came-by-boat). [For Eritreans, Sudanese and Iranians, the main route was joining family here](#fact-safe-and-legal-routes/family-reunion-was-the-main-route-for-boat-nationalities). [Since that route was paused, their boat arrivals have far exceeded the safe-route grants they get](#fact-safe-and-legal-routes/after-the-pause).

While [Afghans had a large route and still crossed](#fact-safe-and-legal-routes/afghans-and-syrians-had-both), [routes failed where they were too small or open only to some](#fact-safe-and-legal-routes/afghans-and-syrians-had-both). [The reopened resettlement scheme expects only hundreds of arrivals at first](#fact-safe-and-legal-routes/planned-scale). That is too small.

### Deterrence has cost a lot and achieved little

[Britain spent £715 million on the Rwanda scheme and a related law, and sent no asylum seekers](#fact-deterrence-and-protection-policies/rwanda-cost-and-relocations). [Australia's offshore camps cost billions](#fact-deterrence-and-protection-policies/offshore-cost-per-year), and [18 people sent there had died by 2021](#fact-deterrence-and-protection-policies/australia-outcomes).

While [Australia's arrivals fell to almost none after it turned boats back](#fact-deterrence-and-protection-policies/australia-arrivals-fell-after-turnbacks), [turning boats back depends on the departure country cooperating](#fact-deterrence-and-protection-policies/turnbacks-need-departure-country).

### People waiting should be allowed to work

[Work bans lower refugees' employment for years afterwards](#fact-deterrence-and-protection-policies/work-bans-reduce-employment). While [shorter bans show a modest link with where people claim](#fact-deterrence-and-protection-policies/work-rights-modest-association), [the study's authors judge that bans are not a strong deterrent](#fact-deterrence-and-protection-policies/work-rights-modest-association).

### Britain should open safe routes, decide claims fairly, and let people work

While [most issues raised to stop removal are eventually refused](#fact-removals-and-what-stops-them/most-issues-refused), the answer is quick, fair decisions. People refused after a fair hearing should leave. [The obstacles to removal are mostly late claims and legal challenges, not human-rights claims](#fact-removals-and-what-stops-them/late-claims-not-human-rights-claims). Leaving the European Convention on Human Rights [would leave the Refugee Convention's ban on return in place](#fact-asylum-law-and-the-echr/refugee-convention-ban-on-return-remains), and [could cost Britain police cooperation with the EU](#fact-asylum-law-and-the-echr/eu-may-end-law-enforcement-cooperation-on-denunciation).

Britain should reopen family reunion, build routes on the scale of the need, let people work, and fund hearings that get decisions right.
