A refusal should mean leaving, or [rules do not mean what they say](#principle-rules-must-mean-what-they-say). Most of what stands in the way is Britain's own doing. British law can fix it without leaving the European Convention on Human Rights (ECHR).

### Most of what stops removals is British, not European

[For people held for removal, the commonest obstacles are late asylum claims, medical reports, modern-slavery referrals and legal challenges](#fact-removals-and-what-stops-them/late-claims-not-human-rights-claims). [Human-rights applications are a small share](#fact-removals-and-what-stops-them/late-claims-not-human-rights-claims). [Foreign offenders' human-rights appeal wins equal only about 3% of deportations](#fact-removals-and-what-stops-them/human-rights-wins-about-3-percent).

### Clearing the queues is already working

[The queue for first decisions is down by more than three-quarters from its peak, and hotel use by over two-thirds](#fact-asylum-backlog-accommodation-and-cost/backlog-and-hotels-below-peaks). [Hotels held about a third of people but took three-quarters of the cost](#fact-asylum-backlog-accommodation-and-cost/hotels-third-of-people-three-quarters-of-cost), so closing them is where the savings are.

### The appeals queue needs a new law, and judges who apply it

[The queue has moved into appeals, which now take over a year](#fact-asylum-backlog-accommodation-and-cost/queue-moved-to-appeals). So the Immigration and Asylum Bill [replaces the tribunal with a new appeals body and rewrites how judges weigh family life](#fact-asylum-law-and-the-echr/immigration-and-asylum-bill). [Its official assessment models thousands more family-life refusals a year, though not as a target](#fact-asylum-law-and-the-echr/the-bill-sets-no-target). While [it expects many of those refused not to leave](#fact-asylum-law-and-the-echr/the-bill-sets-no-target), each refusal still opens the way to removal.

While [a 2014 law cut offenders' human-rights wins by three-quarters before they climbed back](#fact-removals-and-what-stops-them/article-8-reforms-cut-then-faded), Europe now backs the judges. [All 46 countries in the ECHR agreed that its court should need "strong reasons" to overrule national courts on family life](#fact-asylum-law-and-the-echr/chisinau-declaration). [That statement is not binding](#fact-asylum-law-and-the-echr/the-declaration-is-non-binding), but it gives British judges a reading every member signed.

### Fast refusals and real returns keep a route shut

[Albanian arrivals have stayed low since 2023, as claims were fast-tracked, nearly all refused, and returns rose](#fact-channel-crossings-and-irregular-arrivals/albanian-arrivals-have-stayed-low). While [the first fall came before the returns deal](#fact-channel-crossings-and-irregular-arrivals/albanian-arrivals-collapsed-before-the-deal), [researchers say the deal could be a major reason numbers stayed low](#fact-channel-crossings-and-irregular-arrivals/albanian-arrivals-have-stayed-low).

Protection should also last as long as the danger does. [After Syria's government fell, the share of Syrians granted protection dropped from 98% to 10% in a year](#fact-asylum-claims-and-decisions/within-nationality-falls). That is the case for [30-month protection, renewed only if return is still unsafe](#fact-deterrence-and-protection-policies/uk-2026-changes).

### Leaving the ECHR would fix little and risk a lot

[The Refugee Convention's ban on returning refugees to danger would remain](#fact-asylum-law-and-the-echr/refugee-convention-ban-on-return-remains). [The EU could end police and criminal-records cooperation](#fact-asylum-law-and-the-echr/eu-may-end-law-enforcement-cooperation-on-denunciation). And [whether leaving breaks the Good Friday Agreement is disputed](#fact-asylum-law-and-the-echr/good-friday-agreement-is-disputed).

### Britain should press on every stage at once, and keep the Convention

While [crossings fell sharply in 2026](#fact-channel-crossings-and-irregular-arrivals/arrivals-fell-sharply-in-2026), [the fall came during a wider European one](#fact-channel-crossings-and-irregular-arrivals/the-uk-fall-is-within-the-european-range), so no single policy can claim it. Britain also has an [obligation to people in danger](#principle-obligation-to-people-in-danger): [most Sudanese and Eritrean claimants are granted protection](#fact-asylum-claims-and-decisions/grant-rates-by-nationality). It should speed up decisions and appeals, narrow family-life claims in law, remove those refused, and stay in the Convention.
