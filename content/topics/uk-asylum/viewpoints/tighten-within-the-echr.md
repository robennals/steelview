A refusal should mean leaving, or [rules do not mean what they say](#principle-rules-must-mean-what-they-say). Most of what stands in the way is Britain's own doing. British law can fix it without leaving the European Convention on Human Rights (ECHR).

### Few challenges to removal are human-rights applications

A refusal should mean leaving, or [rules do not mean what they say](#principle-rules-must-mean-what-they-say). But [human-rights applications were a small share of what was raised against removal](#fact-removals-and-what-stops-them/human-rights-applications-small-share), and [most claims of risk on return rest on the Refugee Convention, not the ECHR](#fact-asylum-law-and-the-echr/refugee-convention-ban-on-return-remains). Leaving the ECHR would not touch them.

[In 2019, for people held for removal, the commonest issues raised were asylum claims, medical reports, modern-slavery referrals and legal challenges](#fact-removals-and-what-stops-them/late-claims-not-human-rights-claims). [From 2015/16 to 2020/21, foreign offenders' human-rights appeal wins equalled only about 3% of deportations](#fact-removals-and-what-stops-them/human-rights-wins-about-3-percent).

### Britain is already clearing its first-decision queue

[The queue for first decisions is down by more than three-quarters from its peak](#fact-asylum-backlog-accommodation-and-cost/backlog-and-hotels-below-peaks). Faster decisions cut the wait, and the cost with it.

[Hotel use is down by over two-thirds](#fact-asylum-backlog-accommodation-and-cost/hotel-use-far-below-peak). [Hotels held about a third of people but took three-quarters of the cost](#fact-asylum-backlog-accommodation-and-cost/hotels-third-of-people-three-quarters-of-cost), so closing them is where the savings are.

### Parliament is already rewriting appeals without leaving the ECHR

[The queue has moved to appeals](#fact-asylum-backlog-accommodation-and-cost/queue-moved-to-appeals), and the Immigration and Asylum Bill [replaces the tribunal](#fact-asylum-law-and-the-echr/bill-replaces-the-tribunal) and [rewrites family-life appeals](#fact-asylum-law-and-the-echr/bill-rewrites-article-8-appeals). Parliament is doing it while Britain stays in the Convention.

[Its assessment models thousands more refusals, not as a target](#fact-asylum-law-and-the-echr/the-bill-sets-no-target). While [many may not leave](#fact-asylum-law-and-the-echr/many-refused-may-not-leave), each refusal opens the way to removal.

Europe also backs national judges here. [All 46 ECHR countries agreed its court needs "strong reasons" to overrule national courts on family life](#fact-asylum-law-and-the-echr/all-46-agreed-strong-reasons). [That is not binding](#fact-asylum-law-and-the-echr/the-declaration-is-non-binding), but every member agreed it.

### Albanian crossings stayed low once refusals and returns sped up

[Albanian arrivals have stayed low since 2023](#fact-channel-crossings-and-irregular-arrivals/albanian-arrivals-have-stayed-low), as [nearly all claims were refused at first decision by 2024](#fact-channel-crossings-and-irregular-arrivals/albanian-claims-nearly-all-refused) and [returns rose](#fact-channel-crossings-and-irregular-arrivals/returns-to-albania-rose). Albania shows removal can work while Britain stays in the Convention.

[Albanian claims were prioritised](#fact-channel-crossings-and-irregular-arrivals/albanian-claims-prioritised). While [the first fall came before the returns deal](#fact-channel-crossings-and-irregular-arrivals/albanian-arrivals-collapsed-before-the-deal), [researchers say the deal could be a major reason numbers stayed low](#fact-channel-crossings-and-irregular-arrivals/deal-could-be-a-major-driver).

Protection should also last only as long as the danger. After Syria's government fell, [Syrians' grant rate dropped from 98% to 10% in a year](#fact-asylum-claims-and-decisions/syrian-grant-rate-fell). That is the case for the rule that [protection lasts 30 months, renewed only if return is still unsafe](#fact-deterrence-and-protection-policies/thirty-month-protection).

### Leaving the ECHR could cost police cooperation, and the ban on return would remain

[The EU could end police and criminal-records cooperation](#fact-asylum-law-and-the-echr/eu-may-end-law-enforcement-cooperation-on-denunciation), and [the Refugee Convention's ban on returning refugees to danger would remain](#fact-asylum-law-and-the-echr/refugee-convention-ban-on-return-remains). Britain could pay a real price and still not be free to send refugees back to danger.

[Whether leaving breaks the Good Friday Agreement is disputed](#fact-asylum-law-and-the-echr/good-friday-agreement-is-disputed).

### So Britain should fix every stage and keep the Convention

Britain has cut its first-decision queue, is rewriting appeals and has raised returns to Albania, all while staying in. It should press on with each, and stay in the Convention.

While [crossings fell sharply in 2026](#fact-channel-crossings-and-irregular-arrivals/arrivals-fell-sharply-in-2026), [the fall came during a wider European one](#fact-channel-crossings-and-irregular-arrivals/the-uk-fall-is-within-the-european-range), so no single policy can claim it. Britain also has an [obligation to people in danger](#principle-obligation-to-people-in-danger): [most Sudanese and Eritrean claimants are granted protection](#fact-asylum-claims-and-decisions/sudanese-and-eritreans-mostly-granted).
