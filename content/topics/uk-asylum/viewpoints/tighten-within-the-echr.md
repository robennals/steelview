A refusal should mean leaving, or [rules do not mean what they say](#principle-rules-must-mean-what-they-say). Most of what stands in the way is Britain's own doing. British law can fix it without leaving the European Convention on Human Rights (ECHR).

### Few challenges to removal are human-rights claims

[In 2019, for people held for removal, the commonest issues raised were asylum claims, medical reports, modern-slavery referrals and legal challenges](#fact-removals-and-what-stops-them/late-claims-not-human-rights-claims). [Human-rights applications were a small share](#fact-removals-and-what-stops-them/human-rights-applications-small-share). [From 2015/16 to 2020/21, foreign offenders' human-rights appeal wins equalled only about 3% of deportations](#fact-removals-and-what-stops-them/human-rights-wins-about-3-percent).

### Clearing the first-decision queue is already working

[The queue for first decisions is down by more than three-quarters from its peak](#fact-asylum-backlog-accommodation-and-cost/backlog-and-hotels-below-peaks), and [hotel use by over two-thirds](#fact-asylum-backlog-accommodation-and-cost/hotel-use-far-below-peak). [Hotels held about a third of people but took three-quarters of the cost](#fact-asylum-backlog-accommodation-and-cost/hotels-third-of-people-three-quarters-of-cost), so closing them is where the savings are.

### The new Bill would overhaul appeals

The Immigration and Asylum Bill [replaces the tribunal](#fact-asylum-law-and-the-echr/bill-replaces-the-tribunal) and [rewrites family-life appeals](#fact-asylum-law-and-the-echr/bill-rewrites-article-8-appeals). [The queue has moved there](#fact-asylum-backlog-accommodation-and-cost/queue-moved-to-appeals). [Its assessment models thousands more refusals, not as a target](#fact-asylum-law-and-the-echr/the-bill-sets-no-target). While [many may not leave](#fact-asylum-law-and-the-echr/many-refused-may-not-leave), each refusal opens the way to removal.

While [the fall in offenders' human-rights wins around a 2014 law faded](#fact-removals-and-what-stops-them/article-8-reforms-cut-then-faded), Europe now backs the judges. [All 46 ECHR countries agreed its court needs "strong reasons" to overrule national courts on family life](#fact-asylum-law-and-the-echr/all-46-agreed-strong-reasons). [That is not binding](#fact-asylum-law-and-the-echr/the-declaration-is-non-binding), but every member agreed it.

### Albanian crossings have stayed low since the returns deal

[Albanian arrivals have stayed low since 2023](#fact-channel-crossings-and-irregular-arrivals/albanian-arrivals-have-stayed-low), as [claims were prioritised](#fact-channel-crossings-and-irregular-arrivals/albanian-claims-prioritised), [nearly all were refused at first decision by 2024](#fact-channel-crossings-and-irregular-arrivals/albanian-claims-nearly-all-refused), and [returns rose](#fact-channel-crossings-and-irregular-arrivals/returns-to-albania-rose). While [the first fall came before the returns deal](#fact-channel-crossings-and-irregular-arrivals/albanian-arrivals-collapsed-before-the-deal), [researchers say the deal could be a major reason numbers stayed low](#fact-channel-crossings-and-irregular-arrivals/deal-could-be-a-major-driver).

Protection should also last as long as the danger does. After Syria's government fell, [Syrians' grant rate dropped from 98% to 10% in a year](#fact-asylum-claims-and-decisions/syrian-grant-rate-fell). That is the case for the rule that [protection lasts 30 months, renewed only if return is still unsafe](#fact-deterrence-and-protection-policies/thirty-month-protection).

### Leaving the ECHR could cost EU police cooperation

[The EU could end police and criminal-records cooperation](#fact-asylum-law-and-the-echr/eu-may-end-law-enforcement-cooperation-on-denunciation). [Whether leaving breaks the Good Friday Agreement is disputed](#fact-asylum-law-and-the-echr/good-friday-agreement-is-disputed). And [the Refugee Convention's ban on returning refugees to danger would remain](#fact-asylum-law-and-the-echr/refugee-convention-ban-on-return-remains).

### Britain should press on every stage at once, and keep the Convention

While [crossings fell sharply in 2026](#fact-channel-crossings-and-irregular-arrivals/arrivals-fell-sharply-in-2026), [the fall came during a wider European one](#fact-channel-crossings-and-irregular-arrivals/the-uk-fall-is-within-the-european-range), so no single policy can claim it. Britain also has an [obligation to people in danger](#principle-obligation-to-people-in-danger): [most Sudanese and Eritrean claimants are granted protection](#fact-asylum-claims-and-decisions/sudanese-and-eritreans-mostly-granted). It should speed up decisions and appeals, narrow family-life claims in law, remove those refused, and stay in the Convention.
