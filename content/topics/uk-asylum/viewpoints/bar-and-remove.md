Anyone who enters Britain illegally should not be allowed to stay. Britain should refuse their claims, remove them, and leave European human-rights law if it stands in the way.

### Few refugees reach Britain without passing through other countries

[Few refugees can reach Britain without first passing through another country](#fact-asylum-law-and-the-echr/few-reach-the-uk-without-passing-through-another-state). [Since 2022, British law has expected a refugee who stopped in another country to have sought protection there](#fact-asylum-law-and-the-echr/uk-law-ties-article-31-to-the-first-safe-country). [More than two hundred thousand people have crossed in small boats since 2018](#fact-channel-crossings-and-irregular-arrivals/crossings-since-2018). The Refugee Convention, the United Nations treaty on refugees, [forbids sending a refugee back to danger](#fact-asylum-law-and-the-echr/article-33-forbids-return), and [its rule on illegal entry bars penalties](#fact-asylum-law-and-the-echr/article-31-is-about-penalties). Neither article says Britain must decide every claim.

### Most people refused on 2015–2021 claims are not recorded as leaving

[Fewer than half of those refused on claims made in 2015–2021 are recorded as leaving](#fact-removals-and-what-stops-them/about-half-of-refused-claimants-recorded-leaving). [Asylum claims were the commonest issue raised by people held for removal in 2019](#fact-removals-and-what-stops-them/asylum-claims-commonest-in-detention), yet [most issues raised led to no positive decision](#fact-removals-and-what-stops-them/most-issues-refused). [The appeals queue is more than fourteen times as long as in June 2022](#fact-asylum-backlog-accommodation-and-cost/appeals-queue-fourteen-times), and [an asylum appeal takes over a year on average](#fact-asylum-backlog-accommodation-and-cost/asylum-appeal-takes-72-weeks).

### Australia's boat arrivals fell to almost none once boats were turned back

A rule that rewards crossing keeps the boats coming. [Australia's boat arrivals fell to almost none after it began turning boats back and barring boat arrivals from ever settling](#fact-deterrence-and-protection-policies/australia-arrivals-fell-after-turnbacks). While [most small-boat arrivals who got a decision were granted](#fact-asylum-claims-and-decisions/small-boat-claims-mostly-granted), Britain can protect refugees through routes it controls.

### Changing the law inside the ECHR has been tried, and appeal wins soon climbed back

[Around a 2014 law, foreign offenders' human-rights appeal wins fell by about three-quarters, then climbed back most of the way within two years](#fact-removals-and-what-stops-them/article-8-reforms-cut-then-faded). Lord Wolfson, advising the Conservative leader, [judged that new limits inside the European Convention on Human Rights (ECHR) would probably fail in court](#fact-asylum-law-and-the-echr/wolfson-limits-inside-the-echr-likely-fail), and [that leaving is lawful](#fact-asylum-law-and-the-echr/wolfson-withdrawal-possible-refugee-convention-unnecessary).

While [no published source counts how many removals the ECHR blocks](#fact-removals-and-what-stops-them/echr-share-not-measured), [the counts that exist miss danger claims argued inside asylum cases](#fact-removals-and-what-stops-them/counts-miss-article-3-in-asylum-claims). So they may understate it.

### In one poll, a majority backed British laws to deport illegal migrants

Under [democratic consent](#principle-democratic-consent-over-membership), removal rules belong to a Parliament that voters can sack. [A majority in one poll backed replacing European rights law with British laws to promptly deport illegal migrants](#fact-public-opinion-on-asylum-and-the-echr/whitestone-54-percent), though [polls since 2016 have shown more people backing staying in the ECHR than leaving](#fact-public-opinion-on-asylum-and-the-echr/remain-has-led-since-2016). [The EU could end police cooperation if Britain left, but that would be its own choice](#fact-asylum-law-and-the-echr/eu-may-end-law-enforcement-cooperation-on-denunciation), and Britain could try to negotiate it.

### Britain should bar the claims, remove those who come, and leave the ECHR

While [the Conservatives' promised removals would be about 15 times today's enforced returns](#fact-removals-and-what-stops-them/150000-about-15-times-enforced-returns), [most returns are already voluntary](#fact-removals-and-what-stops-them/most-returns-are-voluntary), and [far cheaper](#fact-removals-and-what-stops-them/voluntary-returns-far-cheaper). Meanwhile [asylum support costs around £4 billion a year](#fact-asylum-backlog-accommodation-and-cost/support-costs-about-4bn-a-year). [A government's first duty is to its own people](#principle-first-duty-to-its-own-people). It should take back the power to protect them.
