## Observations {#absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count--observations-voided-elections}

### Most elections voided in Heritage's list involved absentee ballots {#absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count--most-voidings-absentee}

In this report's hand count of Heritage's file, about two-thirds of the voided or overturned elections involved absentee or mail ballots, and half turned mainly on them. [10](#source-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count-10) Heritage's list is a sample, so this is a share of known cases, not of all fraud. [11](#source-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count-11)

### Not every voided election was an absentee case {#absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count--some-voidings-not-absentee}

Eleven were not. Georgia's 28th House District in 2018 was overturned over out-of-district voters, Compton's 2001 election over ballot order, and Pembroke, North Carolina, redid elections over residency. [10](#source-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count-10)

### No voided election is in a long-standing all-mail state {#absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count--no-voidings-in-all-mail-states}

None of the 32 is in Colorado, Oregon, Utah or Washington. Their entries are individuals, often casting a dead or absent relative's ballot; one Washington man was caught because his wife's signature did not match. [10](#source-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count-10)

## Subtleties {#absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count--subtleties-voided-elections}

### This is a hand count of a sample {#absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count--hand-count-of-a-sample}

Heritage tags its entries inconsistently, so each election was coded from Heritage's own description; the chart note lists every case. A different reader could place one or two mixed cases differently. Elections voided without a fraud finding, or never entered by Heritage, are missing. [11](#source-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count-11)

## Observations {#absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count--observations-margin-and-invalid-ballots}

### The voided races were small and very close {#absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count--voided-races-small-and-close}

Most were city, county or primary races decided by a few dozen votes or fewer; Caddo Parish's sheriff runoff was decided by one. [10](#source-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count-10) The one federal race, North Carolina's 9th District, was decided by 905 votes. [1](#source-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count-1)

### Courts voided results when tainted ballots could have changed the winner {#absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count--invalid-could-change-winner}

In each charted case the invalid ballots outnumbered the margin, except East Chicago. There the Indiana Supreme Court ordered a new election anyway, holding that the scheme "perverted the absentee voting process", whether 155 or 100 ballots were invalid. [3](#source-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count-3)

## Observations {#absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count--observations-absentee-and-impersonation-cases}

### Both compilations list far more absentee fraud than impersonation {#absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count--absentee-far-more-than-impersonation}

Two separately built compilations, Heritage's and News21's, each find absentee-ballot fraud many times more often than voting in person under another's name. [10](#source-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count-10) [12](#source-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count-12)

### Detected cases are few against the mail ballots cast {#absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count--few-cases-against-ballots-cast}

Heritage's absentee entries span four decades. In the 2024 general election alone, 47,629,437 returned mail ballots were counted. [14](#source-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count-14) No source turns the case counts into a rate per ballot.

## Subtleties {#absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count--subtleties-absentee-and-impersonation-cases}

### Detected fraud is a floor, not a measure {#absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count--detected-fraud-is-a-floor}

Three things keep fraud out of the counts:

- **Pressure at home goes unreported.** Britain's 2016 fraud review heard that people hide household undue influence for reasons like those of domestic-abuse victims. [16](#source-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count-16)
- **Checks miss some signing by others.** Cobb County's 2020 audit found an envelope "mistakenly signed by the elector's spouse" that had passed. [15](#source-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count-15)
- **The lists are samples.** Heritage calls its map "a sampling" that is "not comprehensive". [11](#source-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count-11)

### A floor is not evidence that fraud is common {#absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count--floor-not-evidence-of-common}

That counts are a floor does not show the true rate is high. No source measures the undetected share. Large schemes did leave traces: North Carolina's was flagged by its lopsided mail vote, and Bridgeport's by drop-box video. [10](#source-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count-10) [9](#source-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count-9)

## Observations {#absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count--observations-absentee-cases}

### Organised absentee schemes have voided elections {#absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count--absentee-schemes-voided-elections}

North Carolina's elections board found its 2018 congressional race "corrupted by fraud, improprieties, and irregularities so pervasive" that it ordered a new election. [1](#source-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count-1) Courts did the same in East Chicago, Bridgeport and elsewhere (see the timeline). [3](#source-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count-3) [9](#source-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count-9)

### Each case was local, caught and rerun {#absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count--each-case-caught-and-rerun}

Every scheme in the timeline whose discovery is recorded was exposed by an election contest, an investigation or a paper trail, and the remedy was a new election. [1](#source-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count-1) [3](#source-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count-3) [9](#source-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count-9) No statewide or presidential result in this record was overturned for absentee fraud.

### Paterson: a court voided the election, but no one has been convicted {#absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count--paterson-voided-no-convictions}

A judge ordered Paterson's May 2020 council election rerun. [6](#source-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count-6) The winner's indictment was dismissed in April 2026 on speedy-trial and disclosure grounds; the state is appealing. As of May 2026 no one has been convicted. [7](#source-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count-7) [8](#source-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count-8)

### Carter–Baker called absentee ballots the largest source of potential fraud {#absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count--carter-baker-largest-source}

The 2005 bipartisan commission wrote that "absentee ballots remain the largest source of potential voter fraud", and that voters at home or in nursing homes are more open to pressure. It urged states to stop candidates and party workers handling absentee ballots. [13](#source-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count-13)

### The same commission found little fraud in all-mail Oregon {#absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count--carter-baker-oregon}

It found "little evidence of fraud in Oregon, where the entire state votes by mail", and "no evidence of extensive fraud in U.S. elections". It still asked Oregon for better precautions against intercepted ballots. [13](#source-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count-13)

## Observations {#absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count--observations-officials}

### Officials have manipulated the count, including through absentee ballots {#absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count--officials-manipulated-count}

In Lincoln County, West Virginia, the county clerk himself falsified absentee ballots and stuffed boxes, and in Philadelphia city commissioners were found complicit. [10](#source-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count-10) Chicago's 1982 fraud was done by election judges. [20](#source-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count-20) [18](#source-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count-18)

### Recent refusals to certify ended in certification {#absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count--refusals-ended-in-certification}

In Otero and Cochise counties in 2022, officials who refused or delayed certification were ordered to certify, and did. Two Cochise supervisors were indicted. [24](#source-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count-24) [25](#source-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count-25) Refusals since 2023 were not searched.

### No case was found of signature review used to change a result {#absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count--no-signature-review-case-found}

The record read contains no case of officials rejecting ballots on signatures to change a winner. The closest is the reverse: Arizona's losing candidate for governor claimed in 2022 that signatures were not checked, and the court found she had not proved it. [26](#source-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count-26) The search was incomplete (see Data still needed).

## Observations {#absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count--observations-in-person}

### Polling-place fraud of the same era produced more convictions {#absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count--in-person-machine-fraud-record}

Kansas City's 1936 fraud and Chicago's 1982 fraud, both run by officials at the polls, each produced more convictions than any absentee case in this report. [21](#source-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count-21) [18](#source-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count-18) [4](#source-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count-4) Absentee fraud is the larger category in modern compilations, but not the only large-scale kind on record.

## Observations {#absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count--observations-survey}

### A widely cited survey of mail voters is not a fraud estimate {#absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count--rasmussen-pointer}

A 2023 Rasmussen–Heartland poll reported that about one in five 2020 mail voters filled out a ballot "in part or in full" for a friend or relative. [27](#source-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count-27) Its questions include lawful help, and critics note an opt-in panel and results "wildly out of line" with signature-flag rates. [28](#source-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count-28) It is weighed beside stronger surveys in [the secret-ballot report](#fact-secret-ballot-and-postal-voting-abroad/rasmussen-heartland-weak-measure).

## About this data {#absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count--about}

**What is measured.** Named cases where a court, an election board or a federal prosecutor described absentee or mail-ballot fraud, or officials' handling of the count. Added to these are two compilations of detected cases (Heritage, News21) and the 2024 EAVS count of mail ballots.

**What is not measured.** Undetected fraud, and fraud as a rate per ballot. No source publishes either.

**How the Heritage counts were made.** The voided-elections chart is this report's own coding of Heritage's descriptions; the note under it lists every election. The type counts match on Heritage's own tags. Heritage presents the list as showing "vulnerabilities in the election process" and says it is a sample, not a complete record.

**Why there is no time series.** No source publishes a consistent count of absentee-fraud cases by year; the Justice Department's reports list examples, not totals, and the compilations differ in method.

**Commonly mistaken for.** A count of detected cases is not a count of fraud, in either direction. A low count does not show fraud is rare, and a voided election does not show fraud is common: the voided cases are local races caught by existing checks.

**Left out.** Wayne County, Michigan (2020) and Georgia's State Election Board (2024) are often cited as officials contesting certification, but only secondary accounts were reached, so they are not in the timeline (see Data still needed). Britain's postal-fraud court cases are in [the secret-ballot report](#fact-secret-ballot-and-postal-voting-abroad/uk-election-courts).
