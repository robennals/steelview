---
name: Ballots should be in by Election Day, and counted fast
summary: "An election should have an end. Ballots should reach officials by the close of Election Day, and states should count fast enough that the result is known within a day or so; states that do both show it can be done."
order: 3
---
An election should have an end. Every ballot should be in officials' hands when the polls close on Election Day, and states should count fast enough that the result is known within a day or so. Both are achievable, both are choices states can make, and both give the losing side less room to doubt the result.

A deadline is a rule, and [rules must mean what they say](#principle-rules-must-mean-what-they-say). Election Day is the day the election is held; a ballot that arrives a week later was not part of it.

The Supreme Court [left the choice of deadline to legislatures](#fact-ballot-deadlines-and-counting-speed/watson-left-it-to-legislatures), and [recognised that an Election Day deadline "avoids the appearance of fraud from late-arriving ballots"](#fact-ballot-deadlines-and-counting-speed/watson-appearance-of-fraud) as a reason a legislature may weigh.

States are choosing it. [The number of states counting late-arriving ballots peaked in 2020 and is back to its 2016 level](#fact-ballot-deadlines-and-counting-speed/back-to-2016-level). This is not a partisan quirk: [the states still counting late ballots include Texas, Alaska and Mississippi as well as California and New York](#fact-ballot-deadlines-and-counting-speed/grace-states-mixed).

A deadline has a cost, and it should be faced. [Late arrival causes a steady share of rejected mail ballots](#fact-ballot-deadlines-and-counting-speed/late-share-of-rejections), and [late rejections rose in some 2026 primaries](#fact-ballot-deadlines-and-counting-speed/late-rejections-rising-2026). Every deadline turns away someone who missed it. The answer is to mail ballots out early, provide drop boxes and tell voters plainly when to post.

A postmark is a weaker guide than it looks: [since 2024 the Postal Service postmarks mail at regional centres rather than where it was posted](#fact-ballot-deadlines-and-counting-speed/postal-delays-and-postmarks). A receipt deadline does not depend on where a postmark is stamped.

Fast counting is a matter of administration. [Most of the national vote is counted within hours](#fact-ballot-deadlines-and-counting-speed/most-votes-counted-within-hours). [Texas and Florida each counted more than ten million votes in about a day, while the slowest counts are mostly in heavy-mail western states](#fact-ballot-deadlines-and-counting-speed/slowest-states-mostly-heavy-mail). [Letting officials prepare mail ballots before Election Day speeds the first hours of the count](#fact-ballot-deadlines-and-counting-speed/preprocessing-speeds-first-hours).

Early processing alone is not enough: [its advantage is gone within a day, and Arizona and Nevada process on receipt yet still count slowly](#fact-ballot-deadlines-and-counting-speed/gap-gone-by-24-hours). In Nevada in 2024, [the lead changed on ballots that had arrived by Election Day](#fact-ballot-deadlines-and-counting-speed/nevada-2024-lead-change). Speed needs staff, early processing and ballots already in hand, together.

Late ballots are rarely decisive: in Nevada's two largest counties [they were under 1% of ballots cast, far below the Senate margins there](#fact-ballot-deadlines-and-counting-speed/late-ballots-small-in-nevada).

But a rule is not justified only by the elections it would have changed. [Mail ballots were central in the three most recent disputed results](#fact-confidence-and-disputed-results/mail-central-in-recent-disputes). Whether a long count by itself lowers confidence [has not been measured](#fact-confidence-and-disputed-results/count-length-not-separated). A democracy that wants [results the losers can accept](#principle-results-the-losers-can-accept) should not wait to find out.

Every state should require mail ballots to arrive by the close of polls, let officials process them weeks ahead, and staff its count to finish within a day or two.
