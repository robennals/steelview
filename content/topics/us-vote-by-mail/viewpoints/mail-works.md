Every voter should be able to vote by mail. The states that mail every voter a ballot show it can be done honestly.

### States that vote almost entirely by mail have not had the fraud critics fear

[Oregon, Washington, Hawaii, Utah and Colorado cast more than nine in ten ballots by mail](#fact-mail-voting-use-and-effects/all-mail-states-lead). Yet [none of the elections thrown out for fraud in the Heritage Foundation's list was in a long-standing all-mail state](#fact-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count/no-voidings-in-all-mail-states). A commission that warned about absentee ballots still [found "little evidence of fraud in Oregon"](#fact-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count/carter-baker-oregon). And [all-mail states reject ballots no more often than other states](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/all-mail-states-not-different).

### Mail ballots have their own checks, and problems can be fixed

[Every returned envelope is checked before the ballot counts, though the checks differ by state](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/checks-differ-by-state). When fraud has happened, it has been found. [The known schemes that overturned elections were exposed by investigations and paper trails, and the elections were rerun](#fact-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count/each-case-caught-and-rerun). [A 2020 audit in Cobb County, Georgia, found no fraudulent absentee ballots](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/cobb-no-fraudulent-ballots).

[About one returned ballot in a hundred is rejected](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/rejection-about-one-percent), and [rejection rates differ widely between states](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/state-rates-range-widely). While that is a real problem, it is a reason to let voters fix mistakes, not to take mail voting away. Where voters can fix problems, [more than half the ballots set aside were corrected and counted](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/more-than-half-cured).

### Mail voting helps the voters who find polling places hardest

[An equal voice for every citizen](#principle-an-equal-voice-for-every-citizen) means a vote people can actually use. [Voters with disabilities rely on mail more, and find it easier than the polling place](#fact-mail-voting-use-and-effects/disabled-voters-use-mail-more). [Older voters use it most](#fact-mail-voting-use-and-effects/older-voters-use-mail-most).

[Sending every voter a ballot raised turnout by about two points](#fact-mail-voting-use-and-effects/universal-mail-raised-turnout-modestly). [In studies from before the parties split over mail voting, it gave neither party an advantage](#fact-mail-voting-use-and-effects/no-partisan-tilt). Two points is small as a rate, but large as a number of citizens.

### Other democracies vote by post, and fix problems when they find them

[Germany cast almost half its 2021 vote by post](#fact-secret-ballot-and-postal-voting-abroad/germany-postal-share-rose), and [Switzerland votes about 90% by post](#fact-secret-ballot-and-postal-voting-abroad/switzerland-about-90-percent). While [Britain's worst frauds came after it let anyone vote by post](#fact-secret-ballot-and-postal-voting-abroad/uk-fraud-under-on-demand), it did not end postal voting. Instead, [it added identity checks and banned campaigners from handling postal votes](#fact-secret-ballot-and-postal-voting-abroad/uk-kept-on-demand-added-checks). [In recent years it has found no evidence of large-scale fraud](#fact-secret-ballot-and-postal-voting-abroad/uk-no-large-scale-fraud).

### Worries about privacy and trust do not justify shrinking mail voting

While [nearly a quarter of mail voters have someone else in the room](#fact-secret-ballot-and-postal-voting-abroad/mail-voters-often-not-alone), [few voters say they felt pressured](#fact-secret-ballot-and-postal-voting-abroad/pressure-rarely-reported). And [polling places are not fully private either](#fact-secret-ballot-and-postal-voting-abroad/in-person-not-fully-private). [People who vote by mail trust the mail count](#fact-confidence-and-disputed-results/mail-voters-trust-mail). [Trust in the count overall mostly depends on which side wins](#fact-confidence-and-disputed-results/confidence-follows-power), so shrinking mail voting would not fix it.

Mail voting has become a party issue. [Democrats now use it far more than Republicans](#fact-mail-voting-use-and-effects/democrats-use-mail-more), and [even Utah is moving to mail ballots on request](#fact-mail-voting-use-and-effects/utah-all-mail-and-opt-in). That is a reason to make the system trusted, not to shrink it. Every voter should get the choice, with a chance to fix signature problems, ballot tracking, and a ban on paid ballot collection.
