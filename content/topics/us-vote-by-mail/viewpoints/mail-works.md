---
name: Voting by mail works, and the states that use it prove it
summary: "Every voter should be able to vote by mail. The states that mail everyone a ballot have done it for years without the fraud critics fear, with checks a polling place lacks, and it helps the voters who find polling places hardest to reach."
order: 2
---
Every voter should be able to vote by mail, and states that mail every voter a ballot show it can be done honestly. A mail ballot gives a voter time, a paper record and a checked envelope. The fraud cases critics point to mostly happened elsewhere, and were caught.

Look at the states that do it: [Oregon, Washington, Hawaii, Utah and Colorado cast more than nine in ten ballots by mail](#fact-mail-voting-use-and-effects/all-mail-states-lead). Yet [none of the elections voided for fraud in Heritage's own list is in a long-standing all-mail state](#fact-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count/no-voidings-in-all-mail-states).

The Carter–Baker commission, wary of absentee ballots generally, [found "little evidence of fraud in Oregon"](#fact-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count/carter-baker-oregon). And [all-mail states reject ballots no more often than other states](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/all-mail-states-not-different).

Mail voting has checks of its own: [every returned envelope is checked, in ways that differ by state, before the ballot counts](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/checks-differ-by-state). [The schemes that voided elections, where the record says how they came to light, were exposed by election contests, investigations and paper trails, and rerun](#fact-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count/each-case-caught-and-rerun). 

[Cobb County's 2020 audit found no fraudulent absentee ballots](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/cobb-no-fraudulent-ballots). Where voters can fix problems, [more than half the ballots set aside were corrected and counted](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/more-than-half-cured).

The checks are not perfect. [About one returned ballot in a hundred is rejected](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/rejection-about-one-percent), and [rates differ widely between states](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/state-rates-range-widely). That is an argument for notice and cure everywhere, not for taking the option away.

[An equal voice for every citizen](#principle-an-equal-voice-for-every-citizen) means a vote people can actually use. [Voters with disabilities rely on mail more, and find it easier than the polling place](#fact-mail-voting-use-and-effects/disabled-voters-use-mail-more), and [older voters use it most](#fact-mail-voting-use-and-effects/older-voters-use-mail-most).

[Sending every voter a ballot raised turnout by about two points](#fact-mail-voting-use-and-effects/universal-mail-raised-turnout-modestly) and, in studies from before the parties split over it, [gave neither party an advantage](#fact-mail-voting-use-and-effects/no-partisan-tilt). Two points is small as a rate and large as a number of citizens.

Mature democracies do this too. [Germany cast almost half its 2021 vote by post](#fact-secret-ballot-and-postal-voting-abroad/germany-postal-share-rose), and [Switzerland votes about 90% by post](#fact-secret-ballot-and-postal-voting-abroad/switzerland-about-90-percent). Britain's worst frauds [came after it allowed postal voting on demand](#fact-secret-ballot-and-postal-voting-abroad/uk-fraud-under-on-demand), and it answered with [identifiers and a ban on campaigners handling postal votes](#fact-secret-ballot-and-postal-voting-abroad/uk-kept-on-demand-added-checks), not abolition. Since then it has [found no evidence of large-scale fraud](#fact-secret-ballot-and-postal-voting-abroad/uk-no-large-scale-fraud).

A kitchen table is less private than a booth: [nearly a quarter of mail voters have someone else in the room](#fact-secret-ballot-and-postal-voting-abroad/mail-voters-often-not-alone). But [few voters report pressure](#fact-secret-ballot-and-postal-voting-abroad/pressure-rarely-reported), and [polling places are not fully private either](#fact-secret-ballot-and-postal-voting-abroad/in-person-not-fully-private). Trust is real too: [people who vote by mail trust the mail count](#fact-confidence-and-disputed-results/mail-voters-trust-mail), while [confidence overall follows who wins](#fact-confidence-and-disputed-results/confidence-follows-power).

Mail voting has become partisan. [Democrats now use it far more than Republicans](#fact-mail-voting-use-and-effects/democrats-use-mail-more), and [even Utah is moving to mail on request](#fact-mail-voting-use-and-effects/utah-all-mail-and-opt-in). That is a reason to make the system trusted, not to shrink it. Every voter should get the choice, with signature cure, ballot tracking and a ban on paid ballot collection.
