Every voter should be able to vote by mail. The states that mail every voter a ballot show it can be done honestly.

### Bills in Congress and a presidential order would cut mail voting back

[Two bills would stop states mailing ballots except on request, and an order would have the Postal Service carry ballots only for voters on a state list](#fact-federal-and-state-power-over-mail-voting/federal-moves-to-narrow-mail). [Utah, after years of mailing every voter a ballot, is moving to mail ballots only on request](#fact-mail-voting-use-and-effects/utah-all-mail-and-opt-in).

Neither bill has passed, and [the Postal Service rule written under the order is blocked for November 2026](#fact-federal-and-state-power-over-mail-voting/usps-rule-blocked). Only that one election is covered, and [the Supreme Court let the order itself go ahead](#fact-federal-and-state-power-over-mail-voting/eo-14399-stands).

### In the all-mail states, no known fraud has overturned an election

[Oregon, Washington, Utah and Colorado cast more than nine in ten ballots by mail](#fact-mail-voting-use-and-effects/all-mail-states-lead). Yet [none of the elections thrown out for fraud in the Heritage Foundation's list was in one of them](#fact-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count/no-voidings-in-all-mail-states).

A commission that warned about absentee ballots still [found "little evidence of fraud in Oregon"](#fact-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count/carter-baker-oregon), where everyone votes by mail. And [in 2024, all-mail states fell on both sides of the national rate of rejected mail ballots](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/all-mail-states-not-different). Mailing everyone a ballot did not mean more ballots thrown out.

### Every mail ballot is checked before it counts, and known schemes were caught

[Every state checks returned envelopes before the ballot counts, though the checks differ](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/checks-differ-by-state). [Known schemes that overturned elections were exposed, and most of those elections were rerun](#fact-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count/each-case-caught-and-rerun).

That includes an election held entirely by mail. [In Paterson's 2020 council election, postal inspectors found bundled ballots and nearly a quarter of the ward's mail ballots were rejected](#fact-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count/paterson-was-all-mail). The election was rerun. While [about one returned ballot in a hundred is rejected](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/rejection-about-one-percent), where voters can fix mistakes, [more than half the ballots set aside were corrected and counted](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/more-than-half-cured).

### Mail helps voters who struggle at polling places, and mailing everyone a ballot adds voters

[Voters with disabilities report far less difficulty by mail than at the polling place](#fact-mail-voting-use-and-effects/disabled-voters-find-mail-easier), and [older voters use mail most](#fact-mail-voting-use-and-effects/older-voters-use-mail-most). And [sending every voter a ballot raised turnout by about two points](#fact-mail-voting-use-and-effects/universal-mail-raised-turnout-modestly).

The gain comes from mailing the ballot, not from letting people ask for one: [Texans who could newly vote by mail without an excuse switched how they voted, not whether](#fact-mail-voting-use-and-effects/eligibility-shifted-method). Two points is a great many citizens, and [it moved the Democratic share of turnout by only 0.1 points](#fact-mail-voting-use-and-effects/no-partisan-tilt), in a study of years before the parties split over mail voting. [An equal voice for every citizen](#principle-an-equal-voice-for-every-citizen) means a vote people can actually use.

### Other democracies vote heavily by post, and Britain fixed its fraud without ending it

[Germany cast almost half its 2021 vote by post](#fact-secret-ballot-and-postal-voting-abroad/germany-postal-share-rose), and [Switzerland votes about 90% by post](#fact-secret-ballot-and-postal-voting-abroad/switzerland-about-90-percent). When Britain found postal fraud, [it added identity checks and banned campaigners from handling postal votes](#fact-secret-ballot-and-postal-voting-abroad/uk-kept-on-demand-added-checks).

While [a court found thousands of postal votes falsified after Britain let anyone vote by post](#fact-secret-ballot-and-postal-voting-abroad/uk-fraud-under-on-demand), [in recent years Britain's Electoral Commission has found no evidence of large-scale fraud](#fact-secret-ballot-and-postal-voting-abroad/uk-no-large-scale-fraud).

### Voting at home rarely means voting under pressure

While [nearly a quarter of mail voters have someone else in the room](#fact-secret-ballot-and-postal-voting-abroad/mail-voters-often-not-alone), [in Oregon surveys 1% or fewer felt pressured by someone else's presence](#fact-secret-ballot-and-postal-voting-abroad/pressure-rarely-reported). An Oregon researcher found no sign that bribes or threats had been a problem.

The evidence is thin on both sides: [in one Oregon pilot about 5% said others had marked their ballots](#fact-secret-ballot-and-postal-voting-abroad/pressure-may-be-underreported). But [polling places are not fully private either: in the same national survey, more than one in five in-person voters had no privacy screen](#fact-secret-ballot-and-postal-voting-abroad/in-person-not-fully-private).

### So every voter should get the choice, under rules both parties can trust

[Democrats now use mail far more than Republicans](#fact-mail-voting-use-and-effects/democrats-use-mail-more), so the rules must earn both parties' trust. Every voter should get the choice, with ballot tracking, a chance to fix signatures and a ban on paid ballot collection.

[Voters of the president's party are usually the more confident in the count](#fact-confidence-and-disputed-results/confidence-follows-power), and [people who vote by mail trust the mail count](#fact-confidence-and-disputed-results/mail-voters-trust-mail). So low trust among one party's voters does not, by itself, show that mail voting fails.
