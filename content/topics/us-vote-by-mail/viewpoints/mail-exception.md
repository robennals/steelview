Voting should normally happen in a polling booth, where no one can see how you vote. Mail ballots should stay open to people who cannot get there, such as the sick, the disabled and people away from home. But they should not be sent to everyone.

### The secret ballot protects voters from being watched

Reformers brought in the secret ballot to stop vote buying. Before it, [a vote buyer could hand a bribed voter a ballot and watch him cast it](#fact-secret-ballot-and-postal-voting-abroad/secret-ballot-against-watching). Secrecy worked. [In Chile, landlords lost their sway over how local people voted once ballots became secret](#fact-secret-ballot-and-postal-voting-abroad/chile-secret-ballot-effect).

### A ballot filled in at home is not secret in the same way

A kitchen table has no booth. [In the best national survey, nearly a quarter of mail voters had someone else in the room](#fact-secret-ballot-and-postal-voting-abroad/mail-voters-often-not-alone). [Mail voters were also more likely to say their choices were not kept secret](#fact-secret-ballot-and-postal-voting-abroad/mail-voters-doubt-secrecy).

While Germany's top court upheld postal voting, [it said postal voting reduces public control of how the vote is cast](#fact-secret-ballot-and-postal-voting-abroad/german-court-reduced-control). [Honest elections must also be seen to be honest](#principle-verifiably-honest-elections). A vote cast where others can watch is harder to see as free.

### Mail ballots are the biggest fraud risk, and most known overturned elections involved them

[A bipartisan commission called absentee ballots "the largest source of potential voter fraud"](#fact-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count/carter-baker-largest-source). In the Heritage Foundation's list of fraud cases, [most elections thrown out for fraud involved absentee ballots](#fact-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count/most-voidings-absentee). [Known schemes were local and caught, and most were rerun](#fact-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count/each-case-caught-and-rerun), and [the known cases are few against tens of millions of mail ballots](#fact-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count/few-cases-against-ballots-cast). But caught fraud is only the part we can see: [checks can miss a wrong signature, as in Cobb County, Georgia, where an envelope signed by a spouse passed](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/cobb-spouse-signature-passed), though there the voter had filled in the ballot. That calls for caution, not comfort.

### Few countries let everyone vote by post, and Britain found fraud when it did

[Only 11 countries let every voter vote by post from home](#fact-secret-ballot-and-postal-voting-abroad/universal-postal-voting-is-rare). [After Britain let anyone vote by post, a court found thousands of postal votes falsified](#fact-secret-ballot-and-postal-voting-abroad/uk-fraud-under-on-demand), and [France banned postal voting in 1975 because of serious fraud](#fact-secret-ballot-and-postal-voting-abroad/france-abolished-over-fraud). [Britain kept postal voting for all, adding identity checks and banning campaigners from handling postal votes](#fact-secret-ballot-and-postal-voting-abroad/uk-kept-on-demand-added-checks), but only after the fraud had been found.

### Mailing everyone a ballot adds little turnout, and voters trust mail counts less

[Sending every voter a ballot raised turnout only about two points](#fact-mail-voting-use-and-effects/universal-mail-raised-turnout-modestly). And [in every Pew poll since 2020, fewer voters trusted the mail count than the in-person count](#fact-confidence-and-disputed-results/mail-below-in-person). While [voters of the president's party are usually the more confident in the count](#fact-confidence-and-disputed-results/confidence-follows-power), [results the losers can accept](#principle-results-the-losers-can-accept) are still easier to build on a way of voting that both sides trust. Mailing everyone a ballot gains little for that cost.

### So polling booths should be the default, with mail for anyone who needs it

Polling booths should be the default, with weeks of early voting. [Voters with disabilities rely on mail ballots more than other voters](#fact-mail-voting-use-and-effects/disabled-voters-use-mail-more). So anyone with a reason to vote by mail should get a mail ballot, and no one else.
