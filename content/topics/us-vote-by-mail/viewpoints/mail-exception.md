Voting should normally happen in a polling booth, where no one can see how you vote. Mail ballots should stay open to people who cannot get there, such as the sick, the disabled and people away from home. But they should not be sent to everyone.

### Mail voting for everyone has spread since 2016

[By 2026, seven more states are expected to let every voter vote by mail than in 2016](#fact-mail-voting-use-and-effects/no-excuse-states-grew). Mailing everyone a ballot is newer still: [six of the nine mostly-mail states began it statewide in 2019 or later](#fact-mail-voting-use-and-effects/recent-adoptions).

And the habit has stuck: [mail voting is still above its level before the pandemic](#fact-mail-voting-use-and-effects/mail-above-pre-pandemic).

### The secret ballot protects voters from being watched

Reformers brought in the secret ballot to stop vote buying. Before it, [a vote buyer could hand a bribed voter a ballot and watch him cast it](#fact-secret-ballot-and-postal-voting-abroad/secret-ballot-against-watching). Secrecy worked. [In Chile, landlords lost their sway over how local people voted once ballots became secret](#fact-secret-ballot-and-postal-voting-abroad/chile-secret-ballot-effect).

### A ballot filled in at home is not secret, and that changes how people vote

A kitchen table has no booth. [In the best national survey, nearly a quarter of mail voters had someone else in the room](#fact-secret-ballot-and-postal-voting-abroad/mail-voters-often-not-alone), and [mail voters were likelier to say their choices were not kept secret](#fact-secret-ballot-and-postal-voting-abroad/mail-voters-doubt-secrecy). That matters: [people who doubt their vote is secret are more swayed by others in how they vote](#fact-secret-ballot-and-postal-voting-abroad/secrecy-doubts-matter).

While [in Oregon surveys 1% or fewer felt pressured](#fact-secret-ballot-and-postal-voting-abroad/pressure-rarely-reported), [in one Oregon pilot about 5% said others had marked their ballots](#fact-secret-ballot-and-postal-voting-abroad/pressure-may-be-underreported), and [some British postal voters said another adult insisted on helping them choose](#fact-secret-ballot-and-postal-voting-abroad/uk-postal-help-insisted). A booth is built to rule that out. [Honest elections must also be seen to be honest](#principle-verifiably-honest-elections).

### Mail ballots are the biggest fraud risk, and most known overturned elections involved them

In the Heritage Foundation's list of fraud cases, [most elections thrown out for fraud involved absentee ballots](#fact-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count/most-voidings-absentee). [A bipartisan commission called absentee ballots "the largest source of potential voter fraud"](#fact-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count/carter-baker-largest-source).

Fraud has struck where every ballot was a mail ballot: [Paterson's 2020 council election, held entirely by mail, was voided](#fact-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count/paterson-was-all-mail). While [the known cases are few against tens of millions of mail ballots](#fact-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count/few-cases-against-ballots-cast), [detected fraud is a floor, not a measure](#fact-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count/detected-fraud-is-a-floor). [In Cobb County, Georgia, an envelope signed by the voter's spouse passed the check](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/cobb-spouse-signature-passed).

### Few countries let everyone vote by post, and Britain found fraud when it did

[Only 11 countries let every voter vote by post from home](#fact-secret-ballot-and-postal-voting-abroad/universal-postal-voting-is-rare). [After Britain let anyone vote by post, a court found thousands of postal votes falsified](#fact-secret-ballot-and-postal-voting-abroad/uk-fraud-under-on-demand). [France banned postal voting in 1975 because of serious fraud](#fact-secret-ballot-and-postal-voting-abroad/france-abolished-over-fraud).

While [Britain kept postal voting for all, adding identity checks and banning campaigners from handling postal votes](#fact-secret-ballot-and-postal-voting-abroad/uk-kept-on-demand-added-checks), those checks came only after the fraud had been found.

### Mailing everyone a ballot adds little turnout, and voters trust mail counts less

[Sending every voter a ballot raised turnout only about two points](#fact-mail-voting-use-and-effects/universal-mail-raised-turnout-modestly). And [in every Pew poll since 2020, fewer voters trusted the mail count than the in-person count](#fact-confidence-and-disputed-results/mail-below-in-person). So mailing everyone a ballot gains little, using a method fewer voters trust.

Letting everyone vote by mail mostly changes how people vote: [Texans who could newly vote by mail without an excuse switched how they voted, not whether](#fact-mail-voting-use-and-effects/eligibility-shifted-method). While [no study tests whether limiting mail voting would raise losers' confidence](#fact-confidence-and-disputed-results/would-restriction-raise-confidence), [results the losers can accept](#principle-results-the-losers-can-accept) are easier to build on a way of voting that both sides trust.

### So polling booths should be the default, with mail for anyone who needs it

Polling booths should be the default, with weeks of early voting. [Voters with disabilities rely on mail ballots more than other voters](#fact-mail-voting-use-and-effects/disabled-voters-use-mail-more). So anyone with a reason to vote by mail should get a mail ballot, and no one else.
