---
name: Mail voting should be the exception, kept for voters who need it
summary: "Voting should normally happen in a booth where no one can watch. Mail ballots should stay available to people who cannot get there, but sending them to everyone gives up the secret ballot and invites fraud that is hard to see, for little gain in turnout."
order: 1
---
Voting should normally happen in a polling booth, where no one can see how you mark your ballot. Mail voting should stay open to people who cannot get there: the sick, the disabled, people away from home.

But sending a ballot to every voter trades away the secret ballot, and opens a door to fraud that is hard to see, for a small gain in turnout.

The secret ballot was adopted because, without it, [a vote buyer could hand a bribed voter a ballot and watch him cast it](#fact-secret-ballot-and-postal-voting-abroad/secret-ballot-against-watching). 

It worked. In Chile, [the landlords' vote disappeared once ballots became secret](#fact-secret-ballot-and-postal-voting-abroad/chile-secret-ballot-effect). A kitchen table has no booth.

In the best national survey, [nearly a quarter of mail voters had someone else in the room, and mail voters were likelier to doubt their vote was secret](#fact-secret-ballot-and-postal-voting-abroad/mail-voters-often-not-alone).

The case does not need the higher figures from the Rasmussen–Heartland poll, which [is an opt-in poll whose questions count lawful family help as filling in a ballot](#fact-secret-ballot-and-postal-voting-abroad/rasmussen-heartland-weak-measure). 

Even [Germany's constitutional court, upholding postal voting, conceded that it reduces public control of how votes are cast](#fact-secret-ballot-and-postal-voting-abroad/german-court-reduced-control). [Verifiably honest elections](#principle-verifiably-honest-elections) need a vote that is both free and seen to be free.

The fraud record points the same way. [Most elections voided for fraud in Heritage's own case list involved absentee ballots](#fact-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count/most-voidings-absentee), from [North Carolina's 9th District to East Chicago and Bridgeport](#fact-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count/absentee-schemes-voided-elections). The bipartisan Carter–Baker commission called absentee ballots ["the largest source of potential voter fraud"](#fact-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count/carter-baker-largest-source).

Those cases were [local, caught and rerun](#fact-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count/each-case-caught-and-rerun), and [they are few against the tens of millions of mail ballots cast](#fact-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count/few-cases-against-ballots-cast).

But [detected fraud is a floor, not a measure](#fact-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count/detected-fraud-is-a-floor): pressure at home goes unreported, and [an envelope signed by the voter's spouse passed Cobb County's signature check](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/cobb-spouse-signature-passed). No one knows how much gets through. That is a reason for caution, not comfort.

Most countries do not offer it to everyone. [Only 11 of the 204 countries International IDEA codes let every voter vote by post at home](#fact-secret-ballot-and-postal-voting-abroad/universal-postal-voting-is-rare). 

[France abolished postal voting in 1975, citing serious fraud](#fact-secret-ballot-and-postal-voting-abroad/france-abolished-over-fraud), and [Britain's largest modern frauds came after it allowed postal voting on demand](#fact-secret-ballot-and-postal-voting-abroad/uk-fraud-under-on-demand). Germany and Britain still do it, but Britain [had to add identifiers and ban campaigners from handling postal votes](#fact-secret-ballot-and-postal-voting-abroad/uk-kept-on-demand-added-checks).

Trust matters too. [Voters trust the mail count less than the in-person count](#fact-confidence-and-disputed-results/mail-below-in-person), and [Republican confidence in it has stayed low](#fact-confidence-and-disputed-results/mail-confidence-by-party). Confidence [follows who wins more than how votes are cast](#fact-confidence-and-disputed-results/confidence-follows-power), so no rule will fix it alone. But [results the losers can accept](#principle-results-the-losers-can-accept) are easier to build on a method both sides trust.

Restriction costs little. [Sending every voter a ballot raised turnout by about two points](#fact-mail-voting-use-and-effects/universal-mail-raised-turnout-modestly), and [no-excuse eligibility changed how people voted, not whether](#fact-mail-voting-use-and-effects/eligibility-shifted-method).

The cost falls hardest on [voters with disabilities, who rely on mail more](#fact-mail-voting-use-and-effects/disabled-voters-use-mail-more), so the exception must be generous. The right system is a booth by default, weeks of early voting, absentee ballots for those who ask with a reason, and a ban on anyone else collecting them.
