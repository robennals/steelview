name: Mail ballots should not be thrown out just because a signature looks wrong to an official
summary: "Matching signatures is a judgement call. It is applied unevenly from county to county, and it rejects young, first-time and minority voters most. Voters must be told about a problem and allowed to fix it."
order: 4

A mail ballot should not be thrown out because an election worker thinks a signature looks wrong. Where states compare signatures, they should tell voters about a problem and let them fix it.

### Matching signatures is a judgement call

[Washington's state auditor called signature matching "inherently subjective", and its own reviewers disagreed about many signatures](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/reviewers-disagree). This matters because [a mismatched signature is now the most common reason given for rejecting a mail ballot](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/signatures-largest-reason).

### Courts have found the process unfair

In New Hampshire, [a federal court found that voters were not told their ballot had been rejected](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/new-hampshire-due-process). [The officials checking had no handwriting training or standards](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/new-hampshire-due-process). [Pennsylvania's top court ruled that its election law does not allow rejecting ballots because signatures do not match](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/pennsylvania-no-signature-rejection).

### It rejects some voters far more than others

[In Washington, Black voters' ballots were rejected at four times white voters' rate, counting unsigned envelopes too](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/washington-race-gaps). [The youngest voters were rejected most, even for mismatches alone, and first-time voters far more than others](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/washington-age-gaps). [Studies in Florida found the same for young and inexperienced voters](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/florida-young-and-inexperienced).

### Where you live matters more than anything else

[The county mattered most. For mismatches alone, two counties were 18 times as likely as another to reject a ballot](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/county-strongest-predictor). That falls short of [an equal voice for every citizen](#principle-an-equal-voice-for-every-citizen).

While [the auditor found no sign of bias in individual decisions](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/no-evidence-of-bias), honest differences in judgement produce unequal results. [It agreed with counties on almost every signature it rechecked](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/auditor-agreed-with-counties), but [counties that rejected fewer ballots accepted less certain matches](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/low-rejection-counties-accept-more). A standard that honest people apply this differently is not a standard.

### Leaving it to judgement is risky, and it does not catch wrong signatures well

[Officials have tampered with counts through absentee ballots before](#fact-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count/officials-manipulated-count). [No case was found of signature review being used to change a result](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/no-case-signature-review-changed-result), and [recent refusals to certify results ended in certification](#fact-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count/refusals-ended-in-certification). While abuse has not happened this way, rules should bind the powerful before it does, not after.

The check is also weaker than it looks. [In Cobb County, Georgia, an envelope signed by the voter's spouse passed](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/cobb-spouse-signature-passed).

### Better checks exist

Dropping checks altogether is not the answer. [Checking an ID number instead also rejects ballots](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/id-number-checks-also-reject). [Texas's rejections rose under one](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/texas-rose-under-id-number), while [Georgia's fell after it switched to one](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/georgia-fell-after-sb202). Design is what matters.

So every state that compares signatures should let voters fix problems, [as most must already](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/most-states-cure), train staff, have [two reviewers from different parties check every mismatch](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/training-and-bipartisan-review), and pair the signature with a fact that needs no judgement, as [Britain does with the date of birth](#fact-secret-ballot-and-postal-voting-abroad/uk-checks-identifiers).
