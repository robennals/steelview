---
name: Mail ballots should not be thrown out just because a signature looks wrong to an official
shortName: "Fair signature checks"
summary: "Matching signatures is a judgement call. It is applied unevenly from county to county, and it rejects young, first-time and minority voters most. Voters must be told about a problem and allowed to fix it."
order: 4
---
A mail ballot should not be thrown out because an election worker thinks a signature looks wrong. Where states compare signatures, they should tell voters about a problem and let them fix it.

### Matching signatures is a judgement call

[Washington's state auditor called signature matching "inherently subjective"](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/reviewers-disagree), and [its own reviewers disagreed about many signatures](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/reviewers-disagree). This matters because [a signature problem is now the most common reason given for rejecting a mail ballot](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/signatures-largest-reason).

### A federal court found New Hampshire's signature process flawed

In New Hampshire, [a federal court found that voters were not told their ballot had been rejected](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/new-hampshire-due-process). [The officials checking had no handwriting training or standards](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/new-hampshire-due-process). [Pennsylvania's top court ruled that its election law does not allow rejecting ballots because signatures do not match](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/pennsylvania-no-signature-rejection).

### It rejects some voters far more than others

[In Washington, Black voters' ballots were rejected at four times white voters' rate](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/washington-race-gaps), counting unsigned envelopes too. [The youngest voters were rejected most](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/washington-age-gaps). [Their gap held for mismatches alone](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/youngest-mismatches-alone), and [first-time voters were rejected far more than others](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/first-time-voters-rejected-more). [Studies in Florida found the same for young and inexperienced voters](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/florida-young-and-inexperienced).

### In Washington, the county of the ballot mattered most

[The county mattered most](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/county-strongest-predictor). [For mismatches alone, two counties were 18 times as likely as another to reject a ballot](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/county-strongest-predictor). That falls short of [an equal voice for every citizen](#principle-an-equal-voice-for-every-citizen).

While [the auditor found no sign of bias in individual decisions](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/no-evidence-of-bias), honest differences in judgement produce unequal results. [It agreed with counties on almost every signature it rechecked](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/auditor-agreed-with-counties), but [counties that rejected fewer ballots accepted less certain matches](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/low-rejection-counties-accept-more). A standard that honest people apply this differently is not a standard.

### Officials have tampered with counts before

[Officials have tampered with counts through absentee ballots before](#fact-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count/officials-manipulated-count). This report's sources contain no case of signature review being used to change a result, and [two refusals to certify results in 2022 ended in certification](#fact-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count/refusals-ended-in-certification). While these sources show no abuse of this kind, rules should bind the powerful before it does, not after.

The check can also miss a wrong signature. [In Cobb County, Georgia, an envelope signed by the voter's spouse passed](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/cobb-spouse-signature-passed). The voter had filled in that ballot.

### ID-number checks also reject ballots

Dropping checks altogether is not the answer. [Checking an ID number also rejects ballots](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/id-number-checks-also-reject). [Texas's rejections rose under one](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/texas-rose-under-id-number), while [Georgia's stayed below its 2016 and 2018 levels after it switched to one](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/georgia-fell-after-sb202). So the design of a check seems to matter.

So every state that compares signatures should let voters fix problems, [as 35 states and DC must already](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/most-states-cure), train staff, have two reviewers from different parties check every mismatch, [as Colorado does](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/training-and-bipartisan-review), and pair the signature with a fact that needs no judgement, as [Britain does with the date of birth](#fact-secret-ballot-and-postal-voting-abroad/uk-checks-identifiers).
