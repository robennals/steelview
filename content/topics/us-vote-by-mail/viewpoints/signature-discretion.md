A mail ballot should not be thrown out because an election worker thinks a signature looks wrong. Signature matching is a judgement call, applied unevenly from one county to the next, and it falls hardest on young, first-time and minority voters.

Where states compare signatures, they should tell voters and let them fix the problem, and they should prefer checks that do not depend on someone's eye.

Even its practitioners call it subjective. [Washington's state auditor described matching as "inherently subjective", and its own reviewers disagreed about many signatures](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/reviewers-disagree). It matters because [a mismatched signature is now the most common listed reason a mail ballot is rejected](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/signatures-largest-reason).

Courts have found the process wanting. In New Hampshire, [voters were not told their ballot had been rejected, and moderators had no handwriting training or standards](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/new-hampshire-due-process). [Pennsylvania's top court held that its election law does not allow rejection on signature comparison at all](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/pennsylvania-no-signature-rejection).

The results are unequal. In Washington, [Black voters' ballots were rejected at four times white voters' rate, counting unsigned ballots as well as mismatches](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/washington-race-gaps). [Young and first-time voters were rejected most, including for mismatches alone](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/washington-age-gaps), and [Florida studies found the same pattern for young and inexperienced voters](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/florida-young-and-inexperienced).

Above all, [the county a ballot was cast in mattered more than anything else, with some counties up to 18 times likelier than another to reject for a mismatch](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/county-strongest-predictor). The same voter, with the same signature, stands a different chance depending on where they live. That falls short of [an equal voice for every citizen](#principle-an-equal-voice-for-every-citizen).

No villain is needed for this. [Washington's auditor found no evidence of bias in individual decisions](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/no-evidence-of-bias) and [agreed with counties on almost every signature it re-checked](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/auditor-agreed-with-counties). The variation comes from [how much doubt each county allows](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/low-rejection-counties-accept-more). A standard that honest people apply this differently is not a standard.

Discretion is also an opening. [Officials have manipulated counts through absentee ballots before](#fact-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count/officials-manipulated-count). [No case was found of signature review used to change a result](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/no-case-signature-review-changed-result), and [recent refusals to certify ended in certification](#fact-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count/refusals-ended-in-certification). The case for limits is a safeguard against a risk, not a record of abuse: the powerful should be bound by rules they cannot bend.

The check is also weaker as a fraud defence than it looks: [in Cobb County an envelope signed by the voter's spouse passed](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/cobb-spouse-signature-passed). Simply abolishing it is not the answer either. [An ID-number check is not rejection-free](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/id-number-checks-also-reject): [Texas's rejections rose under one](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/texas-rose-under-id-number) while [Georgia's fell](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/georgia-fell-after-sb202). Design decides.

So every state that compares signatures should do what the better ones already do. [Most states must already let voters fix signature problems](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/most-states-cure), and [counties that cured more rejected less](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/low-rejection-counties-cure-more). Add statewide training and [bipartisan review of every mismatch](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/training-and-bipartisan-review), and pair the signature with a fact that needs no judgement, as [Britain does with the date of birth](#fact-secret-ballot-and-postal-voting-abroad/uk-checks-identifiers).
