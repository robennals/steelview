---
question: How much mail-ballot fraud and household pressure goes undetected?
kind: assumption
divides: [mail-exception, mail-works, signature-discretion]
positions:
  - viewpoint: mail-exception
    holds: Enough to matter. Pressure at home is the kind of wrong victims do not report, and checks already miss ballots signed by someone else, so the few detected cases tell us little about the real number.
  - viewpoint: mail-works
    holds: Very little. Large schemes leave traces and have been caught every time, and the all-mail states, with the most mail ballots, have the fewest serious cases. An unmeasured gap is not evidence of a large one.
  - viewpoint: signature-discretion
    holds: Unknown, and signature checks are a poor way to find out. They miss a spouse's signature and reject genuine voters, so their rejections measure the reviewer's eye, not fraud.
---
Every count of mail fraud is a count of [what was caught](#fact-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count/detected-fraud-is-a-floor), and [no source measures the share that is not](#fact-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count/floor-not-evidence-of-common). Both sides argue from that silence. One reads it as hidden coercion; the other as the absence of a problem.

If the undetected share is large, the secrecy argument becomes a fraud argument, and the case for keeping mail voting as an exception gets much stronger. If it is small, lost secrecy is a cost to weigh against access, and the record of [few detected cases against tens of millions of ballots](#fact-absentee-fraud-and-manipulation-of-the-count/few-cases-against-ballots-cast) settles most of the fraud question in mail voting's favour.

The evidence that would move people does not yet exist: a probability survey asking mail voters, privately, about pressure and about others filling in their ballots, with in-person voters asked the same; and a test of how many forged signatures checks let through. [Observers who count family voting at polling stations cannot do so for postal votes](#fact-secret-ballot-and-postal-voting-abroad/no-study-of-mail-coercion), and the one audit read here [sampled only accepted ballots](#fact-mail-ballot-rejection-and-signature-checks/cobb-accepted-ballots-only).
