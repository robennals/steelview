[A government's first duty is to its own people](#principle-first-duty-to-its-own-people). This war is costing Americans money and lives, and it was never theirs to fight. The US should end it.

### Americans voted for a President who promised to stop wars, not start them

On election night in 2024, [the President said, "I'm not going to start a war. I'm going to stop wars."](#fact-decisions-law-and-diplomacy/campaign-said-stop-wars) Voters who believed him had a right to expect him to keep that promise.

While [the same campaign called Iran "very close" to a nuclear weapon](#fact-decisions-law-and-diplomacy/campaign-called-iran-very-close), warning of a danger is not a promise to go to war over it.

### The war is costing Americans billions, and the bill keeps growing

[The Pentagon's figure for the war's cost has nearly doubled since April](#fact-human-and-financial-costs-of-the-war/cost-38-to-47-billion). [Each extra month of fighting adds billions more](#fact-human-and-financial-costs-of-the-war/monthly-cost-going-forward). [The Iraq war cost many times the White House's early estimates](#fact-human-and-financial-costs-of-the-war/iraq-overran-estimates), so this war's estimates deserve little trust.

[Experts say the war has used up many of the missiles that protect US troops and allies](#fact-human-and-financial-costs-of-the-war/interceptor-stocks). A war that matters more could need them.

### Families are paying for the war at the pump

[Pump prices rose by about half once the war began, and have stayed high](#fact-oil-shipping-and-prices/pump-prices-up-by-half). [Government budget experts now expect higher inflation than they did before the war](#fact-oil-shipping-and-prices/cbo-inflation-attribution).

While [oil prices, after allowing for inflation, have stayed below the peaks of 1981, 2008 and 2022](#fact-oil-shipping-and-prices/below-earlier-real-peaks), families have paid more at the pump since the war began.

### Most Americans oppose the war

[Most Americans disapprove of the war](#fact-decisions-law-and-diplomacy/most-disapprove-gallup). [Independents oppose it by more than two to one](#fact-decisions-law-and-diplomacy/independents-opposed). [Even among Republicans, approval of the President's handling of the war has slipped](#fact-decisions-law-and-diplomacy/republicans-support-but-falling).

### A smaller war than Iraq still has to be worth its cost

[Far fewer Americans have died than in the Iraq war](#fact-human-and-financial-costs-of-the-war/us-deaths-small-share-of-iraq). But being cheaper than Iraq does not make a war worth fighting. Every dollar and every life still has to be justified.

While [half of the American deaths were not in combat](#fact-human-and-financial-costs-of-the-war/half-of-deaths-non-hostile), those people still died serving in this war.

### So the US should end the war and bring its troops home

While [groups allied with Iran attacked US and coalition forces more than 200 times before the war](#fact-iran-actions-abroad/attacks-on-us-forces-before-the-war), that is a reason to bring the troops home. It is not a reason to widen the war.

[War should be the last resort](#principle-war-only-as-a-last-resort), for when Americans are in danger and nothing else will work. The US should stop new strikes, end the war, and let the region settle its own fights.
