Americans were [told they would get no new wars](#fact-decisions-law-and-diplomacy/campaign-said-stop-wars), and they got one in the Middle East. [It is costing tens of billions of dollars](#fact-human-and-financial-costs-of-the-war/cost-38-to-47-billion), [American lives](#fact-human-and-financial-costs-of-the-war/half-of-deaths-non-hostile) and [money at the pump](#fact-oil-shipping-and-prices/pump-prices-up-by-half). The United States should wind this war down, because [a government's first duty is to its own people](#principle-first-duty-to-its-own-people).

The promise was plain. [On election night 2024 the President said, "I'm not going to start a war. I'm going to stop wars."](#fact-decisions-law-and-diplomacy/campaign-said-stop-wars) Anyone who voted on that promise was entitled to expect it to hold.

The bill keeps growing. [Official estimates of the war's cost have risen from about $25 billion to $47.1 billion](#fact-human-and-financial-costs-of-the-war/cost-38-to-47-billion), and [CBO estimates each further month at $2–3 billion](#fact-human-and-financial-costs-of-the-war/monthly-cost-going-forward). [CSIS estimates the war has drawn down the interceptors that protect US forces and allies](#fact-human-and-financial-costs-of-the-war/interceptor-stocks), which would be needed in a war that mattered more.

Wars cost more than their planners say. [In 2002 the White House put the Iraq war at $50–60 billion; Congress appropriated $815 billion](#fact-human-and-financial-costs-of-the-war/iraq-overran-estimates). There is no reason to trust this war's forecasts more.

Families pay at home too. [Petrol rose by about half after the war began, and has fallen only slightly](#fact-oil-shipping-and-prices/pump-prices-up-by-half). [CBO attributes about half a point of extra inflation to the war](#fact-oil-shipping-and-prices/cbo-inflation-attribution).

The public has not consented. [Most Americans disapprove of the war](#fact-decisions-law-and-diplomacy/majority-disapproves), and [independents oppose it by about two to one or more](#fact-decisions-law-and-diplomacy/independents-opposed). [Republican support remains a majority but is falling](#fact-decisions-law-and-diplomacy/republicans-support-but-falling).

The costs are smaller than Iraq's, and that should be said. [US deaths are a small fraction of the Iraq war's](#fact-human-and-financial-costs-of-the-war/us-deaths-small-share-of-iraq), and [half of them were not from hostile action](#fact-human-and-financial-costs-of-the-war/half-of-deaths-non-hostile).

[Adjusted for inflation, crude oil stayed below the 1981, 2008 and 2022 peaks](#fact-oil-shipping-and-prices/below-earlier-real-peaks). But a war is not justified by being cheaper than the worst one. Every dollar and every life still has to be justified to the people paying.

Nor did the war start from peace. [Iran-aligned militias attacked US forces more than 200 times between late 2023 and late 2024](#fact-iran-actions-abroad/attacks-on-us-forces-before-the-war). That is a reason to protect American troops, or to bring them home. It is not a reason to fight Iran's government until it falls.

[War should be a last resort](#principle-war-only-as-a-last-resort), used when Americans themselves are threatened and nothing else will work. The US should stop new strikes, bring the war to a close, and leave the region's quarrels to the region.
