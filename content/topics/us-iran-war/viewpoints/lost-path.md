The war has [killed thousands of Iranians](#fact-human-and-financial-costs-of-the-war/counters-disagree) and [left no one able to see Iran's nuclear material](#fact-iran-nuclear-programme/stock-unaccounted-for), and it was not necessary. Iran was dangerous but could be contained: [while the 2015 deal held, inspection kept its stock within limits](#fact-iran-nuclear-programme/deal-era-compliance). Choosing bombs over that path was a mistake, and [war only as a last resort](#principle-war-only-as-a-last-resort) is why.

The human cost came first. [By HRANA's minimum count, at least 1,701 Iranian civilians were killed by early April, at least 254 of them children](#fact-human-and-financial-costs-of-the-war/civilian-minimum-by-april). [WHO estimates 3.2 million people were displaced inside Iran](#fact-human-and-financial-costs-of-the-war/displaced-millions).

[No count covers the fighting since April except the government's](#fact-human-and-financial-costs-of-the-war/no-count-after-april), so the toll is a floor. [Priority to the worst off](#principle-priority-to-the-worst-off) means counting these people, who had no say in their government's choices.

Diplomacy had a record. [While the 2015 deal held, Iran's stock stayed under its limit](#fact-iran-nuclear-programme/deal-era-compliance), and [the limits were broken only after the US left the deal](#fact-iran-nuclear-programme/breaches-after-us-exit). The talks had not stopped: [the IAEA's chief attended US–Iran talks in Geneva two days before the strikes](#fact-iran-nuclear-programme/geneva-talks-feb-2026).

Nor was a bomb imminent in the sense that matters. [In May 2025 the IAEA found no ongoing structured weapons programme](#fact-iran-nuclear-programme/no-ongoing-programme-2025), and [US intelligence judged that year that Iran was not building a weapon](#fact-iran-nuclear-programme/ata-2025-not-building). Iran's enrichment to 60% was a real danger. A danger that inspectors could see and negotiators were discussing is a reason to keep watching and talking.

War has now made the problem harder to see. [Since 28 February inspectors have visited only the Bushehr power plant](#fact-iran-nuclear-programme/verification-stopped), and [no one has seen the 60% stock since June 2025](#fact-iran-nuclear-programme/stock-unaccounted-for). The record of force is poor.

[Strikes on nuclear sites have delayed programmes modestly](#fact-outside-attempts-to-change-hostile-regimes/strikes-delay-modestly), and [the same authors say the conditions behind past successes are absent for Iran](#fact-outside-attempts-to-change-hostile-regimes/past-success-conditions-absent-for-iran). [Imposed regime change more than doubles the risk of civil war](#fact-outside-attempts-to-change-hostile-regimes/imposed-regime-change-and-civil-war).

None of this means Iran was becoming benign. [The two lowest presidential turnouts are the last two elections](#fact-iran-government-and-its-people/turnout-record-lows), and [executions rose fastest under the reformist-backed president](#fact-iran-government-and-its-people/executions-rose-under-pezeshkian).

Still, there were openings: [voters elected the one moderate in the field in 2024](#fact-iran-government-and-its-people/reformist-backed-president-2024), and [a harsher hijab law was shelved with the Leader's assent](#fact-iran-government-and-its-people/hijab-law-shelved). Containment does not need a reformed Iran. It needs a watched one.

The war was also begun without the world's backing. [Oman's foreign minister said it was launched without United Nations authorisation](#fact-decisions-law-and-diplomacy/no-un-authorisation-per-oman), and [key European allies did not join the strikes](#fact-decisions-law-and-diplomacy/key-allies-did-not-join). The United States should stop the bombing for good and rebuild what it threw away: inspectors on the ground and a deal that gives Iran a reason to let them in.
