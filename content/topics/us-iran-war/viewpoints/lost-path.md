Iran was dangerous, but talks and inspections were keeping it in check. Choosing war instead was a mistake. [War should be the last resort](#principle-war-only-as-a-last-resort), and this one was not.

### The war has killed thousands of Iranians

[One group counted at least 1,701 civilians killed by early April, hundreds of them children](#fact-human-and-financial-costs-of-the-war/civilian-minimum-by-april). [Millions more have been forced from their homes](#fact-human-and-financial-costs-of-the-war/displaced-millions). [No one outside the government has counted the deaths since April](#fact-human-and-financial-costs-of-the-war/no-count-after-april), so the true toll is higher. These people had no say in their government's choices, and [the people who suffer most should count the most](#principle-priority-to-the-worst-off).

### Talks and inspections were keeping Iran in check

[While the 2015 nuclear deal lasted, Iran kept to its limits](#fact-iran-nuclear-programme/deal-era-compliance). [It broke them only after the US left the deal](#fact-iran-nuclear-programme/breaches-after-us-exit). Even then, the talking went on: [US–Iran talks were under way in Geneva two days before the strikes](#fact-iran-nuclear-programme/geneva-talks-feb-2026).

### Iran was not about to build a bomb

In 2025, [UN nuclear inspectors found no active weapons programme](#fact-iran-nuclear-programme/no-ongoing-programme-2025). [US intelligence judged that Iran was not building a bomb](#fact-iran-nuclear-programme/ata-2025-not-building). While Iran's 60% uranium was a real danger, inspectors could see it and negotiators were discussing it. That called for more watching and talking, not war.

### The war has stopped inspections, and bombing has a poor record

Inspections have now almost stopped. [Since the war began, inspectors have visited only one site, a power plant](#fact-iran-nuclear-programme/verification-stopped). [No one has seen Iran's 60% uranium since June 2025](#fact-iran-nuclear-programme/stock-unaccounted-for).

Bombing cannot make up for that. [Strikes on nuclear sites have only slowed programmes down a little, and worked best long before a country was close to a bomb](#fact-outside-attempts-to-change-hostile-regimes/strikes-delay-modestly). [The same researchers said Iran does not fit the pattern of past successes](#fact-outside-attempts-to-change-hostile-regimes/past-success-conditions-absent-for-iran). Trying to topple the government is riskier still: [when outsiders overthrow a government, the risk of civil war more than doubles](#fact-outside-attempts-to-change-hostile-regimes/imposed-regime-change-and-civil-war).

### Iran did not need to become friendly, only to be watched

While Iran was not becoming a freer country, keeping it in check never depended on that. [Turnout in the last two presidential elections was the lowest ever](#fact-iran-government-and-its-people/turnout-record-lows), and [executions rose fastest under the president backed by reformers](#fact-iran-government-and-its-people/executions-rose-under-pezeshkian). There were even some signs of change. [In 2024, voters chose the one moderate candidate](#fact-iran-government-and-its-people/reformist-backed-president-2024). [A harsher headscarf law was put on hold, with the Supreme Leader's approval](#fact-iran-government-and-its-people/hijab-law-shelved). But what kept Iran in check was inspectors being able to watch.

### The US went to war without the world's support, and should go back to inspections

[Oman's foreign minister said the UN never approved the war](#fact-decisions-law-and-diplomacy/no-un-authorisation-per-oman). [Key European allies did not join the strikes](#fact-decisions-law-and-diplomacy/key-allies-did-not-join). The US should stop the bombing for good. Then it should rebuild what it threw away. That means inspectors on the ground, and a deal that gives Iran a reason to let them in.
