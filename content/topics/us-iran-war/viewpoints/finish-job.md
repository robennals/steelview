The US is already at war with Iran. If it stops halfway, it leaves an angry Iran with its uranium out of sight and its missiles being rebuilt. The US should keep up the pressure until inspectors confirm nothing is left.

### Stopping now would leave Iran's most dangerous uranium out of sight

[No inspector has seen Iran's 60% uranium since June 2025](#fact-iran-nuclear-programme/stock-unaccounted-for). [Iran says it is buried under rubble](#fact-iran-nuclear-programme/iran-says-under-rubble). But [Iran has hidden nuclear weapons work before](#fact-iran-nuclear-programme/past-weapons-work), so its word is not enough.

Until inspectors find the uranium, or see that it was destroyed, the danger remains.

### A pause gives Iran time to rebuild its weapons

[Experts at two US research groups say Iran has been rebuilding its missiles and drones since the April ceasefire](#fact-decisions-law-and-diplomacy/iran-reconstituting-missiles). [The top US intelligence official reportedly warned that Iran is planning attacks on US targets](#fact-decisions-law-and-diplomacy/dni-warning-as-reported).

### Pressure has opened countries to inspectors before, when it was kept up and came with a way out

[Iraq's nuclear programme ended only after a war and years of close inspection](#fact-outside-attempts-to-change-hostile-regimes/iraq-ended-after-war-and-inspection). [Libya gave up its programme and let inspectors in](#fact-outside-attempts-to-change-hostile-regimes/libya-2003-with-inspections). [Countries give up a programme most often when threatened with sanctions, with force on the table, and offered face-saving rewards](#fact-outside-attempts-to-change-hostile-regimes/threats-plus-rewards).

[Bombing alone makes a country give in only about a third of the time](#fact-outside-attempts-to-change-hostile-regimes/air-coercion-about-a-third), so bombs cannot be the whole plan. But [bombing works more often when the target's military is open to attack](#fact-outside-attempts-to-change-hostile-regimes/military-vulnerability). The US should keep the pressure on, and offer Iran a way out that includes inspectors.

### Backing down now could make Iran and its allies bolder

[Researchers disagree on whether backing down makes enemies bolder](#fact-outside-attempts-to-change-hostile-regimes/credibility-split). With [the worst dangers](#principle-safety-from-catastrophic-threats) at stake, that risk is too serious to take. And America's friends are already under fire.

[The Houthis in Yemen fired missiles and drones at Saudi Arabia in October](#fact-decisions-law-and-diplomacy/houthi-fire-on-saudi-arabia). America should [stand by its allies](#principle-loyalty-to-allies).

### Finishing the job means limited goals, not overthrowing Iran's government

[When outsiders overthrow a government, the risk of civil war more than doubles](#fact-outside-attempts-to-change-hostile-regimes/imposed-regime-change-and-civil-war). [Demanding new leaders makes bombing less likely to work](#fact-outside-attempts-to-change-hostile-regimes/leadership-demands-lower-success). So the goals should be limited and clear.

Iran must stop enriching uranium and building missiles. Its uranium must be found, or shown to be destroyed.

### So the US should keep up the pressure until inspectors confirm nothing is left

[Both sides fired during the ceasefire](#fact-decisions-law-and-diplomacy/both-sides-fired), so a pause is not an end. The war should end with inspectors in place and nothing left to find. That can come by force, or by a deal Iran accepts under pressure.

While [researchers said in 2011 that Iran lacked the conditions of past successful strikes](#fact-outside-attempts-to-change-hostile-regimes/past-success-conditions-absent-for-iran), that calls for limited goals, not quitting. While [most Americans disapprove of the war](#fact-decisions-law-and-diplomacy/most-disapprove-gallup), clear goals would show them when it will end. Stopping short leaves the hardest part for the next president.
