The US is already at war with Iran. If it stops halfway, it leaves a weakened, angry Iran with more reason than ever to want a bomb.

### The nuclear danger is still there

[No one has seen Iran's 60% uranium since June 2025](#fact-iran-nuclear-programme/stock-unaccounted-for). [Iran says it is buried under rubble](#fact-iran-nuclear-programme/stock-unaccounted-for). But [Iran has hidden nuclear weapons work before](#fact-iran-nuclear-programme/past-weapons-work), so its word is not enough. Until inspectors find the uranium, the danger remains.

### Iran is using the pause to rearm

[Experts at two US research groups say Iran has been rebuilding its missiles and drones since the April ceasefire](#fact-decisions-law-and-diplomacy/iran-reconstituting-missiles). [The top US intelligence official reportedly warned that Iran is planning attacks on US targets](#fact-decisions-law-and-diplomacy/dni-warning-as-reported).

### Nuclear programmes end after war and inspections, not a few strikes

[Iraq's nuclear programme ended only after a war and years of close inspection](#fact-outside-attempts-to-change-hostile-regimes/iraq-ended-after-war-and-inspection). Bombing alone is less reliable. [It makes a country give in only about a third of the time](#fact-outside-attempts-to-change-hostile-regimes/air-coercion-about-a-third). [It works best against military targets](#fact-outside-attempts-to-change-hostile-regimes/air-coercion-about-a-third). So the US should keep up the pressure on Iran's military until inspectors are in.

### Stopping early would make America look weak and put its friends at risk

While [research is split on whether backing down makes enemies bolder](#fact-outside-attempts-to-change-hostile-regimes/credibility-split), the risk is too serious to ignore with [the worst dangers](#principle-safety-from-catastrophic-threats) at stake. Backing down would also leave America's friends exposed. [Gulf countries that work with the US came under fire again in October](#fact-decisions-law-and-diplomacy/october-escalation), and America should [stand by its allies](#principle-loyalty-to-allies).

### Finishing the job does not mean overthrowing Iran's government

[When outsiders overthrow a government, the risk of civil war more than doubles](#fact-outside-attempts-to-change-hostile-regimes/imposed-regime-change-and-civil-war). [Demanding new leaders makes bombing less likely to work](#fact-outside-attempts-to-change-hostile-regimes/leadership-demands-lower-success). So the goals should be limited and clear. Iran must stop enriching uranium and building missiles. Its uranium must be found, or shown to be destroyed.

### The war's costs are a reason to set an end point, not to quit early

While [researchers said strikes are less likely to work on Iran than in past cases](#fact-outside-attempts-to-change-hostile-regimes/past-success-conditions-absent-for-iran), that calls for limited goals, not for quitting. And while [most Americans disapprove of the war](#fact-decisions-law-and-diplomacy/majority-disapproves), clear goals would show them when it will end. [Both sides kept firing during the ceasefire](#fact-decisions-law-and-diplomacy/both-sides-fired), which shows that a pause is not the same as an end.

The war should end with inspectors on the ground and nothing left to find. That could come by force, or by a deal Iran accepts under pressure. Stopping short leaves the hardest part for the next president.
