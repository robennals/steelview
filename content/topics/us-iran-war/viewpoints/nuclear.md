An Iran with nuclear weapons would be far more dangerous than Iran today. Stopping it was worth a war, because [keeping people safe from the worst dangers](#principle-safety-from-catastrophic-threats) comes first.

### Iran was close to having enough uranium for a bomb

[Iran built up its uranium enriched to 60% for four years, and fastest just before the June 2025 strikes](#fact-iran-nuclear-programme/sixty-percent-growth). [The hardest step in building a nuclear bomb is enriching uranium, which means making it purer](#needed). [Bombs need about 90%, and little extra work gets there from 60%](#fact-iran-nuclear-programme/sixty-is-not-ninety). [Nuclear power plants need uranium enriched to only a few percent](#needed). [In May 2025, US intelligence judged Iran probably under a week from enough bomb-grade uranium for one bomb](#fact-iran-nuclear-programme/close-to-weapons-grade-before-strikes). [Iran is the only country without nuclear weapons making 60% uranium](#fact-iran-nuclear-programme/only-non-weapon-state-at-60).

### Inspectors could not vouch that Iran's programme was peaceful

[In 2025, UN inspectors said they could not assure the world that Iran's programme was peaceful](#fact-iran-nuclear-programme/no-assurance-peaceful). [Iran once ran a secret programme to develop weapons technology, and hid the evidence](#fact-iran-nuclear-programme/past-weapons-work), and [in 2021 it stopped the inspectors' extra visits](#fact-iran-nuclear-programme/complementary-access-ended). [The inspectors' board later found it in breach of its obligations](#fact-iran-nuclear-programme/board-non-compliance). While [US intelligence judged in 2025 that Iran was not building a bomb](#fact-iran-nuclear-programme/ata-2025-not-building), [it warned that pressure on Iran's leader to build one was growing](#fact-iran-nuclear-programme/ata-2025-not-building). With uranium so close to bomb grade, one decision could start the last short step.

### Iran already attacks its enemies, and a bomb would let it do so more freely

[A court in Argentina held Iran-backed Hezbollah responsible for two bombings in Buenos Aires](#fact-iran-actions-abroad/argentine-court-buenos-aires-bombings). [Men hired by Iran's Revolutionary Guards plotted to kill a journalist in America, and US courts have sentenced them](#fact-iran-actions-abroad/us-convictions-iran-directed-plots). [Groups allied with Iran attacked US and coalition forces more than 200 times in 2023 and 2024](#fact-iran-actions-abroad/attacks-on-us-forces-before-the-war). Today Iran must fear being hit back. With a bomb, other countries would fear using force against it, so it could attack more freely.

### An Iranian bomb could lead others in the region to build their own

[Saudi Arabia's crown prince has said his country would build a bomb if Iran did](#fact-outside-attempts-to-change-hostile-regimes/saudi-2018-pledge). A Middle East with several nuclear powers would be more dangerous still. While [past fears that bombs would spread quickly mostly proved wrong](#fact-outside-attempts-to-change-hostile-regimes/predicted-cascades-did-not-happen), the risk is too grave to bet that they will prove wrong again.

### So the US had to strike while Iran's uranium could still be reached

[Sanctions have rarely stopped a nuclear programme once it has started](#fact-outside-attempts-to-change-hostile-regimes/nonproliferation-sanctions-three-of-22). So the choice was never between war and a peaceful Iran. It was between striking now and leaving Iran one short step from a bomb.

Waiting would have meant trusting a government that has hidden weapons work before. A war is a terrible cost. A nuclear-armed Iran would be a worse one.
