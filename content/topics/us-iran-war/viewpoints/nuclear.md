An Iran with nuclear weapons would be far more dangerous than Iran today. Stopping it was worth a war, because [keeping people safe from the worst dangers](#principle-safety-from-catastrophic-threats) comes first.

### Iran was close to having a bomb

Before the strikes, [US intelligence judged Iran probably under a week from bomb-grade uranium for one bomb](#fact-iran-nuclear-programme/close-to-weapons-grade-before-strikes).

[The hardest step in building a nuclear bomb is enriching uranium, which means making it purer](#needed). [Nuclear power plants need uranium enriched to only a few percent](#needed), while [bombs need about 90%](#fact-iran-nuclear-programme/sixty-is-not-ninety). [Iran is the only country without nuclear weapons making 60% uranium](#fact-iran-nuclear-programme/only-non-weapon-state-at-60), and [little extra work takes that to bomb grade](#fact-iran-nuclear-programme/sixty-is-not-ninety). [Its stock more than doubled in the seven months before them](#fact-iran-nuclear-programme/sixty-percent-growth).

### Iran could not be trusted to stay short of a bomb

While [US intelligence judged in 2025 that Iran was not building a bomb](#fact-iran-nuclear-programme/ata-2025-not-building), [it warned that pressure to build one was growing](#fact-iran-nuclear-programme/ata-2025-not-building). [UN inspectors found no active weapons programme](#fact-iran-nuclear-programme/no-ongoing-programme-2025), but [could not be sure Iran's programme was peaceful](#fact-iran-nuclear-programme/past-weapons-work): [Iran once ran a secret programme to develop weapons technology and hid the evidence](#fact-iran-nuclear-programme/past-weapons-work).

[In 2021 Iran stopped short-notice inspections](#fact-iran-nuclear-programme/access-narrowed-in-steps), and [the inspectors' board later found it breaking its promises](#fact-iran-nuclear-programme/board-referral-with-abstentions). With uranium so close to bomb grade, it needed only a decision.

### A bomb would make Iran bolder and more dangerous

Iran already uses violence against its enemies. [Argentina's top court held Iran-backed Hezbollah responsible for two Buenos Aires bombings](#fact-iran-actions-abroad/argentine-court-buenos-aires-bombings). [US courts have convicted men hired by its Revolutionary Guards to kill a critic in America](#fact-iran-actions-abroad/us-convictions-iran-directed-plots). [Iran-allied groups attacked US troops more than 200 times in 2023–24](#fact-iran-actions-abroad/attacks-on-us-forces-before-the-war).

For now, Iran must fear retaliation. A bomb would change that: others would fear using force against it, so it could attack more freely. One day it might use the bomb itself.

### Nothing short of war was going to stop it

[Sanctions have rarely stopped a nuclear programme once it has started](#fact-outside-attempts-to-change-hostile-regimes/nonproliferation-sanctions-three-of-22). An Iranian bomb could also lead others to build their own. While [past fears of bombs spreading quickly mostly proved wrong](#fact-outside-attempts-to-change-hostile-regimes/predicted-cascades-did-not-happen), this time [Saudi Arabia's crown prince has said his country would build a bomb if Iran did](#fact-outside-attempts-to-change-hostile-regimes/saudi-2018-pledge).

So the choice was never between war and a peaceful Iran. It was between striking while Iran's uranium could be reached, and later facing a nuclear Iran no one could stop.
