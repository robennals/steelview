An Iran with nuclear weapons would be far more dangerous than Iran today. Stopping it was worth a war, because [keeping people safe from the worst dangers](#principle-safety-from-catastrophic-threats) comes first.

### Iran was close to having a bomb

Before the June 2025 strikes, [US intelligence judged that Iran was probably less than a week from enough bomb-grade uranium for one bomb](#fact-iran-nuclear-programme/close-to-weapons-grade-before-strikes). That is because Iran had already done most of the hard work.

[The hardest step in building a nuclear bomb is enriching uranium, which means making it purer](#needed). [Nuclear power plants need uranium enriched to only a few percent](#needed), while [bombs need about 90%](#fact-iran-nuclear-programme/sixty-is-not-ninety). [Iran is the only country without nuclear weapons that makes uranium enriched to 60%](#fact-iran-nuclear-programme/only-non-weapon-state-at-60). [From there, little extra work is needed to reach bomb grade](#fact-iran-nuclear-programme/sixty-is-not-ninety). [In the seven months before the strikes, Iran's stock of 60% uranium more than doubled](#fact-iran-nuclear-programme/sixty-percent-growth). [Before the strikes, nuclear experts judged that Iran could build a simple first bomb within months](#fact-iran-nuclear-programme/breakout).

### Iran could not be trusted to stay short of a bomb

While [US intelligence judged in 2025 that Iran was not building a bomb](#fact-iran-nuclear-programme/ata-2025-not-building), [it warned that pressure to build one was probably growing](#fact-iran-nuclear-programme/ata-2025-not-building). [UN inspectors found no active weapons programme that May](#fact-iran-nuclear-programme/no-ongoing-programme-2025), but [they said they could not be sure Iran's programme was peaceful](#fact-iran-nuclear-programme/past-weapons-work). That is because [Iran once ran a secret programme to develop nuclear weapons technology, and later hid the evidence](#fact-iran-nuclear-programme/past-weapons-work).

[In 2021, Iran stopped inspectors from making short-notice visits](#fact-iran-nuclear-programme/access-narrowed-in-steps). Later, [the inspectors' board found Iran was breaking its nuclear promises, and sent its case to the UN Security Council](#fact-iran-nuclear-programme/board-referral-with-abstentions). Now [no inspector has seen its 60% uranium since June 2025](#fact-iran-nuclear-programme/stock-unaccounted-for). With its uranium so close to bomb grade, Iran did not need a weapons programme already running. It only needed to decide to start one.

### A bomb would make Iran bolder and more dangerous

Iran already uses violence against people it sees as enemies. [Argentina's top court found Hezbollah, a group Iran backs, responsible for two bombings in Buenos Aires](#fact-iran-actions-abroad/argentine-court-buenos-aires-bombings). [US courts have convicted men hired by Iran's Revolutionary Guards to kill a critic in America](#fact-iran-actions-abroad/us-convictions-iran-directed-plots). [Groups allied with Iran attacked US troops more than 200 times in 2023 and 2024](#fact-iran-actions-abroad/attacks-on-us-forces-before-the-war).

For now, Iran has to worry that other countries will strike back. A nuclear weapon would change that. Other countries would be afraid to use force against it, so it could attack more freely. And one day it might use the bomb itself.

### Nothing short of war was going to stop it

[Sanctions, which limit a country's trade and money, have rarely stopped a nuclear programme once it has started](#fact-outside-attempts-to-change-hostile-regimes/nonproliferation-sanctions-three-of-22). An Iranian bomb could also lead other countries to build their own. While [past fears of bombs spreading quickly mostly did not come true](#fact-outside-attempts-to-change-hostile-regimes/predicted-cascades-did-not-happen), this time a neighbour has said it would follow. [Saudi Arabia's crown prince has said his country would build a bomb if Iran did](#fact-outside-attempts-to-change-hostile-regimes/saudi-2018-pledge).

So the choice was never between war and a peaceful Iran. It was between striking while Iran's uranium could still be reached, and later facing a nuclear Iran that no one could stop.
