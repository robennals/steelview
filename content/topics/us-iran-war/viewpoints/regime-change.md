Iran's government kills its own people by the thousands. It has no right to be left alone, and the US should help Iranians bring it down.

### The government kills people who protest

In January, [security forces killed thousands of people, most of them protesters](#fact-iran-government-and-its-people/january-2026-crackdown-toll). [They arrested more than 50,000](#fact-iran-government-and-its-people/arrests-january-2026). [A UN team found crimes against humanity](#fact-iran-government-and-its-people/january-2026-crackdown-toll). This was not new: [each of the last three waves of protest was crushed with deadly force](#fact-iran-government-and-its-people/crackdowns-2019-2022-2026).

### The killing goes on between protests, no matter who wins elections

[In 2025, Iran put more people to death than in any year since 1989](#fact-iran-government-and-its-people/executions-most-since-1989). [That was most of the world's known executions](#fact-iran-government-and-its-people/iran-top-recorded-executioner). Elections have not changed this: [executions rose under every recent president, and fastest under the one backed by reformers](#fact-iran-government-and-its-people/execution-rate-by-president).

The government also goes after its critics abroad. [Most recent Iranian plots in Europe targeted opponents of the government](#fact-iran-actions-abroad/plots-mostly-target-opponents). [US courts have convicted men hired to kill one of its critics in America](#fact-iran-actions-abroad/us-convictions-iran-directed-plots).

### Iranians are turning away from their government

[Turnout in the last two presidential elections was the lowest ever](#fact-iran-government-and-its-people/turnout-record-lows). [In one online survey, few Iranians said they want the current system of government](#fact-iran-government-and-its-people/few-prefer-islamic-republic). [Most said they were glad to see it weakened during the war](#fact-iran-government-and-its-people/satisfaction-at-weakening). [The new Supreme Leader is the son of the old one](#fact-iran-government-and-its-people/hereditary-succession), chosen behind closed doors.

### America cannot choose Iran's next government

While [overthrowing a government from outside more than doubles the risk of civil war](#fact-outside-attempts-to-change-hostile-regimes/imposed-regime-change-and-civil-war), that argues against imposing a government, not for standing aside. [Democracy has followed only where conditions helped it](#fact-outside-attempts-to-change-hostile-regimes/democracy-only-in-favourable-conditions), so Iranians must choose their leaders.

While war has been followed by more repression, that argues for helping Iranians directly, not only striking. [Arrests rose sharply after the June 2025 war](#fact-iran-government-and-its-people/arrests-after-june-2025), [political executions peaked during and after this one](#fact-iran-government-and-its-people/political-executions-peaked-april-may), and [many Iranians surveyed called the strikes an attack](#fact-iran-government-and-its-people/strikes-seen-as-aggression).

### America should help Iranians do it themselves

The US can weaken the government's security forces and stop it rebuilding them, protect its critics abroad, and help Iranians get online and organise beyond its reach.

[We have a duty to help people in danger](#principle-obligation-to-people-in-danger), even when helping is hard. Iranians have risked their lives for a better government, [in three waves of protest met with deadly force](#fact-iran-government-and-its-people/crackdowns-2019-2022-2026). [They want a government chosen by its people](#principle-democratic-consent-over-membership), and America should stand with them until they get it.
