The Islamic Republic [kills its own people in thousands when they protest](#fact-iran-government-and-its-people/crackdowns-2019-2022-2026) and [executes them in record numbers in between](#fact-iran-government-and-its-people/executions-most-since-1989). A government that does this forfeits any claim to be left alone. [Few Iranians in GAMAAN's online survey want it](#fact-iran-government-and-its-people/few-prefer-islamic-republic), and the United States should help them bring it down.

Look at January. [HRANA confirmed 7,007 deaths in the 50 days of the January 2026 crackdown, most of them protesters, with thousands more cases under review; the UN mission found crimes against humanity](#fact-iran-government-and-its-people/january-2026-crackdown-toll). [It brought more than 50,000 arrests](#fact-iran-government-and-its-people/arrests-january-2026). [Each of the last three protest waves was put down with lethal force](#fact-iran-government-and-its-people/crackdowns-2019-2022-2026).

Killing goes on between the protests. [Executions in 2025 were the most since 1989](#fact-iran-government-and-its-people/executions-most-since-1989), and [Iran carried out most of the world's recorded executions that year](#fact-iran-government-and-its-people/iran-top-recorded-executioner). [The execution rate rose under every recent president, and more than doubled under the reformist-backed one](#fact-iran-government-and-its-people/execution-rate-by-president). The regime's cruelty does not depend on who wins its elections.

Its reach goes abroad. [Most recent Iranian plots in Europe targeted dissidents](#fact-iran-actions-abroad/plots-mostly-target-opponents), and [US courts have sentenced men hired to kill a critic of the regime on American soil](#fact-iran-actions-abroad/us-convictions-iran-directed-plots).

Iranians are withdrawing their consent wherever they can. [The two lowest presidential turnouts are the last two elections](#fact-iran-government-and-its-people/turnout-record-lows). [In GAMAAN's online sample about 13% prefer the Islamic Republic](#fact-iran-government-and-its-people/few-prefer-islamic-republic), and [about 60% felt satisfaction at its weakening during the war](#fact-iran-government-and-its-people/satisfaction-at-weakening). [The new Leader is the old Leader's son](#fact-iran-government-and-its-people/hereditary-succession), chosen with no published vote.

The hard truths should shape the help, not cancel it. [Imposed regime change more than doubles the risk of civil war](#fact-outside-attempts-to-change-hostile-regimes/imposed-regime-change-and-civil-war), and [democracy has followed only where conditions already favoured it](#fact-outside-attempts-to-change-hostile-regimes/democracy-only-in-favourable-conditions). [Repression widened after the June 2025 war](#fact-iran-government-and-its-people/arrests-after-june-2025), and [political executions peaked during and just after this one](#fact-iran-government-and-its-people/political-executions-peaked-april-may). [In GAMAAN's sample 42% called the strikes aggression against Iran](#fact-iran-government-and-its-people/strikes-seen-as-aggression).

So the United States should not try to install a government. Outside force has rarely built a democracy, and outsiders cannot choose Iran's next leaders. What the US can do is weaken the instruments of repression, keep the regime from rebuilding them, protect dissidents abroad, and give Iranians the tools to organise and be heard.

The [obligation to people in danger](#principle-obligation-to-people-in-danger) does not lapse because helping is hard. Iranians have shown, [in three waves of protest met with lethal force](#fact-iran-government-and-its-people/crackdowns-2019-2022-2026), that they will risk their lives for a different government. [Democratic consent](#principle-democratic-consent-over-membership) is what they are asking for. America should be on their side until they get it.
