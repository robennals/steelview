Iran's government kills its own people by the thousands. It has no right to be left alone, and the US should help Iranians bring it down.

### The government kills people who protest

[In the crackdown of winter 2025–26, thousands of people were killed, most of them protesters](#fact-iran-government-and-its-people/january-2026-crackdown-toll). [A UN team found crimes against humanity](#fact-iran-government-and-its-people/un-crimes-against-humanity). [Each of the last three waves of protest was crushed with deadly force](#fact-iran-government-and-its-people/crackdowns-2019-2022-2026), and [in the latest the authorities arrested more than 50,000 people](#fact-iran-government-and-its-people/arrests-january-2026).

### The killing goes on between protests, whoever wins elections

[In 2025, Iran put more people to death than in any year since 1989](#fact-iran-government-and-its-people/executions-most-since-1989). [Executions rose under every recent president, and fastest under the moderate one](#fact-iran-government-and-its-people/execution-rate-by-president), so voting in a new president has not stopped the killing. [Iran carried out most of the world's known executions in 2025](#fact-iran-government-and-its-people/iran-top-recorded-executioner). It also goes after critics abroad: [most recent Iranian plots in Europe targeted opponents of the government](#fact-iran-actions-abroad/plots-mostly-target-opponents).

### Iranians are turning away from their government

[Turnout in the last two presidential elections was the lowest since the revolution](#fact-iran-government-and-its-people/turnout-record-lows). [In one online survey, few Iranians said they want the current system of government](#fact-iran-government-and-its-people/few-prefer-islamic-republic), and [most said they were glad to see it weakened during the war](#fact-iran-government-and-its-people/satisfaction-at-weakening). Nor do Iranians choose their top leader: [the new Supreme Leader is the son of the old one](#fact-iran-government-and-its-people/hereditary-succession).

### Overthrow from outside risks civil war, so Iranians must do it themselves

While [overthrowing a government from outside more than doubles the risk of civil war](#fact-outside-attempts-to-change-hostile-regimes/imposed-regime-change-and-civil-war), that argues against imposing a government, not for standing aside. [Democracy has followed only where conditions helped it](#fact-outside-attempts-to-change-hostile-regimes/democracy-only-in-favourable-conditions), so Iranians must choose their own leaders.

[More than 20,000 people were arrested after the June 2025 war](#fact-iran-government-and-its-people/arrests-after-june-2025). [Political executions peaked around this war's ceasefire](#fact-iran-government-and-its-people/political-executions-peaked-april-may). [In one survey, about as many Iranians called the strikes an attack on Iran as called them humanitarian help](#fact-iran-government-and-its-people/strikes-seen-as-aggression). While war has brought more repression and split opinion, that argues for helping Iranians directly, not only striking.

### So America should help Iranians end this government themselves

The US can weaken the government's security forces and stop it rebuilding them. It can protect the government's critics abroad. It can help Iranians get online and organise beyond the government's reach.

We have a duty to help people in danger, even when helping is hard. Iranians have risked their lives for a better government. They want a government chosen by its people, and America should stand with them until they get it.
