[The research on credibility is split: one study finds a state's past actions predict later challenges, another finds they do not](#fact-outside-attempts-to-change-hostile-regimes/credibility-split).

If backing down does invite challenges, the cost of stopping includes future wars, and finishing the job looks prudent. If it does not, the main reason to keep fighting after the aims stop being reachable falls away, and a deal or withdrawal costs less than it seems. How Iran and its partners behave after the war ends, whichever way it ends, will be a test case.
