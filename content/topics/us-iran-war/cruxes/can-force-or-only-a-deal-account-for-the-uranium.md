[No one has seen the 60% stock since June 2025](#fact-iran-nuclear-programme/stock-unaccounted-for), and [since the war began inspectors have visited only the Bushehr power plant](#fact-iran-nuclear-programme/verification-stopped). [Iraq's programme ended after a war and years of intensive inspection](#fact-outside-attempts-to-change-hostile-regimes/iraq-ended-after-war-and-inspection), and [Libya gave up its programme in 2003 and accepted inspections without one](#fact-outside-attempts-to-change-hostile-regimes/libya-2003-with-inspections).

If pressure alone can bring inspectors back, the case for finishing the job gains force. If more force drives Iran to hide the material or build in secret, a deal now is the only route to finding it. Whether Iran accepts the Qatari plan, and on what terms of inspection, will test which prediction is right.
