The record so far cuts both ways. [Repression widened after the June 2025 war](#fact-iran-government-and-its-people/arrests-after-june-2025), and [political executions peaked during and just after this one](#fact-iran-government-and-its-people/political-executions-peaked-april-may). But [total executions fell sharply in the war's first months](#fact-iran-government-and-its-people/executions-fell-in-first-war-months), and [about 60% of GAMAAN's respondents felt satisfaction at the regime's weakening](#fact-iran-government-and-its-people/satisfaction-at-weakening).

If attacks weaken the regime, outside pressure is a way to help Iranians, and the regime-change case holds. If they rally it, the war has set back the people it was meant to help, and that case falls. No systematic study of how air wars affect a target's cohesion was found, and there are no data on defections since February. A year of protest, defection and repression data after the war ends would show which way it went.
