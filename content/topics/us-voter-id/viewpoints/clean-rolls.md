---
name: Clean the rolls, rather than burdening voters at the door
summary: "Most ineligible registrations are [records of people who moved or died](#fact-voter-roll-maintenance/mostly-moves-deaths-nonresponse) or [paperwork errors made at registration](#fact-non-citizens-on-voter-rolls/how-noncitizens-reach-rolls), which a photo-ID check at the polls does not catch. States should find them in the records before Election Day, with matching that is audited and voters given time to answer."
order: 3
---
The weak point in American elections is the list, not the queue at the polling place. [Voter lists will always contain some records of people no longer eligible, the Election Assistance Commission says, because removal can take two federal election cycles](#fact-voter-roll-maintenance/lists-contain-ineligible). [The only direct national measure, from 2011 data, found about one record in eight inaccurate or no longer valid](#fact-voter-roll-maintenance/pew-only-direct-measure).

Stale records are not votes, and [many "inaccurate" records belong to eligible voters with out-of-date details](#fact-voter-roll-maintenance/pew-not-fraud). But they are what fraud has used. [Chicago's 1982 fraud cast ballots in the names of registrants who had died or moved but were left on the list](#fact-election-fraud-and-voter-exclusion-history/stale-rolls-enabled-fraud).

A photo-ID rule would not have stopped it, because [the election judges casting those ballots were the people who would have checked the ID](#fact-election-fraud-and-voter-exclusion-history/checkers-complicit). Taking a dead voter's name off the list removes the opportunity, whoever sits at the table.

The same holds for non-citizens. [They reach the rolls mostly through clerical errors, miscoded licence records and misunderstanding at registration](#fact-non-citizens-on-voter-rolls/how-noncitizens-reach-rolls). The records that reveal them are government files, such as [a licence issued to a green-card holder](#fact-non-citizens-on-voter-rolls/detected-is-a-floor), not anything a poll worker sees.

Those records can now be checked at scale. [Running a whole statewide roll through a citizenship check is now routine](#fact-non-citizens-on-voter-rolls/full-roll-check-feasible), and [Georgia held applicants it could not verify before they ever reached the rolls](#fact-non-citizens-on-voter-rolls/georgia-2022-held-at-registration). [ERIC, the states' data-sharing compact, has sent its members millions of leads on voters who moved away](#fact-voter-roll-maintenance/eric-cross-state-movers).

This only works if the matching is honest about its own errors. [Every bulk-matching program on record flagged eligible voters](#fact-voter-roll-maintenance/bulk-matching-errs). [The revamped federal SAVE database has no published error rate](#fact-non-citizens-on-voter-rolls/save-national-unaudited), and Texas sent counties its SAVE list [without first checking its own licence records, which would have cleared some citizens](#fact-non-citizens-on-voter-rolls/state-records-would-have-cleared).

The largest removal route, [an unanswered notice followed by two missed federal elections, rests on silence rather than on a record of a move](#fact-voter-roll-maintenance/nonresponse-largest). [In Ohio's 2012 mailing, more of the people who replied said they had not moved than said they had](#fact-voter-roll-maintenance/ohio-notice-replies).

So the cleaning has to be careful. Match against the state's own records before writing to anyone. Finish systematic programs early, because [federal law bars them in the 90 days before a federal election, and courts have stopped late ones](#fact-voter-roll-maintenance/nvra-90-day-rule). Publish each program's error rate, and give flagged voters time and a simple way to answer.

Scale matters too. [Nine Republican-led states left ERIC in 2022–23](#fact-voter-roll-maintenance/eric-departures), and [its cross-state mover reports fell as members left](#fact-voter-roll-maintenance/eric-reports-fell), because a match needs both states inside the compact. A state that wants clean rolls should rejoin it or build something that matches as widely.

[Verifiably honest elections](#principle-verifiably-honest-elections) depend on knowing who is on the list, and [rules must mean what they say](#principle-rules-must-mean-what-they-say) for the officials who keep it. That is where effort should go: removing the dead and the departed, on evidence, in the open, without asking an eligible citizen to prove twice what the state already knows.
