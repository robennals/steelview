The weak point in American elections is the voter list, not the line at the polls. Cleaning the list stops more fraud than checking ID at the door, and burdens fewer voters.

### Voter lists hold many out-of-date names, and fraud has used them

[A national estimate, from data to March 2011, found about one record in eight wrong or out of date](#fact-voter-roll-maintenance/pew-only-direct-measure). [Chicago's 1982 fraud cast ballots in the names of people who had died or moved](#fact-election-fraud-and-voter-exclusion-history/stale-rolls-enabled-fraud). While [a stale record is not a vote](#fact-voter-roll-maintenance/pew-not-fraud), and [some belong to eligible voters with wrong details](#fact-voter-roll-maintenance/pew-not-fraud), a dead voter's name can still be used by someone else.

### Taking a dead voter off the list stops fraud that an ID check can miss

[Where people voted in others' names at the polls, the officials checking were often part of it](#fact-election-fraud-and-voter-exclusion-history/checkers-complicit); in Chicago, [election judges took part in the fraud](#fact-election-fraud-and-voter-exclusion-history/chicago-judges-took-part). Those officials are the people who would check ID. A name taken off the list cannot be used, whoever is checking.

### Non-citizens show up in government records, not at the polls

[Reviews that give a cause trace non-citizens on the lists to clerks' errors, wrong licence records and confusion about the rules](#fact-non-citizens-on-voter-rolls/how-noncitizens-reach-rolls). The records that reveal them are government files, such as [transportation records and sworn jury excusals](#fact-non-citizens-on-voter-rolls/checks-rest-on-records). A poll worker never sees these.

[The government says the federal SAVE database has now checked more than 65 million voters in 26 states](#fact-federal-and-state-power-over-voting-rules/save-whole-rolls). [Georgia held back applicants it could not verify](#fact-non-citizens-on-voter-rolls/georgia-2022-held-at-registration), before they reached the list.

### Matching must be careful, because it also flags eligible voters

[Matching programs from Florida in 2000 to Alabama in 2024 and Texas in 2025–26 have flagged eligible voters](#fact-voter-roll-maintenance/bulk-matching-errs). [Eleven voters flagged in Travis County, Texas, had already given the state licence agency proof of citizenship](#fact-non-citizens-on-voter-rolls/state-records-would-have-cleared). The state's own records would have cleared them.

[Challengers say the government has not released an error rate for SAVE](#fact-federal-and-state-power-over-voting-rules/save-database-revived). [In 2024, the biggest reason for removal was an unanswered notice and two missed federal elections](#fact-voter-roll-maintenance/nonresponse-largest). Silence is not proof of a move. [In Ohio, more of those who replied said they had not moved than said they had](#fact-voter-roll-maintenance/ohio-notice-replies).

### Finding voters who moved needs states to share data, and nine have stopped

ERIC is a group of states that share voter records. [By October 2023, nine Republican-led states had left it](#fact-voter-roll-maintenance/eric-departures). A match needs both states as members, so each state that leaves takes its voters out of the match. [ERIC's leads on voters who moved away peaked at about 3.5 million in 2021, and since 2024 have been about 0.7 million a year or fewer](#fact-voter-roll-maintenance/eric-reports-fell), though [ERIC does not say how much of that fall comes from states leaving](#fact-voter-roll-maintenance/eric-reports-fell).

### So states should clean their lists early, carefully, and together

States should check their own records before writing to anyone, and publish each program's error rate. They should give flagged voters time and a simple way to answer. A state that wants clean lists should rejoin ERIC, or build an equally wide match.

They should finish large clean-ups early. [Federal law bars them in the 90 days before a federal election](#fact-voter-roll-maintenance/ninety-day-limit), and [courts have stopped late ones](#fact-voter-roll-maintenance/nvra-90-day-rule). Honest elections that can be checked depend on knowing who is on the list. And rules should mean what they say for those who keep it. Eligible citizens should not have to re-prove what the state already knows.
