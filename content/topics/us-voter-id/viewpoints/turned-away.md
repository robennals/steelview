Every citizen should have an equal voice. A rule that makes voting harder for some citizens must prevent enough harm to be worth it. Photo-ID and [proof-of-citizenship](#glossary-documentary-proof-of-citizenship) rules aim at fraud that is rarely found, and their cost falls on people who can least afford it.

### ID checks at the polls aim at a kind of fraud that is almost never found

An ID check at the polls stops people voting in someone else's name. [In one count of alleged fraud since 2000, that was 10 of 2,068 cases](#fact-election-fraud-and-voter-exclusion-history/impersonation-rare-modern), and [another found about 31 incidents among more than 1 billion ballots](#fact-election-fraud-and-voter-exclusion-history/two-counts-rare). Found cases are only the ones caught, but nothing shows a large hidden share, and [a national study found strict ID had no effect on fraud, actual or perceived](#fact-opinion-and-confidence-in-elections/studies-find-no-effect). When the Supreme Court upheld Indiana's ID law, [the record held no evidence of such fraud in Indiana's whole history](#fact-federal-and-state-power-over-voting-rules/crawford-record). And [in 14 of 20 recorded cases, an ID check would not have reached the fraud](#fact-election-fraud-and-voter-exclusion-history/id-would-not-reach).

### Non-citizens' votes are too rare to justify demanding papers from every citizen

[The votes by non-citizens that reviews have found are between about 1 and 22 per million ballots](#fact-non-citizens-on-voter-rolls/votes-per-ballots), [far fewer than the margins in recent close races](#fact-non-citizens-on-voter-rolls/below-margins). Each review charted found some non-citizens who voted, but the numbers are tiny. [The reviews that give a cause trace non-citizens on the lists to mistakes](#fact-non-citizens-on-voter-rolls/how-noncitizens-reach-rolls). Officials can fix those without asking every citizen for a birth certificate.

### In Kansas, demanding citizenship papers blocked far more citizens than non-citizens

[Kansas blocked 31,089 applicants who did not show citizenship documents in time](#fact-burdens-of-id-and-citizenship-rules/kansas-blocked-vs-noncitizens). From 1999 to 2013, only 39 non-citizens were confirmed on its rolls. [Even the state's own expert's figures suggested nearly all those on its suspense list were citizens](#fact-burdens-of-id-and-citizenship-rules/kansas-expert-citizens). While [the court did not know how many actually lacked a document](#fact-burdens-of-id-and-citizenship-rules/kansas-dpoc-possession-unknown), every one was kept off the list, and many citizens would struggle: [about one adult citizen in ten cannot quickly produce proof of citizenship](#fact-burdens-of-id-and-citizenship-rules/dpoc-not-readily-available).

### Texas's ID-number rule for mail ballots cost thousands of voters their vote

[In the first primary under its new rule, Texas rejected 12.38% of mail ballots](#fact-burdens-of-id-and-citizenship-rules/texas-march-2022-spike), mostly for a missing or mismatched ID number, and [about 90% of the voters rejected did not vote another way](#fact-burdens-of-id-and-citizenship-rules/rejected-texans-did-not-vote). While [the rate fell sharply after that first primary](#fact-burdens-of-id-and-citizenship-rules/texas-rate-fell-after-primary), [Texas has stayed well above its 2020 rate and the national rate](#fact-burdens-of-id-and-citizenship-rules/texas-still-above-baseline). These were voters already on the list. How an ID rule is written decides who loses a vote.

### Records checks flag many citizens, so a flag must start a check, not end a registration

[Most people Alabama made inactive as non-citizens in 2024 were citizens](#fact-non-citizens-on-voter-rolls/alabama-2024), and in every program with a published follow-up, a large share of those flagged turned out to be citizens. [In Texas and Alabama, more citizens were wrongly flagged than people were referred or removed](#fact-non-citizens-on-voter-rolls/both-errors-side-by-side). A flag is a question, not an answer. Officials have shut voters out before: [Louisiana cut Black voters from about 44% of registered voters to under 1%](#fact-election-fraud-and-voter-exclusion-history/louisiana-registration-collapse). A free, uniform check is not a literacy test, but a flag acted on without notice can still keep citizens out.

### The burden falls hardest on young, poor, Black and Hispanic citizens

[The young and poor are most likely to lack an up-to-date licence](#fact-burdens-of-id-and-citizenship-rules/licence-by-age-income), and [Black and Hispanic citizens are at least three times as likely as white citizens to have no licence](#fact-burdens-of-id-and-citizenship-rules/no-licence-by-race-party). The worst off should be weighed first. While [a national study found no clear fall in turnout](#fact-burdens-of-id-and-citizenship-rules/national-panel-no-turnout-effect), [it cannot rule out drops like those in Kansas and Tennessee](#fact-burdens-of-id-and-citizenship-rules/burden-without-turnout-effect), which [were biggest among young, newly registered and Black voters](#fact-burdens-of-id-and-citizenship-rules/gao-larger-drops-young-new-black). [Campaigns contacted more non-white voters](#fact-burdens-of-id-and-citizenship-rules/mobilisation-may-offset), which may have offset some. But a cost voters must overcome is still a cost.

### So keep the sworn promise of citizenship, and back it with records checks that give notice

Keep [the federal form's sworn statement of citizenship, made under penalty of perjury](#fact-federal-and-state-power-over-voting-rules/federal-form-sworn-citizenship). Check registrations against records, but treat a match as a reason to ask, and give flagged voters notice and time to answer before anything changes. Where states ask for ID, let anyone without it sign a sworn statement instead.

Congress should not pass the SAVE America Act, which [would require proof of citizenship to register](#fact-federal-and-state-power-over-voting-rules/save-america-act-provisions).
