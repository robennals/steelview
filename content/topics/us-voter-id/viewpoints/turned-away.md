Every citizen should have [an equal voice](#principle-an-equal-voice-for-every-citizen). A rule that makes voting harder for some citizens must prevent enough harm to be worth it. Photo-ID and proof-of-citizenship rules prevent almost nothing, and their cost falls on people who can least afford it.

### ID checks at the polls stop a kind of fraud that almost never happens

An ID check at the polls stops one thing: someone voting as another person. [Two separate counts find this the rarest kind of fraud on record](#fact-election-fraud-and-voter-exclusion-history/impersonation-rare-modern). When the Supreme Court upheld Indiana's ID law, [there was no evidence of such fraud in Indiana's whole history](#fact-federal-and-state-power-over-voting-rules/crawford-record). [Britain brought in voter ID as a precaution, not because more cases were being found](#fact-voter-id-rules-in-the-us-and-abroad/britain-precaution-not-fraud).

The frauds that did change results worked differently. [In 14 of 20 recorded cases, no one voted at the polls under a false name. Officials changed ballots, ballots went by mail, or voters sold their votes](#fact-election-fraud-and-voter-exclusion-history/id-would-not-reach). [All three recent results that were thrown out turned on mail or absentee ballots](#fact-election-fraud-and-voter-exclusion-history/modern-result-changing-cases-absentee). [Investigators and courts caught these frauds, not ID checks](#fact-election-fraud-and-voter-exclusion-history/caught-by-prosecutors).

### Voting by non-citizens is rare, and mostly comes from paperwork errors

While [every review finds a few non-citizens who voted](#fact-non-citizens-on-voter-rolls/some-noncitizens-vote), the numbers are tiny. [The votes found are between about 1 and 22 per million ballots](#fact-non-citizens-on-voter-rolls/votes-per-ballots). That is [far fewer than the margins in recent close races](#fact-non-citizens-on-voter-rolls/below-margins). And [most were added to the list through paperwork errors](#fact-non-citizens-on-voter-rolls/how-noncitizens-reach-rolls). Officials can fix those without asking every citizen for a birth certificate.

### Proof-of-citizenship rules block many citizens

[Kansas blocked 31,089 applicants who did not show citizenship documents in time. In 19 years, it found at most 39 non-citizens on its rolls](#fact-burdens-of-id-and-citizenship-rules/kansas-blocked-vs-noncitizens). [Even the state's own expert's figures suggested nearly all those blocked were citizens](#fact-burdens-of-id-and-citizenship-rules/kansas-blocked-vs-noncitizens). While [the court did not know how many of them actually lacked a document](#fact-burdens-of-id-and-citizenship-rules/kansas-dpoc-possession-unknown), every one was kept off the list.

Many citizens would struggle to prove it. [About one adult citizen in ten cannot quickly produce proof of citizenship](#fact-burdens-of-id-and-citizenship-rules/dpoc-not-readily-available). [Arizona found 218,000 long-time voters who had never had proof on file](#fact-burdens-of-id-and-citizenship-rules/arizona-218000), because no one had ever asked them.

### Checks run by officials wrongly flag citizens

Officials have used voting rules to shut people out before. [Louisiana cut Black voters from about 44% of its registered voters to under 1%](#fact-election-fraud-and-voter-exclusion-history/louisiana-registration-collapse), using [tests that let registrars decide who could vote](#fact-election-fraud-and-voter-exclusion-history/jim-crow-tools-discretionary). While a free, uniform ID is not a literacy test, checks run in bulk by officials still sweep up eligible voters. [Florida's 2000 list for removing voters was full of errors](#fact-election-fraud-and-voter-exclusion-history/florida-2000-list).

The same happens today. [Most of the people Alabama flagged as non-citizens in 2024 turned out to be citizens](#fact-non-citizens-on-voter-rolls/alabama-2024). [About a fifth of the people Texas flagged have so far proved they are citizens](#fact-non-citizens-on-voter-rolls/texas-save-reversals). [In reviews that followed up, more citizens were wrongly flagged than non-citizens were found](#fact-non-citizens-on-voter-rolls/both-errors-side-by-side). And a voter who is turned away often does not vote at all. When Texas required an ID number on mail ballots, [about 90% of the voters it rejected in one primary did not vote another way](#fact-burdens-of-id-and-citizenship-rules/rejected-texans-did-not-vote).

### The cost falls hardest on young, poor, Black and Hispanic voters

[Young and poor people are most likely to lack a licence with current details](#fact-burdens-of-id-and-citizenship-rules/licence-by-age-income). [Black and Hispanic citizens are about three times as likely as white citizens to have no licence at all](#fact-burdens-of-id-and-citizenship-rules/no-licence-by-race-party). [The losses of those worst off should be weighed first](#principle-priority-to-the-worst-off).

While [the largest national study found no fall in turnout overall](#fact-burdens-of-id-and-citizenship-rules/national-panel-no-turnout-effect), [it does not rule out larger drops in Kansas and Tennessee](#fact-burdens-of-id-and-citizenship-rules/burden-without-turnout-effect). [A government study found those drops were biggest among young, newly registered and Black voters](#fact-burdens-of-id-and-citizenship-rules/gao-larger-drops-young-new-black). [Campaigns also contacted more non-white voters after the laws passed, which may have made up for some of the effect](#fact-burdens-of-id-and-citizenship-rules/mobilisation-may-offset). A cost that campaigns must work to overcome is still a cost.

### A sworn promise and careful records checks are enough

Keep [the federal form's sworn statement of citizenship, which is a crime to lie on](#fact-federal-and-state-power-over-voting-rules/federal-form-sworn-citizenship). Check registrations against records, and give voters notice and time to answer. Where states ask for ID, let anyone without it sign a sworn statement instead. Congress should not pass the SAVE America Act, which [would require documents proving citizenship](#fact-federal-and-state-power-over-voting-rules/federal-form-sworn-citizenship).
