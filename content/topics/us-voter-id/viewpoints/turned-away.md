---
name: These rules turn away eligible citizens to fix a problem that barely exists
summary: "[Impersonation at the polls is the rarest kind of detected fraud](#fact-election-fraud-and-voter-exclusion-history/impersonation-rare-modern) and [non-citizen votes are found at a few per million ballots](#fact-non-citizens-on-voter-rolls/votes-per-ballots), while [Kansas's citizenship-document rule blocked tens of thousands of applicants](#fact-burdens-of-id-and-citizenship-rules/kansas-blocked-vs-noncitizens). A sworn oath backed by careful records checks protects the rolls without keeping eligible citizens out."
order: 2

[An equal voice for every citizen](#principle-an-equal-voice-for-every-citizen) is real only if each eligible person can actually use their vote. A rule that makes voting harder for some citizens has to earn that cost by what it prevents. Photo-ID and proof-of-citizenship rules prevent almost nothing, and the cost lands on the people with the least room to absorb it.

The fraud a polling-place ID check is built to stop barely appears in the record. [Two independent counts find impersonation at the polls the rarest kind of detected fraud](#fact-election-fraud-and-voter-exclusion-history/impersonation-rare-modern). When the Supreme Court upheld Indiana's ID law, [the record held no in-person impersonation in Indiana at any time in its history](#fact-federal-and-state-power-over-voting-rules/crawford-record).

Britain adopted ID [as a precaution, not after a rise in detected personation](#fact-voter-id-rules-in-the-us-and-abroad/britain-precaution-not-fraud).

The frauds that did change results worked differently. [In 14 of 20 documented cases nobody came to the polls under a false identity](#fact-election-fraud-and-voter-exclusion-history/id-would-not-reach), and [the modern results that were voided or rerun all turned on absentee or mail ballots](#fact-election-fraud-and-voter-exclusion-history/modern-result-changing-cases-absentee). [Investigators and courts caught them, not ID checks](#fact-election-fraud-and-voter-exclusion-history/caught-by-prosecutors).

Some non-citizens do register and vote: [every review finds a few](#fact-non-citizens-on-voter-rolls/some-noncitizens-vote), [mostly through paperwork errors](#fact-non-citizens-on-voter-rolls/how-noncitizens-reach-rolls). But [the votes found run from about 1 to 22 per million ballots](#fact-non-citizens-on-voter-rolls/votes-per-ballots), [far below recent close margins](#fact-non-citizens-on-voter-rolls/below-margins). Fixing clerical errors does not require every citizen to find a birth certificate.

The cost is real. [Kansas blocked 31,089 applicants who did not produce citizenship documents in time, against at most 39 non-citizens found on its rolls in 19 years](#fact-burdens-of-id-and-citizenship-rules/kansas-blocked-vs-noncitizens).

[The court had no evidence of how many lacked a document](#fact-burdens-of-id-and-citizenship-rules/kansas-dpoc-possession-unknown), but every one was an applicant kept off the rolls, and [even the state's own expert's estimate implied nearly all were citizens](#fact-burdens-of-id-and-citizenship-rules/kansas-blocked-vs-noncitizens).

Nationally, [about one adult citizen in ten cannot readily produce proof of citizenship](#fact-burdens-of-id-and-citizenship-rules/dpoc-not-readily-available). Arizona discovered [218,000 long-registered voters who had never had it on file](#fact-burdens-of-id-and-citizenship-rules/arizona-218000), because no one had asked.

Database checks err against citizens too. [Most of the people Alabama flagged as non-citizens in 2024 were citizens](#fact-non-citizens-on-voter-rolls/alabama-2024), [about a fifth of Texas's SAVE flags have so far proved citizenship](#fact-non-citizens-on-voter-rolls/texas-save-reversals), and [in the reviews with published follow-ups, wrongly flagged citizens outnumber confirmed non-citizens](#fact-non-citizens-on-voter-rolls/both-errors-side-by-side).

When Texas required an ID number on mail ballots, [about 90% of the voters it rejected in the first primary did not vote another way](#fact-burdens-of-id-and-citizenship-rules/rejected-texans-did-not-vote).

The burden is not spread evenly. [The young and the poor are most likely to lack a licence with current details](#fact-burdens-of-id-and-citizenship-rules/licence-by-age-income), and [Black and Hispanic citizens are about three times as likely as white citizens to have no licence at all](#fact-burdens-of-id-and-citizenship-rules/no-licence-by-race-party). [Priority to the worst off](#principle-priority-to-the-worst-off) means weighing their losses first.

The best national study finds [no turnout effect from strict ID laws](#fact-burdens-of-id-and-citizenship-rules/national-panel-no-turnout-effect). But [that average does not rule out GAO's larger drops in Kansas and Tennessee](#fact-burdens-of-id-and-citizenship-rules/burden-without-turnout-effect), which [fell hardest on young, newly registered and Black voters](#fact-burdens-of-id-and-citizenship-rules/gao-larger-drops-young-new-black).

The same study found [campaigns stepped up contact with non-white voters, which may have offset the effect](#fact-burdens-of-id-and-citizenship-rules/mobilisation-may-offset). A cost that organisers pay to overcome is still a cost.

Officials have used eligibility rules before. Louisiana took [Black voters from about 44% of its registrants to under 1%](#fact-election-fraud-and-voter-exclusion-history/louisiana-registration-collapse) with tests that gave registrars ["a virtually uncontrolled discretion"](#fact-election-fraud-and-voter-exclusion-history/jim-crow-tools-discretionary). A free, uniform ID is not a literacy test. But [Florida's 2000 removal list was badly inaccurate](#fact-election-fraud-and-voter-exclusion-history/florida-2000-list), and checks run in bulk by officials still sweep up eligible voters.

Keep [the sworn statement of citizenship the federal form asks for, under penalty of perjury](#fact-federal-and-state-power-over-voting-rules/federal-form-sworn-citizenship). Check registrations against records, with notice and time to answer. Where states ask for ID, accept a signed affidavit from anyone without it. Congress should not pass the SAVE America Act.
