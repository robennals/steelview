Every citizen should have an equal voice. A rule that makes voting harder for some citizens must prevent enough harm to be worth it. Photo-ID and [proof-of-citizenship](#glossary-documentary-proof-of-citizenship) rules prevent almost nothing, and their cost falls on people who can least afford it.

### ID checks at the polls stop a kind of fraud that almost never happens

An ID check at the polls stops people voting in someone else's name. [In one count of alleged fraud since 2000, that was 10 of 2,068 cases](#fact-election-fraud-and-voter-exclusion-history/impersonation-rare-modern). [Another found about 31 incidents among more than 1 billion ballots](#fact-election-fraud-and-voter-exclusion-history/two-counts-rare). When the Supreme Court upheld Indiana's ID law, [the record held no evidence of such fraud in Indiana's whole history](#fact-federal-and-state-power-over-voting-rules/crawford-record).

### Non-citizens' votes are too rare to justify demanding papers from every citizen

[The votes by non-citizens that reviews have found are between about 1 and 22 per million ballots](#fact-non-citizens-on-voter-rolls/votes-per-ballots), [far fewer than the margins in recent close races](#fact-non-citizens-on-voter-rolls/below-margins). While [each review charted found some non-citizens who voted](#fact-non-citizens-on-voter-rolls/some-noncitizens-vote), [the reviews that give a cause trace non-citizens on the lists to mistakes](#fact-non-citizens-on-voter-rolls/how-noncitizens-reach-rolls). Officials can fix those without asking every citizen for a birth certificate.

### In Kansas, demanding citizenship papers blocked far more citizens than non-citizens

[Kansas blocked 31,089 applicants who did not show citizenship documents in time](#fact-burdens-of-id-and-citizenship-rules/kansas-blocked-vs-noncitizens). [From 1999 to 2013, only 39 non-citizens were confirmed on its rolls](#fact-burdens-of-id-and-citizenship-rules/kansas-blocked-vs-noncitizens). [Even the state's own expert's figures suggested nearly all those on its suspense list were citizens](#fact-burdens-of-id-and-citizenship-rules/kansas-expert-citizens). While [the court did not know how many actually lacked a document](#fact-burdens-of-id-and-citizenship-rules/kansas-dpoc-possession-unknown), every one was kept off the list. And such a rule would reach many citizens: [about one adult citizen in ten cannot quickly produce proof of citizenship](#fact-burdens-of-id-and-citizenship-rules/dpoc-not-readily-available).

### Checks run by officials wrongly flag citizens

[Most people Alabama flagged as non-citizens in 2024 were citizens](#fact-non-citizens-on-voter-rolls/alabama-2024). [In Texas and Alabama, more citizens were wrongly flagged than people were referred or removed](#fact-non-citizens-on-voter-rolls/both-errors-side-by-side). Bulk checks sweep up eligible voters, and voters shut out often do not vote at all: [about 90% of Texas mail voters rejected in one primary did not vote another way](#fact-burdens-of-id-and-citizenship-rules/rejected-texans-did-not-vote).

### The burden falls hardest on young, poor, Black and Hispanic citizens

[The young and poor are most likely to lack an up-to-date licence](#fact-burdens-of-id-and-citizenship-rules/licence-by-age-income). [Black and Hispanic citizens are at least three times as likely as white citizens to have no licence](#fact-burdens-of-id-and-citizenship-rules/no-licence-by-race-party). The worst off should be weighed first. While [a national study found no clear fall in turnout](#fact-burdens-of-id-and-citizenship-rules/national-panel-no-turnout-effect), [it cannot rule out drops like those in Kansas and Tennessee](#fact-burdens-of-id-and-citizenship-rules/burden-without-turnout-effect), which [were biggest among young, newly registered and Black voters](#fact-burdens-of-id-and-citizenship-rules/gao-larger-drops-young-new-black).

### So keep the sworn promise of citizenship, and back it with records checks

Keep [the federal form's sworn statement of citizenship, made under penalty of perjury](#fact-federal-and-state-power-over-voting-rules/federal-form-sworn-citizenship). Check registrations against records, and give flagged voters notice and time to answer. Where states ask for ID, let anyone without it sign a sworn statement instead.

Congress should not pass the SAVE America Act, which [would require proof of citizenship to register](#fact-federal-and-state-power-over-voting-rules/save-america-act-provisions).
