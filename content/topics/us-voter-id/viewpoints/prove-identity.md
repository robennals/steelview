---
name: Voters should prove who they are and that they are citizens
summary: "An election is only as honest as its check on who is voting. [Non-citizens reach the rolls through gaps at registration](#fact-non-citizens-on-voter-rolls/how-noncitizens-reach-rolls) and [very few citizens lack photo ID of any kind](#fact-burdens-of-id-and-citizenship-rules/no-id-versus-outdated-id), so every state should ask for photo ID at the polls, supply it free, and confirm citizenship before a name goes on the roll."
order: 1
---
An election should be decided by the people entitled to vote, each voting once. That is what [verifiably honest elections](#principle-verifiably-honest-elections) means, and the check has to happen before the ballot goes in the box. A secret ballot cannot be pulled back out once it is mixed with the rest. A prosecution can punish a fraud afterwards; it cannot undo the result.

American history shows the danger is not imaginary. [Machine fraud in Kansas City and Chicago was large, and led to hundreds of convictions](#fact-election-fraud-and-voter-exclusion-history/machine-fraud-real-and-prosecuted). Part of it was exactly what an ID check is for: [Tammany's paid repeaters, and Chicago votes cast at the polls under other people's names](#fact-election-fraud-and-voter-exclusion-history/in-person-ghost-voting).

The bipartisan Carter–Baker Commission concluded that [a good ID system could deter voting under the identities of others or the dead](#fact-election-fraud-and-voter-exclusion-history/carter-baker-id-deters).

It is true that [most documented fraud was done another way, by officials or by mail](#fact-election-fraud-and-voter-exclusion-history/id-would-not-reach). That is a reason to secure those routes as well, not to leave the polling place open. And [every count of impersonation is a count of cases detected](#fact-election-fraud-and-voter-exclusion-history/detected-is-a-floor); a successful one leaves no complaint behind.

Citizenship is the plainest test of who may vote, and it is the one least checked. [The federal registration form asks only for a sworn statement](#fact-federal-and-state-power-over-voting-rules/federal-form-sworn-citizenship). [Every state that has looked has found non-citizens on its rolls](#fact-non-citizens-on-voter-rolls/found-on-rolls), and [Ohio has found them in every batch since 2019](#fact-non-citizens-on-voter-rolls/ohio-recurs).

Most got there [through clerical errors, miscoded licence records and misunderstanding](#fact-non-citizens-on-voter-rolls/how-noncitizens-reach-rolls). Those are failures of the check at registration, so that is where the fix belongs.

The numbers found are small: [confirmed non-citizen registrants are hundredths of a percent of the rolls or fewer](#fact-non-citizens-on-voter-rolls/hundredths-of-a-percent), and [the votes found are far below recent close statewide margins](#fact-non-citizens-on-voter-rolls/below-margins).

But [some local races are decided by a handful of votes](#fact-non-citizens-on-voter-rolls/close-local-races), and [these reviews find only people whose status shows up in some government record](#fact-non-citizens-on-voter-rolls/detected-is-a-floor). A rule that is broken rarely and invisibly still has to be enforced.

The cost of checking is lower than critics fear. [Few citizens have no photo ID at all; many more just hold a licence with an old address](#fact-burdens-of-id-and-citizenship-rules/no-id-versus-outdated-id).

The largest national study found [strict ID laws had no negative effect on registration or turnout, for any group](#fact-burdens-of-id-and-citizenship-rules/national-panel-no-turnout-effect). [Northern Ireland, which adopted ID after organised personation](#fact-voter-id-rules-in-the-us-and-abroad/northern-ireland-after-personation), [saw no fall in turnout once photo ID came in](#fact-voter-id-rules-in-the-us-and-abroad/ni-after-photo-id).

Americans agree. [Photo ID has majority support in both parties](#fact-opinion-and-confidence-in-elections/photo-id-majorities) and [in every racial group Pew reports](#fact-opinion-and-confidence-in-elections/photo-id-across-races), and [proof of citizenship at registration has the same level of support](#fact-opinion-and-confidence-in-elections/proof-of-citizenship-support).

The Supreme Court upheld Indiana's law on [the state's interests in deterring fraud and in public confidence](#fact-federal-and-state-power-over-voting-rules/crawford-upheld-photo-id). [One study finds strict ID raised confidence in state elections](#fact-opinion-and-confidence-in-elections/milyo-finds-effect), though [two national studies find no effect](#fact-opinion-and-confidence-in-elections/studies-find-no-effect).

How the check is done matters. [Kansas demanded documents from every new applicant and blocked 31,089 of them, against at most 39 non-citizens on its rolls](#fact-burdens-of-id-and-citizenship-rules/kansas-blocked-vs-noncitizens). That is the model to avoid. [Several states ask for documents only when state records cannot confirm citizenship](#fact-voter-id-rules-in-the-us-and-abroad/dpoc-not-all-in-effect), and [a check of a whole statewide roll is now routine](#fact-non-citizens-on-voter-rolls/full-roll-check-feasible).

So: photo ID at the polls, with [a free card for anyone without one, as Georgia offers](#fact-voter-id-rules-in-the-us-and-abroad/strict-states-exceptions); and citizenship confirmed from records at registration, with documents asked for only when the records fail. [Rules must mean what they say](#principle-rules-must-mean-what-they-say), and a citizenship requirement that is never checked is a requirement in name only.
