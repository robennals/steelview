An election should be decided by the people allowed to vote, each voting once. That needs a check before the ballot goes in, because a secret ballot cannot be taken back out. That is what [verifiably honest elections](#principle-verifiably-honest-elections) means.

### Fraud at the polls has happened, and when it works it leaves no trace

[People have voted several times, or in other people's names](#fact-election-fraud-and-voter-exclusion-history/in-person-ghost-voting). [Frauds in Kansas City and Chicago led to hundreds of convictions](#fact-election-fraud-and-voter-exclusion-history/machine-fraud-real-and-prosecuted). Without a check at the polls, a vote cast in someone else's name that works leaves no one to complain.

While [modern counts find few cases of voting as someone else at the polls](#fact-election-fraud-and-voter-exclusion-history/two-counts-rare), those are the cases that were caught. They cannot say whether the share that goes unseen is large or small. A bipartisan commission [said good ID could deter this fraud](#fact-election-fraud-and-voter-exclusion-history/carter-baker-id-deters). And while [14 of the 20 recorded cases were done by officials or by mail](#fact-election-fraud-and-voter-exclusion-history/officials-or-mail), those routes need guarding too. That is no reason to leave the polls open.

### State reviews keep finding non-citizens on voter lists

Only citizens may vote. Yet [all five state reviews charted found or suspected non-citizens on the voter list](#fact-non-citizens-on-voter-rolls/found-on-rolls). [Ohio referred apparent non-citizens for prosecution in 2019, 2021, 2022 and 2024](#fact-non-citizens-on-voter-rolls/ohio-recurs).

A photo ID cannot catch this, because non-citizens can hold licences. [The federal registration form asks people only to swear that they are citizens](#fact-federal-and-state-power-over-voting-rules/federal-form-sworn-citizenship), and [the reviews that give a cause trace non-citizens on the lists to mistakes](#fact-non-citizens-on-voter-rolls/how-noncitizens-reach-rolls). Those are failures of the check at registration, so that is where to fix them.

### Even a few illegal votes matter when races turn on a handful

[Louisiana's top election official says some local elections are decided by a handful of votes](#fact-non-citizens-on-voter-rolls/close-local-races). In a race that close, a few votes that should not count can change who wins. A rule that is broken rarely still has to be enforced.

[The non-citizen votes found are at most about 22 per million ballots](#fact-non-citizens-on-voter-rolls/votes-per-ballots). While [they are far fewer than the margins in recent close races](#fact-non-citizens-on-voter-rolls/below-margins), they are only the votes found. [The checks rest on government records](#fact-non-citizens-on-voter-rolls/checks-rest-on-records), so others may go unseen.

### Where photo ID came in after organised fraud, suspected fraud fell and turnout held

[Northern Ireland brought in ID after organised voting in other people's names](#fact-voter-id-rules-in-the-us-and-abroad/northern-ireland-after-personation). [Once it required photo ID, suspected personation fell sharply](#fact-voter-id-rules-in-the-us-and-abroad/ni-personation-fell), and [there was no evidence of a fall in turnout](#fact-voter-id-rules-in-the-us-and-abroad/ni-after-photo-id).

The American evidence is less clear. [A national study found strict ID had no effect on fraud, actual or perceived](#fact-opinion-and-confidence-in-elections/studies-find-no-effect). But a study can count only the fraud that is found. Northern Ireland's fall showed up in a measure that does not wait on a prosecution: ballots issued when a real voter found someone had already voted in their name.

### Few citizens lack photo ID, and strict ID laws show no clear effect on turnout

[Very few citizens have no photo ID at all; many more just have a licence with an old address](#fact-burdens-of-id-and-citizenship-rules/no-id-versus-outdated-id). [A national study found strict ID laws had no clear effect on registration or turnout](#fact-burdens-of-id-and-citizenship-rules/national-panel-no-turnout-effect).

The same study found [no extra effect on non-white voters compared with white voters](#fact-burdens-of-id-and-citizenship-rules/no-extra-effect-non-white). How a rule is written still matters. [Texas's ID-number rule for mail ballots rejected one in eight in its first primary](#fact-burdens-of-id-and-citizenship-rules/texas-march-2022-spike). That is why the ID must be free, and the rule easy to meet.

### So states should ask for free photo ID, and check citizenship against records

This is not one side's rule: [large majorities in both parties support photo ID](#fact-opinion-and-confidence-in-elections/photo-id-majorities). States should ask for photo ID at the polls, [free to anyone without one, as in Georgia](#fact-voter-id-rules-in-the-us-and-abroad/strict-states-exceptions). They should check citizenship against government records before adding anyone to the list, and ask for documents only when the records fail.

[83% of adults favour proof of citizenship when people first register](#fact-opinion-and-confidence-in-elections/proof-of-citizenship-support). While [Kansas blocked 31,089 applicants without papers, against 39 confirmed non-citizens on its rolls](#fact-burdens-of-id-and-citizenship-rules/kansas-blocked-vs-noncitizens), that is the model to avoid. [The government says it has checked more than 65 million voters in 26 states against federal records](#fact-federal-and-state-power-over-voting-rules/save-whole-rolls). [Rules should mean what they say](#principle-rules-must-mean-what-they-say): a citizenship rule never checked is a rule in name only.
