An election should be decided by the people allowed to vote, each voting once. That needs a check before the ballot goes in, because a secret ballot cannot be taken back out. That is what [verifiably honest elections](#principle-verifiably-honest-elections) means.

### Fraud at the polls has happened, and ID checks are meant to stop it

[Frauds in Kansas City and Chicago led to hundreds of convictions](#fact-election-fraud-and-voter-exclusion-history/machine-fraud-real-and-prosecuted). Other recorded cases were what an ID check stops: [people voted several times, or in other people's names](#fact-election-fraud-and-voter-exclusion-history/in-person-ghost-voting). The bipartisan [Carter–Baker commission said good ID could deter it](#fact-election-fraud-and-voter-exclusion-history/carter-baker-id-deters).

While [14 of the 20 recorded cases were done by officials or by mail](#fact-election-fraud-and-voter-exclusion-history/officials-or-mail), that is a reason to guard those routes too, not to leave the polls open. And the cases on record are only those that were caught.

### State reviews find non-citizens on voter lists, though citizenship is the most basic rule

Only citizens may vote. [All five state reviews charted found or suspected non-citizens on the voter list](#fact-non-citizens-on-voter-rolls/found-on-rolls), and [Ohio referred apparent non-citizens for prosecution in 2019, 2021, 2022 and 2024](#fact-non-citizens-on-voter-rolls/ohio-recurs). Yet [the federal registration form asks people only to swear that they are citizens](#fact-federal-and-state-power-over-voting-rules/federal-form-sworn-citizenship).

[The reviews that give a cause trace them to mistakes](#fact-non-citizens-on-voter-rolls/how-noncitizens-reach-rolls). Those are failures of the check at registration, so that is where to fix them.

### Some races turn on a handful of votes, so even a few illegal votes matter

[Louisiana's top election official says some local elections are decided by a handful of votes](#fact-non-citizens-on-voter-rolls/close-local-races). So while [non-citizens' votes are far fewer than the margins in recent close races](#fact-non-citizens-on-voter-rolls/below-margins), and [those found are at most about 22 per million ballots](#fact-non-citizens-on-voter-rolls/votes-per-ballots), that does not make them harmless. And [the checks rest on government records](#fact-non-citizens-on-voter-rolls/checks-rest-on-records), so others may go unseen. A rule that is broken rarely still has to be enforced.

### Few citizens lack photo ID, so checking it costs voters little

[Very few citizens have no photo ID at all; many more just have a licence with an old address](#fact-burdens-of-id-and-citizenship-rules/no-id-versus-outdated-id). [The national panel study found strict ID laws had no clear effect on registration or turnout overall](#fact-burdens-of-id-and-citizenship-rules/national-panel-no-turnout-effect), or [on non-white voters against white voters](#fact-burdens-of-id-and-citizenship-rules/no-extra-effect-non-white). [Northern Ireland brought in ID after organised voting in other people's names](#fact-voter-id-rules-in-the-us-and-abroad/northern-ireland-after-personation), and [there was no evidence of a fall in turnout once it required photo ID](#fact-voter-id-rules-in-the-us-and-abroad/ni-after-photo-id).

### Most Americans, in both parties, back photo ID

[Large majorities in both parties support photo ID](#fact-opinion-and-confidence-in-elections/photo-id-majorities), as do [at least seven in ten people in every racial group Pew reports](#fact-opinion-and-confidence-in-elections/photo-id-across-races). [83% of adults favour requiring proof of citizenship when people first register](#fact-opinion-and-confidence-in-elections/proof-of-citizenship-support). The Supreme Court upheld Indiana's ID law 6–3, without a majority opinion, [accepting preventing fraud and keeping public trust as "sufficiently strong" reasons](#fact-federal-and-state-power-over-voting-rules/crawford-upheld-photo-id).

While [one national study found no greater trust in ID states and another no effect on perceived fraud](#fact-opinion-and-confidence-in-elections/studies-find-no-effect), [a third found that strict ID did raise trust in state elections](#fact-opinion-and-confidence-in-elections/milyo-finds-effect).

### Done badly, the check blocks far more people than it catches

[Kansas blocked 31,089 applicants for lack of proof of citizenship, against 39 confirmed non-citizens on its rolls](#fact-burdens-of-id-and-citizenship-rules/kansas-blocked-vs-noncitizens). That is the model to avoid, now that [the government says it has checked more than 65 million voters in 26 states against federal records](#fact-federal-and-state-power-over-voting-rules/save-whole-rolls).

States should ask for photo ID, [free to anyone without one, as in Georgia](#fact-voter-id-rules-in-the-us-and-abroad/strict-states-exceptions), and check citizenship against records, with documents only if those fail. [Rules should mean what they say](#principle-rules-must-mean-what-they-say): a citizenship rule never checked is a rule in name only.
