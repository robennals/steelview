Every count on this topic is of cases someone found. [Detected impersonation is
a floor](#fact-election-fraud-and-voter-exclusion-history/detected-is-a-floor),
and [non-citizen reviews find only people whose status is in some government
record](#fact-non-citizens-on-voter-rolls/detected-is-a-floor). No source
estimates the rest.

This is the gap that most decides the argument about photo ID at the polls. If
undetected impersonation and non-citizen voting were many times the detected
numbers, the central claim against these rules, that the problem barely exists, would
fail. If they are close to the detected numbers, the case for proving identity falls
back on confidence and principle. The evidence that would settle it is an
independent audit: for example, matching a sample of ballots cast against
death, residence and citizenship records, rather than waiting for complaints.
