title: "Stopping ineligible votes and protecting eligible voters"
tension: "Every check can make two mistakes: counting a vote that should not count, or blocking one that should. Evidence can say how often each happens, but not how much of one to accept to avoid the other."
kind: tradeoff
divides: [prove-identity, turned-away, clean-rolls]
positions:
  - viewpoint: prove-identity
    holds: "An ineligible vote cancels a lawful one and cannot be undone, while a citizen without ID can get one free."
  - viewpoint: turned-away
    holds: "Keeping an eligible citizen from voting falls on a real person with a right, and is the worse error."
  - viewpoint: clean-rolls
    holds: "The choice is forced only by bad tools: records checks with notice and time to answer cut both errors."
