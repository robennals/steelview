tension: "People want only citizens voting, and every citizen able to. A national proof rule depends on how many would get the papers they need, and the sides expect very different things."
kind: prediction
divides: [prove-identity, turned-away, id-plus-access]
positions:
  - viewpoint: prove-identity
    holds: "With records checked first, few citizens would ever need to produce a paper."
  - viewpoint: turned-away
    holds: "Millions of citizens cannot readily reach a birth certificate or passport, and eligible people would stay off the rolls."
  - viewpoint: id-plus-access
    holds: "It depends on whether the state supplies free documents and help; without them the rule would block citizens."
