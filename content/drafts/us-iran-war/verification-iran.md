# Verification — Iran repression, record abroad, moderation (batch B)

Checked 26 Oct 2026 against primary sources fetched with curl, falling back to
web.archive.org where the live site refused. Quotes are copied from the
fetched text. "As of" is the date the figure describes, not the date it was
fetched.

**Summary of status (20 candidates):** 15 verified (C24, C25, C40, C43, C44,
C49, C50, C52, C54, C55, C56, C57, C59, C62, C63), 4 corrected (C8, C51, C53,
C58), 1 unverifiable (C9 counts; method verified), 0 dropped. Details per candidate below; the
blocked-source list is at the end.

**Corrections that change outline wording:**

1. **"More than 7,000 protesters" is wrong (C53/C25).** HRANA's 7,007 is
   *all* deaths. Protesters were 6,488. The 7,007 also includes 236 children,
   76 bystanders and 207 security-force deaths. Say "more than 7,000 people,
   including 6,488 protesters" or "nearly 6,500 protesters".
2. **"At least 975 in 2024" is IHRNGO's figure (C49/C50).** Amnesty's figure
   for 2024 is 972. If the outline pairs 975 (2024) with "1,639–2,159 (2025)",
   the 2024 number belongs to the IHRNGO series. Label each number with its
   counter.
3. **"About 1% of presidential registrants allowed to stand" does not hold for
   2024 (C58).** In 2024 six of "more than 80" were approved, about 7%. The
   roughly-1% rate holds for 2005, 2013, 2017 and 2021 (0.4–1.2%). Reword to
   "in most elections since 2005, about 1% or fewer".
4. **HRANA October-year series (C51).** The candidate's list skips 2017–18
   (265). The full ten-period series is below. HRANA's peak *month* was
   December 2025 (404). March 2026, the first month of the war, had only 10
   executions.
5. **WINEP tracker (C8).** The serial numbers (223 at 3 Dec 2024) count
   attacks WINEP *confirmed*, not raw claims. They are not spread evenly over
   fourteen months either: by my parse of the table, about 170 of roughly 200
   fall between Oct 2023 and Jan 2024.

### C50 — IHRNGO/ECPM annual death-penalty report — verified; full series read

- **2025: at least 1,639, up 68% on 975 in 2024.** "At least 1,639 people were
  executed, marking the highest number of recorded executions since 1989 and
  a dramatic 68% increase compared to 2024, when 975 executions were
  recorded."
  https://iranhr.net/en/articles/8673/ (live 403; read via archive capture
  13 Apr 2026). Full report PDF downloaded directly:
  https://iranhr.net/media/files/2025_Annual_Report_on_the_Death_Penalty.pdf.
  Publisher: Iran Human Rights (IHRNGO) with ECPM. Published about
  12–13 Apr 2026 (page metadata). As of calendar 2025.
- **Breakdown (as of 2025):** "Only 113 executions (under 7%) were announced
  by official sources"; "795 people (48.5%) were executed for drug-related
  offences"; "747 people (45.6% of all executions) were sentenced to qisas";
  "At least 57 people were executed for the security charges … They include 2
  protesters, 18 political prisoners, 13 espionage defendants"; "At least 48
  women were executed".
- **Timing:** "With 222 executions, October was the bloodiest month in 2025 …
  60% of the executions took place in the second half of the year." And: "A
  new surge in executions began in the months after the 12-day war between
  Iran and Israel in June".
- **Supports the view that conflict worsens repression (cuts against
  regime-change readings that the war "weakened" the regime's grip):**
  "Execution numbers tend to increase when authorities fear social unrest and
  when the attention of the international community is focused on other
  geopolitical issues." The report adds: "Periods of crisis and conflict have
  historically provided the authorities with opportunities to intensify
  repression and carry out executions away from international scrutiny."
- **Full series 2008–2025.** Read off the labels of the PDF chart "EXECUTION
  TRENDS IN THE LAST 18 YEARS (2008-2025)", p.16. The labels sum to 11,196,
  matching the report's total: "From IHRNGO's first annual report in 2008 to
  2025, Iranian authorities have executed at least 11,196 people".

  | Year | 2008 | 2009 | 2010 | 2011 | 2012 | 2013 | 2014 | 2015 | 2016 |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Executions | 350 | 402 | 546 | 676 | 580 | 687 | 753 | 972 | 530 |

  | Year | 2017 | 2018 | 2019 | 2020 | 2021 | 2022 | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Executions | 517 | 273 | 280 | 267 | 333 | 582 | 834 | 975 | 1,639 |

  - 2023 = 834 is now verified (it was *unverified* in the candidate).
  - Cross-check from the World Coalition summary of the 2022 report: "At
    least 582 people were executed in 2022, compared to 333 in 2021 … more
    than double the number of executions in 2020 (267 people executed)."
    https://worldcoalition.org/annual-report-iran-2022/ (World Coalition
    Against the Death Penalty, 2023).
- **A finding for the moderation argument:** executions under Rouhani fell
  from 972 (2015) to 273 (2018). IHRNGO attributes the drop elsewhere to
  drug-law changes (2017 amendment, per its legal section; not quoted here).
  Executions then rose every year from 2021, under Raisi and then Pezeshkian.
