## What the builders changed or could not verify
- **Cure rate (R2).** The EAC prints 54.3% itself, dividing state by state; the published totals (317,191 of 585,457) give 54.2%. Both are explained; prose says "more than half". The draft's 54.3% was the EAC's own figure, not an arithmetic slip.
- **R1 range is 2016–2026, not 2004.** Census publishes its method-of-voting table only from 2018; the 2004–2016 series exists only as a CRS chart image. EAVS 2016 mail share uses the 2020 report's recalculation (24.5%, not 23.7%).
- **R2 range extended to 2012.** 2012 and 2014 EAVS reports fetched; a break marks "absentee" becoming "mail".
- **Heritage voidings (R4): 32, not "about 25".** Hand-coded from Heritage's own text: 16 mainly absentee or mail, 5 mixed, 11 not absentee; none in CO, OR, UT or WA. The case list is in the chart note.
- **SAVE America and mail voting (R6) is unclear.** Only an advocacy outlet says it would end universal mail voting; CRS calls the mail limit a proposed amendment. The outline's "per coverage, end universal mail voting" is downgraded accordingly.
- **Nevada "about 2% of mail ballots" (R3)** not confirmed; replaced by a derived floor ("nearly one in fifty").
- **Dropped as secondary-only:** Australia's 1856 dates, South Carolina 1950 and the Chartist quote (R5 now starts at 1872); Wayne County 2020 and the Georgia board 2024 (R4, in `dataStillNeeded`); Box 13 kept but marked "method not confirmed"; Georgia 2018 0.15%; the disability 2022 figure; the Paterson rejection-rate figures from the candidate's petition.
- **Baringer, Herron & Smith (2020)** cited only by abstract; full text recorded as a gap.
- **UK 2024 (R5)** quoted from the Electoral Commission report the human downloaded: 2.2% of returned postal votes rejected (about 168,000), postal registration 18% → 20%, 89% of postal voters satisfied, "no widespread, systemic issues", and some voters saying another adult insisted on helping. It gives no postal share of valid votes, so the UK series stops at 2019.
- **Two possible errors found in the voter-ID report** (not edited here): its Gallup accuracy-series quote lacks the midterm-year values its chart uses, and its `winner-gap-worldwide` "US 2025: 51 / 45" bars look derived (Gallup prints only a two-year average). R7 fixed both in its own copy.
