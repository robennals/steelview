# Sources to download by hand

These are the sources the voter-ID, vote-by-mail and Iran-war research needed but could not fetch automatically (as of 26 Oct 2026). Each one is listed once, even if more than one topic uses it. It is collected from the "Blocked" lines in `us-voter-id/stage5-results.md` and the `candidate-data.md` files, the `dataStillNeeded` notes in the voter-ID facts, and the verification files for mail and Iran. **P1** means a viewpoint's argument or a key figure now rests on a secondary source (a news story, an advocacy group or a reprint), or is missing. **P2** means the item would replace an archive or reprint copy already used, or would fill a stated data gap. **P3** is nice to have. Where a complete Internet Archive copy has already been read, the row says **Have archive copy**, and you can skip it if short of time. Under "Blocked how": **bot-blocked** means the site refuses automated requests (403, bot check, TLS error, rate limit) and a normal browser should work; **paywalled** needs library access or purchase; **login** needs a free account; **not online** needs a library or records request; **large/scanned** can be downloaded but is too big or needs OCR. Save each file under the name in the first column. Research gaps that were never searched for (for example the signature cases *Martin v. Kemp* and *Jaeger*, or the Converse–Burnham–Kousser turnout debate) are not listed, because they are not downloads.
