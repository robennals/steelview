---
name: Those who contribute earn a share
---
A shared enterprise lasts when the people who draw on it have also put
something in. Rights that come with membership — a pension, support when out
of work, a voice in how things are run — are fairest when they grow with what
a person has contributed, so that no one carries others who could carry
themselves.

The contributory state pension works this way: years of National Insurance
earn the right to it. Mutual societies and cooperatives were built on the same
idea, giving members a share of what they had built together in proportion to
what they had paid in.
