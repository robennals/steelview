---
name: Freedom to cooperate and exchange
---
People should be free to trade, share knowledge and work together for mutual
benefit. Those who cherish open exchange see cooperation as a way for people to
improve one another's lives through their different skills, ideas and talents.

Scientists sharing discoveries so that doctors around the world can treat disease
show this ideal at its best. So do farmers and craftspeople exchanging food and
tools: each offers something the other values, and together they can do more than
either could alone. The freedom to make those connections is worth protecting.
