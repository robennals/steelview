---
name: Continuity of a shared way of life
---
People should be able to cherish a shared way of life and pass it on to their
children. A language, a tradition or a community's memory can be precious to the
people who inherit it, giving them a sense of belonging across generations.

The preservation of Indigenous languages is a clear example: children learning
the words, stories and songs of their grandparents keep an irreplaceable part of
human life alive. Restoring a historic town centre after a war expresses the same
commitment. What people treasure deserves care, and they should have a voice in
its future.
