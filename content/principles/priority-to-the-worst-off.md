---
name: Priority to the worst off
---
The people in greatest need deserve our first concern. A decent society pays
special attention to those with the least power, fewest resources and least
ability to protect themselves.

Feeding children during a famine and giving emergency care to the most seriously
injured make this principle easy to recognise. The urgency of their need gives
them a special claim on help. Providing shelter for people who have lost their
homes expresses the same conviction: people whose lives are most precarious
should come first in our care.
