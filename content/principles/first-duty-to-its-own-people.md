---
name: A government's first duty is to its own people
---
A government holds power because its citizens grant it, and it spends their
taxes and, in war, their children's lives. That makes it their agent, not a
free actor with causes of its own. Before it takes on a burden for anyone
else, it owes its citizens a clear account of what they gain.

When a government sends ships to evacuate its citizens from a collapsing
country, or rebuilds a flooded town before it funds anything abroad, almost
no one objects, because those are the things a government is for. Holding
it to that order of priority is how citizens stop their own state from
being spent on purposes they never chose.
